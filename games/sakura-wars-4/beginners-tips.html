Beginner Tips

This campaign spans roughly four to five hours per run while juggling thirteen romanceable heroines across two divisions. Establishing clean ビジュアルメモリ preparation, timed map discipline, and battle stance habits prevents wasted playthroughs from the opening scene onward.

Core Habits Checked: 0/8 (0%)

Preparation, Trust & Combat Essentials

  • Prepare VMU blocks and run Data Merge before starting. Keep at least 9 free blocks on your primary ビジュアルメモリ for SAKURA_4.SYS and your first save slot, plus extra 6-block slots for anchors. Insert memory cards holding cleared saves from サクラ大戦, サクラ大戦2, and サクラ大戦3 and trigger データ合体 so flashback scenes appear naturally.
  • Pick your triangle pair intentionally in Yoneda's office. During 第一幕 inside 支配人室, naming a Paris heroine and selecting a Tokyo heroine photograph sets the two leads for your love triangle and grants both a starting 恋愛度 surge. Selecting neither option defaults your focus to 真宮寺さくら and エリカ・フォンティーヌ.
  • Watch the 5-minute clock during free-roam exploration. Entering a room during free movement advances the theater clock by five minutes, and several heroines rotate rooms at exact timestamps. Check the Main Story time tables before stepping into any corridor so you visit time-limited rooms such as 売店 before they close.
  • Learn all five LIPS variants before timed scenes begin. Standard three-choice prompts share space with アナログLIPS where the Dreamcast analog stick sets response strength, タイミングLIPS where waiting transforms the wording, クリックLIPS where clicking off-screen opens marriage questions, and セレクト choices.
  • Shape your Top 5 roster during the final Act 3 free roam. Only your five highest-ranked heroines in 恋愛度 can co-pilot 双武. During the second free-roam block in 第三幕, every heroine has a dedicated conversation with a massive positive or negative swing, letting you push a neglected favorite into the Top 5 or drop a rival down.
  • Master the Fire-to-Mountain Captain Command swap in ARMS. 大神一郎 can switch 隊長コマンド freely mid-turn. Switch to 火 to use ため at minimal AP cost, then swap to 山 before ending your turn to 防御 or heal cheaply.
  • Wiggle the analog stick to slip through narrow battle alleys. In ARMS stages where street debris narrows a lane, push forward while rapidly rocking the Dreamcast analog stick left and right to edge your 光武 past tight corners without wasting a full turn detouring.
  • Maintain four dedicated VMU anchor saves. Keep permanent saves at the start of 第一幕, right before the 第三幕 final free roam, at the 最終幕 pre-boss selection, and right before talking to 米田一基 in the epilogue.
