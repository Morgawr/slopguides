Chronological Play Order & Ending Roadmap

Strategic ビジュアルメモリ save anchors reduce full replays when chasing all fourteen endings. Clear 大神一郎 first to unlock fast-forward options and 絶対正義組, then sweep four to five heroines per 第三幕 anchor across three focused cycles.

Phase 1 · VMU Save Import & First Clear Anchor

  • Import SW1, SW2, and SW3 cleared saves via Data Merge. Before beginning Run 1, load データ合体 with your サクラ大戦, サクラ大戦2, and サクラ大戦3 VMU cards inserted. You can also repeat データ合体 later if you finish additional heroine routes in the earlier Dreamcast titles.
  • Create an Act 3 Top-5 Anchor before entering the Command Room. In 第三幕, save on a separate 6-block VMU slot at the start of the second free-roam block before visiting any rooms. This anchor lets you shift up to five different heroines into the 双武 partner slot by boosting your targets and lowering rival scores.
  • Claim the Ogami Solo Ending on your first clear. Save right before 第三幕 asks whether someone waits in your heart or during the 最終幕 conversation with 米田一基. Selecting the duty-only answer unlocks こだわり設定, おまけ, and satisfies the solo-ending requirement for 絶対正義組.
  • Reload the Act 3 Command Room anchor to sweep your current Top 5. Save over your system file SAKURA_4.SYS after each ending so clear flags accumulate, then reload your pre-boss 第三幕 save to take each remaining Top-5 heroine aboard 双武.

Phase 2 · Three-Cycle Minimum Playthrough Plan

  • Cycle 1 (Sakura & Erica Focus plus Top-5 Sweep). Pick 真宮寺さくら and エリカ・フォンティーヌ in Act 1, spread Act 1 and Act 2 positive LIPS choices toward 神崎すみれ, マリア・タチバナ, and アイリス, buy 2 bromides, clear 大神一郎 Solo first, then reload Act 3 to clear those five heroines.
  • Cycle 2 (Kanna/Kohran & Glycine/Coquelicot Focus). Start New Game with こだわり設定 active, form 絶対正義組 for two-turn boss clears, focus Act 1 on 桐島カンナ or 李紅蘭 plus グリシーヌ・ブルーメール, buy 2 new bromides, and use the Act 3 pre-free-roam anchor to clear 桐島カンナ, 李紅蘭, グリシーヌ・ブルーメール, and コクリコ.
  • Cycle 3 (Orihime/Reni & Lobelia/Hanabi Focus). Choose ソレッタ・織姫 or レニ・ミルヒシュトラーセ alongside ロベリア・カルリーニ in Act 1, visit 売店 in Act 1 and Act 3 for 2 more bromides, and clear ソレッタ・織姫, レニ・ミルヒシュトラーセ, ロベリア・カルリーニ, and 北大路花火. Clearing all 13 heroine endings unlocks the special post-credits group illustration.
  • Quick Cleanup Runs for Bromides. Since only 2 ブロマイド can be bought per playthrough, run short skip-enabled replays whenever you want the remaining shop portraits after finishing all 14 endings.
