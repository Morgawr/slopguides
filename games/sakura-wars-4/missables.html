Ending Priority & Top-5 Tie-Breaker Rules

When multiple heroines tie in 恋愛度 entering the 第三幕 partner prompt, internal roster order and your initial 第一幕 triangle selections decide which five heroines appear on 米田一基's shortlist. Always separate your target heroine's score during the second 第三幕 free roam before entering 作戦指令室.

Timed Spots, Bromides & Save-Locked Events

  • Buy Act 1's Bromide at exactly 00:35 inside 売店. 高村椿 opens the shop only during that five-minute slot in 第一幕, selling one bromide per playthrough here.
  • Buy Act 3's Bromide at 00:00 inside 売店. Visit 高村椿 at the start of 第三幕's first free roam before the clock advances past 00:00. Only two ブロマイド are purchasable per full playthrough.
  • Watch 5-minute single-slot heroines in Act 1 free roam. 桐島カンナ and 真宮寺さくら appear in their rooms at 00:00, アイリス and マリア・タチバナ at 00:05, レニ・ミルヒシュトラーセ and ソレッタ・織姫 at 00:10, and 李紅蘭 at 00:15. Use the テラス and 舞台 slots later in the hour to catch missed characters.
  • Trigger the 結婚観 prompt on every Act 1 and Act 3 click scene. During クリックLIPS segments, move the cursor outside the character onto the background to ask about marriage and secure an extra 恋愛度 boost.
  • Complete the Act 2 closet jealousy sequence for your Paris lead. After hiding your Tokyo heroine in the closet in 大神の部屋, give a positive reply to your Paris partner so the jealousy confrontation triggers rather than fizzling out.
  • Merge cleared SW1, SW2, and SW3 VMU data for all Memory Pictures. Flashback 思い出絵 require matching cleared saves from サクラ大戦, サクラ大戦2, and サクラ大戦3. Paris heroine illustrations check whether that specific Paris heroine was cleared on your imported サクラ大戦3 save.
  • Catch the Act 3 pre-Mikasa free-roam CG event for all 13 heroines. Every heroine sits at a fixed room during 第三幕's second free roam with an event CG that feeds her post-clear 思い出絵 slots.
  • Clear 大神一郎 Solo Ending before attempting 絶対正義組. Answering duty-only to Yoneda unlocks the solo clear flag required on later runs alongside merged prior saves, high 隊長値, and motivated Top-5 members.
  • Protect battle triggers that award extra Trust in Acts 1 and 2. Move アイリス to the shutter lever and keep her near 大神一郎 by the Ferris wheel in Battle 1, and move near エリカ・フォンティーヌ, グリシーヌ・ブルーメール, ロベリア・カルリーニ, and 桐島カンナ in Battle 2.
  • Check サクラ大戦ジャン unlocks in おまけ. Clearing Survival unlocks Free Match and Tile Editor, defeating all 13 heroines unlocks support opponents, and completing all special hands awards the final minigame illustration.
