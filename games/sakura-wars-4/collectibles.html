Memory Pictures, Gallery & Post-Clear Unlocks

  • First Clear Bonus, こだわり設定 & おまけ. Complete one full playthrough to unlock fast-forward skip options, CG/Movie/BGM viewers, and サクラ大戦ジャン.
  • Complete 思い出絵 via VMU Merge. Import cleared saves for every heroine from サクラ大戦, サクラ大戦2, and サクラ大戦3 using データ合体 so all flashback variations appear in the gallery.
  • All-13 Heroines Completion Bonus Illustration. Clear all thirteen heroine endings on the same SAKURA_4.SYS file to unlock the extra post-credits illustration following Yoneda's farewell scene.
  • Act 3 Pre-Mikasa Room Event CGs, All 13 Heroines. Visit every heroine during 第三幕 Free Roam 2 to record her individual eve-of-battle illustration.

サクラ大戦ジャン 〜ロンせよ乙女〜

Match four sets of three identical character tiles faster than your opponents. Clearing modes and assembling special hand combinations awards bonus illustrations in おまけ.

  • Win 勝ち抜き戦. Defeat five straight opponents to unlock 自由対戦 and パイ編集.
  • Defeat All Opponents in 自由対戦. Beat all 13 Hanagumi heroines to unlock the Three Maids, Kayama, Rose Division, Mell & Ci, Yoneda, and Grand-Mère, then beat every newly unlocked opponent for a bonus CG.
  • Complete All 特殊役. Form every character combo listed below to earn the final minigame reward CG.
