Beginner Tips & Core Habits

Most early frustration in Guild Wars comes from treating combat like a modern action MMO instead of a tactical party RPG built around aggro bubbles, attribute math, and interrupt discipline. Lock in these twelve core habits early and even dense Normal Mode missions become predictable and smooth.

Combat & Pulling Gear & Economy Party & Routing

Combat, Aggro Rings & Target Calling 0/0 (0%)

  • Habit 1: Play a Prophecies Roleplaying Character with Reforged Mode On. During character creation, check Reforged Mode while leaving Dhuum's Covenant and Melandru's Accord unchecked. Reforged Mode halves skill trainer gold prices (capping early trainers cleanly at 500g), adds 5% extra gold and XP in Prophecies explorable zones, boosts early mission payouts, upgrades early henchmen skill bars, and unlocks Devona and Ghost of Althea as permanent Heroes right inside Ascalon.
  • Habit 2: Live by the Three Compass Rings (U). Your radar displays three concentric circles added in the Reforged UI overhaul. The bright inner white circle is your Aggro Bubble (roughly "earshot"): stationary foes inside it rush your party, while patrolling groups engage once their red dot touches the line. The middle grey ring marks long spellcasting and bow range, and the outer ring shows shout and spirit radius.
  • Habit 3: Pull Single Patrols at Max Longbow or Flatbow Range. Primary Rangers already wield a Flatbow or Longbow in weapon slot F1, while caster and melee professions should buy the cheapest white Longbow or Flatbow in Ascalon and keep it in F2. When two patrols overlap ahead, fire a single arrow at one foe from the outer compass ring (switching back to F1 on non-Rangers) and immediately sprint backward so only that group chases you into cleared ground.
  • Habit 4: Focus Enemy Healers First with Ctrl + Space. Enemy groups routinely run dedicated Monks or Mesmers that out-heal scattered damage. Hold Ctrl and press Space (or double-click your target) on an enemy Monk, Ritualist, or Mesmer at the start of every pull. Heroes and Henchmen immediately converge all spells and interrupts onto your called target.

Attributes, Armor Scaling & Economy Discipline

  • Habit 5: Skip Intermediate Armor Crafters. Do not drain your gold crafting AL 49, AL 55, or AL 61 armor sets along the Shiverpeaks and Kryta road. After picking up cheap AL 30/40 Ascalon City armor (300g at Armorer Corwen, already providing AL 70 Elemental defense for Rangers), sail directly from Lion's Arch to Kaineng Center in Factions to craft full Max Armor (AL 60/70/80) from Armorer Ryoko or Suki for roughly 5 Platinum plus common materials.
  • Habit 6: Specialize into Two or Three Attributes Max. Raising an attribute from 10 to 12 costs more points than raising two unused attributes from 0 to 6, yet scales exponentially harder on every spell and attack. Push your main damage attribute to 12 (before your +1 headpiece and runes) and your primary utility attribute to 8 to 10, leaving unused lines at zero. Reallocating points in any outpost is completely free.
  • Habit 7: Identify Drops Before Selling or Salvaging Runes. Carry a Superior Identification Kit and a Salvage Kit (Regular for cheap white cloth/iron items; Expert or Superior for extracting valuable runes and insignias from gold/purple/blue armor). Identifying items raises their merchant sell price well above the 5g identification cost and reveals rare weapon mods and valuable runes.
  • Habit 8: Unlock Your Account-Wide Xunlai Chest Immediately. Speak to any Xunlai Agent in Ascalon City (Post-Searing), Lion's Arch, Kaineng Center, or Kamadan and pay the one-time 50 gold fee. Every character on your account shares this bank and its dedicated Crafting Material Storage tab, letting you deposit common and rare materials right from your inventory and preserve Pre-Searing or campaign drops for later.

Party Micro, Death Penalty & Campaign Routing

  • Habit 9: Flag Your Backline Apart Before AoE Encounters. Left-clicking an individual Hero's flag icon beneath the compass (or using the full-party flag) pins them to stand away from the frontline. Spreading Heroes just outside melee range stops bosses from wiping your healers in one Fire Storm or Meteor Shower, while pressing Clear All Flags lets them follow again once a room is clean.
  • Habit 10: Always Bring Resurrection & Clear Heavy Death Penalty. Keep Resurrection Signet on your bar until Heroes reliably pack hard resurrections (Flesh of My Flesh or Resurrection Chant). Each death applies -15% Death Penalty (stacking to -60% max HP and Energy); killing foes slowly grants +2% Morale Boost chunks up to +10%, and re-entering any town resets Death Penalty to zero.
  • Habit 11: Grab Level 20 Heroes & Max AL 60/70/80 Armor at Lion's Arch. Instead of trudging through Prophecies alone with Level 12 to 15 henchmen, take the Level 10 quests in Lion's Arch to visit Cantha (Kaineng Center), Elona (Kamadan / Consulate Docks), and optionally the Far Shiverpeaks (Boreal Station / Eye of the North). Returning to Kryta with Level 20 Heroes, Signet of Capture, and max armor turns midgame Prophecies into a breeze.
  • Habit 12: Use /wiki Inside In-Game Chat Anytime. Guild Wars includes native official wiki lookup right from the chat bar. Typing /wiki followed by any quest name, boss, or collector opens the official Guild Wars Wiki page in your default browser, making quick location checks effortless on multi-monitor setups or Steam Deck desktop mode.
