Missables, One-Way Cutoffs & Bug Workarounds

Most of Guild Wars lets you replay missions, adjust attributes, and revisit every region freely at Level 20. However, Pre-Searing Ascalon enforces a permanent one-way exit, several Reforged Mode quests contain sequence bugs to sidestep, and Nightfall features mutually exclusive Hero forks until the campaign finale.

Pre-Searing Point of No Return & Tutorial Lockouts 0/0 (0%)

  • Pre-Searing Point of No Return (Sir Tydus → Ascalon Academy): Speaking to Sir Tydus inside Ascalon City courtyard and choosing "Yes, I am ready" to start Ascalon Academy permanently destroys Pre-Searing Ascalon for that character. Before stepping through the gate, finish A Bastion In the North, unlock Devona in Forsaken Tunnels, claim your secondary profession freebies, and pick up Gwen's Tapestry Shred. (Missed skills from Pre-Searing can still be bought post-Searing from Halbrik in Ascalon City, and Devona can be claimed in Lion's Arch, but you can never revisit the idyllic green zone.)
  • Getting Started in Guild Wars (Town Crier Lockout Bug): In Reforged Mode, accept Getting Started in Guild Wars from the Town Crier inside Ascalon City immediately after War Preparations. If you delay talking to the Town Crier until after finalizing your Secondary Profession (A Second Profession), the quest can become permanently unobtainable on that character, forfeiting its easy 500 XP and 100g.
  • Across the Wall & Ben Wolfson Spawn Rule: After escorting Ben Wolfson south across the Great Northern Wall back into Lakeside County during Across the Wall, return straight to Ascalon City and turn the quest in to Nente / Captain Arne before stepping back into the Northlands. Re-entering The Northlands without finishing the turn-in leaves Devona, Vell, and Bock despawned at the gate so A Bastion In the North cannot start properly.
  • Long Thought Forgotten Floor 1 Lock Trigger: Inside Pre-Searing Forsaken Tunnels (Floor 1) during Long Thought Forgotten, walk close to the glowing Dungeon Lock in the southwestern cavern so Objective 3 (Push further into the tunnels) updates immediately before sweeping the rest of the floor. Leaving the floor early or bypassing the lock trigger can stall quest progression until re-entering.
  • Gwen's Tapestry Shred (Pre-Searing Keepsake for EotN): Speak to young Gwen outside the Ascalon City gate in Lakeside County for Gwen's Flute, pick up the Broken Flute near the ruined bridge west of Ashford, buy a replacement Flute (100g) from an Ascalon City or Ashford merchant, and gather 3 Red Iris Flowers. Hand Gwen the Broken Flute, replacement Flute, and 3 Red Iris Flowers, momentarily drop any extra Red Iris Flowers on the ground, and speak to Gwen once more while solo to receive her Tapestry Shred keepsake. Do not delete or merch it: bringing it to adult Gwen inside the Eye of the North Hall of Monuments rewards extra dialogue and unique armor/unlock lore in Then and Now, Here and There.
  • Legendary Defender of Ascalon (LDoA) Pre-Searing Title: Reaching Level 20 without ever leaving Pre-Searing earns the coveted LDoA account title. While unnecessary and slow on a very first playthrough (our main walkthrough leaves Pre-Searing cleanly around Level 8), alt characters achieve LDoA comfortably via Lieutenant Langmar's daily Vanguard quests starting at Level 10 and repeatable clears of Forsaken Tunnels.

Reforged Mode Badges, Permanence Rules & Attribute Points

  • Permanent Mode Locks (Reforged, Dhuum's Covenant, Melandru's Accord): Reforged Mode () can only be disabled permanently via an outpost mode-remover NPC and cannot be turned back on after starting. Dhuum's Covenant loses its badge forever upon a single character death. Melandru's Accord permanently locks out Xunlai bank access, player-to-player trading, and crafters until revoked irreversibly.
  • Native +30 Attribute Point Quests (200 Cap): Leveling to 20 provides only 170 points; the final +30 come strictly from the two native Attribute Quests of your character's starting campaign (listed on Campaigns). Foreign travelers from Prophecies do not receive Factions or Nightfall +15 quests, so make sure a Prophecies starter finishes both Forgotten Wisdom and The Hero's Challenge.

Nightfall Hero Forks, Optional Heroes & Beyond State Toggles

  • Nightfall Fork 1 – Master of Whispers vs Margrid the Sly (Yohlon Haven): Taking Secrets in the Shadow unlocks Necromancer Hero Master of Whispers while locking out Margrid the Sly until after finishing Nightfall's finale (Abaddon's Gate). Always take Master of Whispers first so your Offensive Mesmerway team has two Necromancer Heroes right away.
  • Nightfall Fork 2 – Norgu vs Goren (Mihanu Township): Choosing Brainwashing / Norgu's recruitment unlocks Mesmer Hero Norgu while delaying Warrior Goren until after Abaddon's Gate. Always choose Norgu first to complete your three-Mesmer core (Gwen, Ghost of Althea, Norgu).
  • Post-Campaign & DLC Optional Heroes (Razah, Xandra, M.O.X., Zei Ri): After beating Nightfall, pick up Omni-Profession Hero Razah in Heart of Abaddon via Finding a Purpose. Grab Ritualist Hero Xandra anytime at the Norn Fighting Tournament in Gunnar's Hold (Eye of the North). Zei Ri (Ritualist) is the long-tail reward for finishing Cantha's post-story Winds of Change Hard Mode chain.
  • Guild Wars Beyond World-State Spawns (War in Kryta & Winds of Change): Starting War in Kryta (Lion's Arch after beating Prophecies/EotN) or Winds of Change (Kaineng after beating Factions) replaces normal explorable foes in Kryta or Cantha with high-difficulty Peacekeepers and Ministry of Purity patrols. You can pause or restore normal spawns by speaking to the Herald NPCs in Lion's Arch or Kaineng Center once enabled, or hold off starting Beyond quests until done capturing standard bosses.
