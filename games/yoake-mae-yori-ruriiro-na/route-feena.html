フィーナ・ファム・アーシュライト serves as both the mandatory opening route and the canon finale unlocked after clearing every other heroine. On your first playthrough, any choice combination leads to her normal ending, whereas on subsequent runs you follow the exact choices below to re-enter her route or prepare the 6月24日 master save.

1st Playthrough (Forced Lock)

Play through the game freely on your first run. All choices resolve to Feena's normal ending and unlock the remaining heroines for Playthrough 2 onward.

2nd+ Playthrough Choices

Use this path on Playthrough 2 or later if you want to replay Feena's route for missed common-route CGs or set up a late-June save before unlocking リースリット.

True Route (Canon Finale)

Prerequisites
Clear every standard heroine in your edition and then clear リースリット. A new option named 夜明け前より瑠璃色な appears directly on the title screen.

Selecting the new title-screen entry starts directly from 8月9日 and plays linearly through the canon ending without further branching choices. Completing this route unlocks the post-clear おまけシナリオ.
