Missables, Bad Ends & CG Completion

Completion Checks: 0/8 (0%)

Clearing every heroine ending covers the majority of the CG gallery, yet several expression differentials (差分), alternate event scenes, and post-clear bonus episodes require checking both sides of specific choices. Use this checklist during your cleanup pass to reach 100% gallery completion.

Differential CG & Scene Branches

Save right before each of these choices, pick one option to register its CG, then reload and pick the opposite option.

Common Route Bad End

The Bad End cannot occur on Playthrough 1 due to the forced Feena lock. It is not required for unlocking Wreathlit or the True Route, so trigger it only if you want 100% scene viewing.

Post-Clear Bonus Scenarios (おまけシナリオ)

After clearing the True Route, check the Extras / Bonus menu on the title screen. Viewing every bonus scenario is required to reach 100% CG and Scene Gallery completion.
