Beginner Tips

Establishing reliable save, battle, and inn habits during Phase 1 prevents soft-locks, missed トークマター, and unnecessary repeat playthroughs. Apply these six practices from your opening hours in プラティナ and ホルスの翼.

Early Setup & Play Habits

  • Maintain staggered backup saves. Keep permanent saves before the Phase 2 route choice, before the end-of-Phase-2 commitment question, and before entering the final dungeon of Phase 3. There is no New Game+ carryover, so staggered saves are the fastest path to all seven endings.
  • Rest at inns regularly to clear トークマター. Resting repeatedly until no new conversations trigger prevents queue overflow bugs that can freeze the game when dozens of topics unlock at once during late インストール scenes.
  • Build ダイブポイント with your active レーヴァテイル. Only the deployed backline singer earns full battle ダイブポイント and トークマター battle triggers. Swap between オリカ and ミシャ during Phase 1 so both enter Phase 2 with spare points.
  • Keep 詩魔法 singing until the burst percentage climbs. Releasing Red 詩魔法 at 100% deals minimal damage compared to letting it charge past 300% to 1000%+ while your Vanguards attack and guard to raise ハーモニクス.
  • Buy recipes and グラスノ結晶 after every boss. Visit スピカ's shop in ネモ whenever you return from a dungeon. Her stock rotates after battles and supplies rare Rank A and S crystals once you reach mid-game.
  • Craft items at inns with every heroine. Each レーヴァテイル unlocks distinct グラスメルク variations and reaction トークマター when synthesizing new recipes for the first time.
