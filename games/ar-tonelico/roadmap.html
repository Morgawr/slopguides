Chronological Play Order

Because the Phase 2 route choice splits the story cleanly in half while Phase 3 gates Complete Endings behind シュレリア's コスモスフィア, following this ten-step sequence lets you clear every dungeon, unlock all seven endings, and collect missable トークマター with only one Phase 1 run.

Milestones Cleared: 0/10 (0%)

Phase 1 & Master Branch Save

  • Clear Phase 1 and build baseline ダイブポイント. Follow Phase 1 through 謳う丘 and 天覇研究所. Clear Lv.1 and Lv.2 of オリカ and ミシャ as they open.
  • Complete ミシャ Lv.3 before her Phase 2 transformation. Enter Misha Lv.3 while she is in her child form so her Cosmosphere completion flag registers cleanly.
  • Create Master Save A before the Phase 2 Route Split. Save in a dedicated slot right before choosing between オリカ and ミシャ in Phase 2.

First Full Path (Aurica or Misha Route)

  • Progress chosen Phase 2 route & create Save B at the Phase 2 climax. Before answering the final Phase 2 question, save in a second slot, pick the upper option to view that heroine's Early Ending, then reload Save B and pick the lower option to enter Phase 3.
  • Run the グラバート羅針盤 loop and craft endgame gear. Once shops in ネモ, エムフェイナ, and プラティナ are open, use Exploits to fund Party Builds and スピカ crystals.
  • Grab シュレリア's Department Store topic immediately in Phase 3. Visit the department store in プラティナ as soon as シュレリア joins, before clearing Level 2 of her Cosmosphere.
  • Create Save C before clearing シュレリア Lv.5. Finish the final dungeon from Save C without clearing Lv.5 to record the Incomplete Ending, then reload Save C and finish Shurelia Lv.1–5.
  • Create Save D before the Phase 3 final confrontation. From Save D you can unlock your chosen heroine's Complete Ending, シュレリア's Ending, and the Bad Ending.

Second Heroine Cleanup (Reload Master Save A)

  • Load Master Save A and select the opposite Phase 2 heroine. Play through her exclusive Phase 2 dungeons and collect her Early Ending before entering Phase 3.
  • Clear Phase 3 on the second route for her Incomplete & Complete Endings. Finish Lv.1–9 and Lv.E of her Cosmosphere and use a pre-シュレリア-Lv.5 save to unlock both her Incomplete and Complete Endings.
