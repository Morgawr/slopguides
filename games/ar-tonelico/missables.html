Missable Triggers & Progression Locks

Clear each window before crossing the listed phase or コスモスフィア threshold.

  • Phase 2 Route Split Backup Save. Save in a separate slot before choosing オリカ or ミシャ in Phase 2. Selecting one heroine permanently locks the other heroine's Phase 2 path and exclusive トークマター for that save.
  • ミシャ Lv.3 Child-Form Dive Window. Enter Misha Lv.3 while she is still in child form during Phase 2. Advancing past her Phase 2 plot transformation before opening Lv.3 caps her completion display at Lv.2.
  • シュレリア Department Store Topic 1. As soon as シュレリア joins in Phase 3, examine the sparkling point inside the プラティナ department store before clearing Lv.2 of her Cosmosphere.
  • シュレリア Pre-Airship Tower Topics. Ascend the upper tower sectors on foot before acquiring the upgraded airship so location-triggered シュレリア topics along the climb appear cleanly.
  • シュレリア Lv.5 Dual-NPC Flag Check. Inside シュレリア's Lv.5 Cosmosphere, speak with both どんすけ and 波摩 before triggering the academy event to avoid a progression lock.

All 7 Endings Checklist

Every ending is determined by your Phase 2 heroine commitment, your Phase 2 closing answer, whether シュレリア's Lv.5 コスモスフィア is complete, and your Phase 3 choices.

  • Aurica Phase 2 Early Ending. Choose オリカ in Phase 2, then select the upper choice to live together peacefully when prompted at the end of Phase 2.
  • Misha Phase 2 Early Ending. Choose ミシャ in Phase 2, then select the upper choice at the end of Phase 2.
  • Aurica Incomplete Ending. Take オリカ's path into Phase 3 and clear the final boss while leaving シュレリア's Cosmosphere incomplete.
  • Misha Incomplete Ending. Take ミシャ's path into Phase 3 and clear the final boss while leaving シュレリア's Cosmosphere incomplete.
  • Aurica True Ending. Clear オリカ's route through Phase 3, complete all 5 levels of シュレリア's Cosmosphere, defeat the final boss, and choose to go to カルル村 with オリカ.
  • Misha True Ending. Clear ミシャ's route through Phase 3, complete all 5 levels of シュレリア's Cosmosphere, defeat the final boss, and choose to travel with ミシャ.
  • シュレリア Ending & Bad Ending. Accessible from either Phase 3 route once シュレリア's Lv.5 Cosmosphere is finished by choosing to remain in プラティナ with シュレリア, or failing the ミュール endgame sequence for the Bad Ending.
