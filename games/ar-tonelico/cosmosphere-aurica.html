Aurica Cosmosphere

オリカ・ネストミール

Tower priestess specializing in elemental Red bursts and party sustain magic. Levels 1 through 5 are accessible on every route during Phase 1 and early Phase 2, while Levels 6 through 9 require selecting her path in プラティナ during Phase 2.

Aurica Levels Cleared: 0/10 (0%)

Cosmosphere Levels Lv.1 – Lv.E Checklist

  • Lv.1 いばらの姫. Unlocks automatically in Phase 1 upon gaining dive access. Reward includes fire burst and barrier magic (火炎弾, ファイアーガード, フレイメア) and standard gear. Always treat Aurica warmly rather than mocking her insecurities to keep DP costs low.
  • Lv.2 竜の持つ傷. Clear in Phase 1. Teaches ウンディーネ and オリカ一号, plus unlocks costume スクワート.
  • Lv.3 癒される手. Clear before finishing Phase 1. Unlocks シルフのダンス and priestess costume 天守聖衣. This is the last level guaranteed before the Phase 2 route choice locks deep dives on Misha's path.
  • Lv.4 力を失う力. Opens after early Phase 2 events. Unlocks サンダーガード and modern uniform スクールデイズ. Speak reassuringly during her self-doubt scenes.
  • Lv.5 歪守護者. Highest level reachable if you chose Misha's path, and the threshold level for オリカ's path. Grants high-tier Red and Blue skills and costume どんすけ.
  • Lv.6 艶やかな心. Aurica Route exclusive. Requires Phase 2 Cathedral choice 教会へ恩返しする and relevant トークマター cleared at the inn. Unlocks costume バブルパッション.
  • Lv.7 聖戦. Aurica Route exclusive. Unlocks SERAPH costume along with endgame burst magic. Clear all dialogue topics tied to her ヒュムノス heritage before descending to Level 8.
  • Lv.8 本音の慟哭. Aurica Route exclusive. Clear before the Phase 3 boundary point. Completing this stage awards costume LILIM and unlocks the final emotional confrontation required for her Lv.9 pledge.
  • Lv.9 契り. Aurica Route exclusive during Phase 3 once you commit to her. Completing Lv.9 satisfies the dive condition for Aurica's Good Ending and awards bridal costume セレモニー.
  • Lv.E シグザール. Optional post-Lv.5 Extra stage unlocked by finding the corresponding トークマター key in late Phase 2 or Phase 3 shops and treasure chests. Rewards マルローネ cameo costume and bonus グラスメルク recipes.
