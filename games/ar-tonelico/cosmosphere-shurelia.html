Shurelia Cosmosphere

シュレリア

Administrator of the Tower of アルトネリコ. Unlike オリカ and ミシャ, her Binary Field spans Levels 1 through 5 and becomes accessible late in Phase 2 and Phase 3 on either heroine route.

Critical Warning · Academy Event Deadlock

During the 学園 scenario inside シュレリア's Cosmosphere, speak with both どんすけ and 波摩 before exhausting your daily conversation steps. Skipping either NPC can lock progression toward Lv.5 世界の勇者.

Shurelia Levels Cleared: 0/5 (0%)

Binary Field Levels Lv.1 – Lv.5 Checklist

  • Lv.1 愛の使い手. Unlocks late in Phase 2 once Shurelia formally joins combat deployments. Introduces her humorous self-defense fantasies and provides starter Song Magic.
  • Lv.2 はち合わせ. Features simulated school encounters where you learn about Shurelia's hidden longing for ordinary friendship and expand her offensive spells.
  • Lv.3 固い約束. Talk thoroughly to both どんすけ and 波摩 around the 学園 grounds to keep every event flag intact.
  • Lv.4 二人の想い. Confronts the emotional isolation of guarding ソル・シエール across centuries. Unlocks late-game party protection spells and prepares her ending branch.
  • Lv.5 世界の勇者. Completing Lv.5 unlocks シュレリア's high-tier offensive and support 詩魔法 and makes her ending selectable at the Phase 3 commitment choice regardless of whether you walked Aurica's or Misha's Phase 2 path.
