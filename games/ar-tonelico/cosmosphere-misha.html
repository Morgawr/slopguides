Misha Cosmosphere

彌紗・アルトセルク・リューン

Star Singer commanding ice field magic and multi-hit lightning and tower bursts. Her コスモスフィア tracks both her child form (子供ミシャ) and adult form (大人ミシャ), making early Phase 1/2 dive timing essential for Lv.3 and Lv.4 costumes.

Form Timing Rule · Child Form Lockout

Clear ミシャ Lv.3 and Lv.4 while she remains in 子供ミシャ form during Phase 1 or early Phase 2. Entering Lv.3 after she permanently transitions to her 大人ミシャ appearance skips the マジカルシャワー costume reward.

Misha Levels Cleared: 0/10 (0%)

Cosmosphere Levels Lv.1 – Lv.E Checklist

  • Lv.1 塗り絵の心. Available right after rescuing Misha in セロの森. Awards lightning magic (雷弾, 雷護) and スタンダード outfit.
  • Lv.2 闇世界. Clear in Phase 1 before ascending back to プラティナ. Grants Analyzer utility and field ice spell 樹氷弾.
  • Lv.3 使命. Dive here as 子供ミシャ before Phase 2 progression changes her form. Unlocks Ice Guard, Frost Ball, Blossom, and costume マジカルシャワー.
  • Lv.4 PP症候群. Addresses the strain of forced Star Singer transformations. Rewards child costume シープスイーツ and strengthens her Blue healing repertoire.
  • Lv.5 幼き賢者. Maximum depth on Aurica's route, and the pivot point on Misha's own route. Unlocks 大人ミシャ costume セーラーガール.
  • Lv.6 朱の女王. Misha Route exclusive. Choose 捜索隊に志願する at the Cathedral in Phase 2 and exhaust all inn トークマター. Rewards adult costume Y.
  • Lv.7 籠の中の虎. Misha Route exclusive. Awards ninja costume SINOBI and unlocks advanced Song Magic tied to her childhood memories with ライナー.
  • Lv.8 二律背反. Misha Route exclusive. Clear in late Phase 2 prior to locking Phase 3. Rewards kimono-themed adult costume KASHA and confronts her conflict between duty and personal affection.
  • Lv.9 契り. Misha Route exclusive during Phase 3 once committed to her. Unlocks signature endgame Song Magic including 我愛弥 and 塔結線, plus traditional bridal costume 白無垢.
  • Lv.E ケントニス. Optional Extra stage unlocked via late-game トークマター triggers. Rewards cameo costume エルフィール for 子供ミシャ and specialty crafting recipes.
