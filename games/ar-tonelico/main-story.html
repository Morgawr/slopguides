Main Story Walkthrough

Progression across ソル・シエール divides into three phases, with a heroine route split midway through Phase 2 and a commitment lock before Phase 3. Follow these steps alongside your Aurica and Misha Cosmosphere dives to avoid missing temporary zones or quests.

Phase 1 · Platina to the Wings of Horus

1. Platina Opening & Descent

  • Acquire the Hymn Crystal and descend via Hovering Cliff. Speak with シュレリア at the 謳う丘 and プラティナ Cathedral, descend through 滞空岸壁 with ライナー, and defeat the グラブジャベリン mechanical guardian while protecting オリカ.
  • Clear Viola Forest and recruit Krusche and Jack. Travel across the ホルスの翼 into ヴィオラの森 to reach カルル村. Complete the repair errand with クルシェ and ジャック, then use グラスメルク at the village camp to craft your early weapons and dive items.

2. Airport City Nemo, Cello Forest & Misha's Join

  • Explore Airport City Nemo and Firefly Alley. Enter 空港都市ネモ and locate 蛍横丁 to visit スピカ's crystal shop and the local ダイブ屋. Note that 謳う丘 and 滞空岸壁 treasure chests from the descent must be cleared on first visit or returned to once air travel unlocks.
  • Traverse Cello Forest and rescue Misha. Head through セロの森 using オリカ's green field magic to burn brush obstacles. Complete the rescue sequence to bring ミシャ into your party alongside ラードルフ. Check Missables for her Lv.3 Costume timing before triggering adult transitions later on.
  • Infiltrate Tenba Labs and Falcon's Claw. Proceed north to 天覇研究所 and clear the facility security floors, then climb たかのつめあと. Charge Red 詩魔法 past 400% while defending with ラードルフ before unleashing burst casts on Phase 1 boss encounters.
  • Finish Phase 1 at the Hexagonal Plate. Fight your way through the 六角板 and clear both オリカ and ミシャ up to Cosmosphere Lv.3 at ネモ or プラティナ before initiating the return ascent.

Phase 2 · The Route Split in Platina

3. Cathedral Events & Decision Point

  • Clear the Altar of Apostles battle in Platina. Upon returning to プラティナ, advance to the Altar of Apostles to battle the mechanical 四八式 units, then search the Gather area before returning to the Cathedral. Create a permanent archive save right before speaking with the Bishop inside the Cathedral.

4A. Aurica Route Tasks

  • Gather Church relics and repair the Airship. Travel with オリカ and ラードルフ to gather the ソレイユストン at 追憶の尾翼 and Squad Metal inside スクワート廃墟. Grab recipe card 合C：カゴ in Squad Ruins, synthesize the フリッパー, and report back to クルシェ in ヴィオラの森.

4B. Misha Route Tasks

  • Clear Crescent Chronicle and craft Sharing Core. Escort ジャック through クレセントクロニクル to locate ミシャ and defeat ボールド using charged 詩魔法 while guarding his High Voltage follow-ups. Collect ソレイユストン and Squad Metal, save before crafting the シェアリングコア, and return to クルシェ.

Phase 2 Convergence · Blastline to Silver Horn

5. Breaking Through the Blastline

  • Cross the Blastline to Em Pheyna. Board the repaired airship to pierce the ブラストライン energy barrier and land in エムフェイナ. Visit its shops immediately for rare materials including パラドクスアイ and Resonant Shield Boxes used in the Gravard Compass synthesis loop.
  • Clear Dragon's Nest, Silver Horn & recruit Ayatane. Fight through 竜の巣 and ascend シルヴァホルン, defeating Ikaruga Mk. II and Child of Light 奏 before 絢胤 joins the active roster. Sweep シルヴァホルン chests for ドゥームスレイヤー and ヴァストラッド components, plus 大皇冠 in 竜の巣.
  • Lock your Phase 3 ending commitment. Before advancing to the top of the tower, push your chosen heroine through Cosmosphere Lv.8 and clear all available トークマター at the inn. Keep a clean backup save prior to the commitment scene so you can branch later for Normal, Good, and シュレリア outcomes.

Phase 3 · Peak of the Tower

6. Silvaplate, Tower Inside & Temple of Elemia

  • Ascend Silvaplate and unlock Shurelia's Cosmosphere. Cross シルヴァプレート and defeat the tower defenses blocking the upper levels. As soon as シュレリア's dive opens, check Shurelia CS and visit the 学園 event carefully so どんすけ and 波摩 do not deadlock Lv.5 progression.
  • Clear Tower Inside and confront the antagonist at Temple of Elemia. Craft ultimate armor and グラスノ結晶 in Party Builds before entering the final sanctum against ミュール. Maintain steady Harmonics with ライナー, protect your レーヴァテイル against wide-area interrupts, and discharge EXEC_PHANTASMAGORIA/. on Good Ending branches.
