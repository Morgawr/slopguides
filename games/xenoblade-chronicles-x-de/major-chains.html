Quest Chains

Some of the richest rewards on 惑星ミラ sit at the end of multi-chapter questlines whose prerequisites weave across Normal, Affinity, and Basic missions. Following the chains on this page lets you unlock level-5 field treasure chests, claim your free starter ドール, recruit ミーア, bring every アームズカンパニー online, and open hidden endgame boss caverns while keeping every prerequisite in sequence.

シークレットオーダー0/12 (0%)

Locked トレジャーボックス scattered across Mira hold generous Battle Point payouts, probes, and schematics, while all フロンティアネット probe sites open freely on first contact. Clearing the three riddle-based Mission Control quests below unlocks the Level 5 field skills needed to open every high-grade container on the planet.

シークレットオーダー（サーチ） -> サーチ Ch. 5+ · Lv. 18

Complete Story Chapter 5 (Wii U required Archaeological Field Skill Lv. 4). Accept randomly from the BLADE Concourse Mission Control Board.

  • Step 1 · 忘却の渓谷などで「パグルスの白甲羅」を10個集める. Defeat ランド・パグルス enemies in Oblivia, such as along the coast near FN Site 317 or south-east waters near FN Site 313, and break their head shell appendage (or exchange Material Tickets) to obtain パグルスの白甲羅.
  • Step 2 · 夜光の森の秘境「大蛇の小袋」を発見する. Skip Travel to FN Site 220 in Noctilum, head north across the stone bridge, and drop down into the pit/water on the right (north side underneath the bridge) to enter the cave housing 大蛇の小袋.
  • Step 3 · 原初の荒野の秘境「タートルネスト」を発見する. Head northeast from FN Site 106 (or west from FN Site 108) in Primordia, drop down to the recessed lake/cove at the base of the sea cliffs, and swim into the cleft cave entrance to discover タートルネスト.
  • Step 4 · 早朝（5:00〜6:59）にNLA住宅エリア・セイブパーク南西にいるソランに話しかける. Set in-game time to Early Morning (05:00 to 06:59), travel to セイブパーク in the Residential District, and speak directly to ソラン standing on the west/southwest edge near the Dozer Skell and transport helicopter (he has no '!' quest marker over his head) and choose 'Accept'.
シークレットオーダー（メカニカル） -> メカニカル Ch. 8+ · Lv. 34

Complete Story Chapter 8 to accept from the BLADE Concourse Mission Control Board, however, Step 2 requires the Skell Flight Module (unlocked after Story Chapter 9 via 'A Girl's Wings') to reach Mount Edge Peak.

  • Step 1 · 原初の荒野・ジャンパス湖東平原のオーバード「幽寂のイェレナ」（Lv.34）を倒す. Travel to East ジャンパス湖の東平原 northwest of FN Site 120 in eastern Primordia and walk directly underneath the overhanging triangular boulder surrounded by Ciniculas so Yelena, 幽寂のイェレナ drops down from above, then defeat her.
  • Step 2 · 忘却の渓谷の絶景・秘境「マウントエッジ頂上」を発見する（要フライトカスタム）. Using a Skell equipped with the Flight Module (available after Chapter 9), fly to the very top of the needle-like summit north of the Giant Ring / near FN Site 315 in Oblivia to discover マウントエッジ頂上.
  • Step 3 · 白樹の大陸の迷妄山頂上周辺で「ハーコーン」を4個集める. Fly to Delusians North 北迷妄山頂上 and Delusians South 南迷妄山頂上 near FN Site 419 in eastern Sylvalum and gather 4 blue collectible crystals yielding ハーコーン.
  • Step 4 · 夕方（17:00〜18:59）にNLA住宅エリア・浄水場南西の路地裏にいるソランに話しかける. Set in-game time to Evening (17:00 to 18:59), go to the narrow alley between houses just south/southwest of the 浄水場 in the Residential District, speak directly to ソラン, and choose 'Accept'.
シークレットオーダー（バイオロジカル） -> バイオロジカル Ch. 10+ · Lv. 51

Complete Story Chapter 10 (Wii U required Biological Field Skill Lv. 4). Accept randomly from the BLADE Concourse Mission Control Board. Requires Skell Flight for Step 2.

  • Step 1 · 白樹の大陸・シエル湖西部砂海南部のオーバード「餓狼のアンドレア」を倒す. Travel to the southern West シエル湖の西部砂海 (near FN Sites 402 / 406) in Sylvalum among the white trees and green crystal formations, locate the patrolling beast-pack Tyrant Andrea, 餓狼のアンドレア, and defeat it.
  • Step 2 · 夜光の森・神鳥の寝床にある秘境「剣兵の巣」を発見する（要フライトカスタム）. Fly high up to the 神鳥の寝床 in northern Noctilum (east of FN Sites 206 / 213) and land on the terraced circular pools at 剣兵の巣 to register the Unexplored Territory (beware high-level camouflaged Mortifole enemies).
  • Step 3 · 黒鋼の大陸の東海岸周辺でコレクションアイテム「スケッチモンスター」を6個集める. Travel to the rocky eastern shoreline cliffs of Cauldros around FN Sites 514 and 515 and collect 6 rare blue collectible crystals yielding スケッチモンスター.
  • Step 4 · 夜（19:00〜23:59）にNLA工業エリア・アームズ性能試験場内にいるソランに話しかける. Set in-game time to Night (19:00 to 23:59), enter the アームズ性能試験場 in NLA's Industrial District, speak directly to ソラン, and choose 'Accept'.
