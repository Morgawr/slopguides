Side Quests

ノーマルクエスト are where NLA grows from a human outpost into a bustling multi-species city. Beyond Credits and EXP, these 194 side quests welcome new alien districts aboard, unlock fresh アームズカンパニー, award unique armor pieces and ホロフィギュア, and drive Mira's 開拓率 toward 100%.

Every quest below is grouped by the earliest chapter it becomes available so you can clear a batch between story missions or jump directly here from Main Story and Quest Chains. When a quest prerequisite mentions a social rumor from the クエストボード terminal, simply close and reopen the board menu until that lead-in entry rolls into view.
