フロンティアネット

Expanding フロンティアネット is the single best investment you can make while exploring 惑星ミラ, unlocking permanent fast-travel beacons while generating passive ミラニウム, Credits, and rare crafting ores every few minutes. Because Mechanical Field Skill checks are gone from probe beams, 98 of the 104 FNスポット can be activated on foot as soon as you reach them, leaving only six cliff-top sites (FN109, FN110, FN213, FN304, FN310, FN320) for the Chapter 9 ドールフライトパック.
