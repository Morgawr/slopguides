Heroines & Ogami

Every member of the 花組 carries distinct LIPS preferences, キネマトロン call windows, attack ranges, and Special Attack progressions across 光武・改, 天武, and 光武・改2.

大神一郎

Captain · Lucky Color White · Melee Tank & Stance Commander

  • Combat Role High durability melee anchor with 6 Move across all chassis tiers, 風林火山 stance switching, and three charges of かばう per stage.
  • Tactical Setup Open every turn with 風 to reach enemy clusters for 合体攻撃 positioning, then shift to 火 before attacking or 山 before ending your turn near bosses.
  • Chassis Stats & Specials 光武・改 (HP 120, Atk 22, Def 15, Move 6, 狼虎滅却・天地一矢) · 天武 (HP 150, Atk 29, Def 19, Move 6, 狼虎滅却・三刃成虎) · 光武・改2 (HP 120, Atk 32, Def 23, Move 6, 狼虎滅却・天狼転化).
Configure Ogami's 硬派 or 軟派 alignment for the current run.

真宮寺さくら

Preferred Gauge 硬派 · Lucky Color Peach · Melee Duelist & Line Sweeper

  • LIPS & Ending Threshold Honest, earnest replies raise her trust easily, while peeping or teasing about other members triggers sharp jealousy drops. Available from Episode 1 onward, stay polite during her Episode 4 summer dress scene and Episode 10 New Year preparation.
  • Battle Role Balanced frontliner whose Special Attacks slice wide paths, making her ideal for clearing 御柱 and clustered infantry.
  • Chassis Stats & Specials 光武・改 (HP 120, Atk 17, Def 11, Move 5, 破邪剣征・桜花霧翔) · 天武 (HP 150, Atk 23, Def 16, Move 5, 破邪剣征・百花斉放) · 光武・改2 (HP 120, Atk 26, Def 20, Move 5, 破邪剣征極意・桜花爛満).
Complete 真宮寺さくら's 3-step Chain Event (Episodes 5, 6, 7) and Ending.

神崎すみれ

Preferred Gauge 軟派 · Kinematron 13800 · Lucky Color Purple · Short-Range All-Around Sweeper

  • LIPS & Ending Threshold Returns in Episode 3, so dial 13800 during Episodes 1 and 2 Free Events. Indulge her pride gracefully while stepping in firmly when she pushes too far. Complete her Tea Ceremony chain in Episodes 4, 6, and 7 (intentionally fail step 1 in Episode 4 so it continues).
  • Battle Role Hits all adjacent enemies within Range 2 simultaneously, great for softening groups around Ogami or clearing escort obstacles.
  • Chassis Stats & Specials 光武・改 (HP 120, Atk 16, Def 11, Move 5, 神崎風塵流・連雀の舞) · 天武 (HP 150, Atk 22, Def 16, Move 5, 神崎風塵流・飛竜の舞) · 光武・改2 (HP 120, Atk 25, Def 20, Move 5, 神崎風塵流奥義・不死鳥の舞).
Complete 神崎すみれ's Tea Chain (Episodes 4, 6, 7), 舞ガール, and Ending.

マリア・タチバナ

Preferred Gauge 硬派 · Kinematron 13900 · Lucky Color Black · Long-Range Sniper

  • LIPS & Ending Threshold Latest permanent joiner (joins at the Episode 4 boss fight), making キネマトロン calls across Episodes 1 to 4 mandatory for her route. Pick sincere, dutiful 硬派 answers and complete her Billiards Chain in Episodes 5, 6, and 7.
  • Battle Role Longest single-target range (Range 5) with high Attack (22 to 27) and lowest Defense (9 to 18). Keep her behind melee tanks or under かばう while sniping ranged cannons and bosses.
  • Chassis Stats & Specials 光武・改 (HP 120, Atk 22, Def 9, Move 5, ピーカヴァヤ・ダーマ) · 天武 (HP 150, Atk 24, Def 14, Move 5, トローイツァ) · 光武・改2 (HP 120, Atk 27, Def 18, Move 5, シェルクーンチク).
Complete マリア・タチバナ's Billiards Chain (Episodes 5, 6, 7), 雨に走れば, and Ending.

アイリス

Preferred Gauge 軟派 · Lucky Color Yellow · Squad Healer & Teleporter

  • LIPS & Ending Threshold Present from Episode 1. Treat her as an equal teammate rather than a helpless child, support her letters and arithmetic lessons in Episodes 2, 4, and 5, and answer honestly when her parents appear in Episode 10.
  • Battle Role Essential support unit whose Special Attacks restore HP to all allies in range and whose teleport movement ignores terrain gaps, though her regular Attack (13 to 23) stays low.
  • Chassis Stats & Specials 光武・改 (HP 120, Atk 13, Def 10, Move 5, イリス プティ・ジャンポール) · 天武 (HP 150, Atk 19, Def 15, Move 5, イリス ポワット・ド・スクール) · 光武・改2 (HP 120, Atk 23, Def 19, Move 5, イリス グラン・ジャンポール).
Complete アイリス's Arithmetic Chain (Episodes 4, 5, 7), 帝都の休日, and Ending.

李紅蘭

Preferred Gauge 軟派 · Kinematron 13700 · Lucky Color Green · Splash Artillery

  • LIPS & Ending Threshold Rejoins permanently in Episode 4, so call 13700 during Episodes 1 to 3 (also listen to 13510 before dialing in Episode 2). Play along with her 軟派 puns during her Hangar Chain Events in Episodes 5, 6, and 7 while remaining truthful.
  • Battle Role Mid-long artillery (Range 4, Splash 1) that hits clustered targets safely from behind the frontline and triggers frequent 協力攻撃 strikes from central tiles.
  • Chassis Stats & Specials 光武・改 (HP 120, Atk 16, Def 10, Move 5, 雀牌ロボ) · 天武 (HP 150, Atk 22, Def 15, Move 5, 球電ロボ) · 光武・改2 (HP 120, Atk 25, Def 19, Move 5, 聖獣ロボ・改).
Complete 李紅蘭's Pun Chain (Episodes 5, 6, 7), 烏！, and Ending.

桐島カンナ

Preferred Gauge 硬派 · Kinematron 14000 · Lucky Color Red · Heavy Melee Brawler

  • LIPS & Ending Threshold Rejoins in Episode 3, so call 14000 in Episodes 1 and 2. Answer sparring challenges head-on, finish all three massive meals in the Cafeteria during Episodes 4, 5, and 6, and treat her as a woman as well as a comrade.
  • Battle Role Highest raw Attack in the division (24 to 34) with solid Defense (13 to 22), able to one-shot standard 脇侍 and 黄童子 without buffs.
  • Chassis Stats & Specials 光武・改 (HP 120, Atk 24, Def 13, Move 5, 三進転掌) · 天武 (HP 150, Atk 31, Def 18, Move 5, 鷺牌五段) · 光武・改2 (HP 120, Atk 34, Def 22, Move 5, 三十六掌).
Complete 桐島カンナ's Cafeteria Chain (Episodes 4, 5, 6), クライマー・クライマー, and Ending.

ソレッタ・織姫

Preferred Gauge 軟派 · Lucky Color Pink · Mid-Range Area Sweeper

  • LIPS & Ending Threshold Her chimes stay silent until the Episode 7 boss battle, where secret Orihime Points convert into massive affection. Stand your ground calmly against her sarcasm, succeed in the Episode 1 night patrol persuasion, complete her Attic Chain Events in Episodes 2, 3, and 4, and click her door three times in Episode 7 Free Event B.
  • Battle Role Starts in her custom アイゼンクライト with 140 HP and Range 3 area fire (excluding directly adjacent tiles), pairing well with Iris on deck defense maps.
  • Chassis Stats & Specials アイゼンクライト (HP 140, Atk 17, Def 11, Move 6, クアットロ・スタジオーニ) · 天武 (HP 150, Atk 23, Def 15, Move 5, イル・フラウト・マジコ) · 光武・改2 (HP 120, Atk 24, Def 18, Move 5, ヴィアッジョ・ローズ).
Complete ソレッタ・織姫's Attic Chain (Episodes 2, 3, 4), ピアノ・レッスン？, and Ending.

レニ・ミルヒシュトラーセ

Preferred Gauge 硬派 · Lucky Color Blue · Precision Mid-Range Lancer

  • LIPS & Ending Threshold Joins in Episode 2, with trust chimes hidden until her secret is revealed (earliest in Episode 3 Locker Room, or Episode 4 Pool, or Episode 5 Beach). Answer her Battle LIPS tactical question in Episode 2 ("How should we attack?"), pronounce her surname as ミルヒシュトラーセ, and complete her Courtyard Dog Chain in Episodes 4, 5, and 7.
  • Battle Role Single-target Range 3 spear strikes with high Attack (21 to 31) and 140 starting HP, ideal for destroying obstacles and piercing bosses without standing adjacent.
  • Chassis Stats & Specials アイゼンクライト (HP 140, Atk 21, Def 12, Move 6, ダス・ラインゴルト) · 天武 (HP 150, Atk 28, Def 16, Move 5, ディ・ワリキューレ) · 光武・改2 (HP 120, Atk 31, Def 19, Move 5, ジークフリード).
Complete レニ・ミルヒシュトラーセ's Dog Chain (Episodes 4, 5, 7), おジョーズ！, and Ending.
