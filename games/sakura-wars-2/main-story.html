Episodes 1 to 12 Walkthrough

Spoiler-safe step-by-step progression across all three Dreamcast GD-ROM discs. Free Event clocks list exact active hours and ending triggers so you can gather ブロマイド portraits, play every mini-game, and maintain zero-retreat 優 battle ranks.

Episode 1 · 花萌える帝都

  • Prologue & Free Events A / B
    • At the 屋形船, answer 米田一基's question to pick your remembered heroine (requires SW1 clear data for 神崎すみれ, マリア・タチバナ, 李紅蘭, or 桐島カンナ). Answer Ginza at the pier for +3 Trust.
    • Free Event A (11:20 to 12:00, 8 visits). Visit the 事務局 first, check 空き部屋 if SW1 data is linked, buy 真宮寺さくら's ブロマイド at the 売店, and report to the Manager's Office between 11:50 and 12:00.
    • In Forced Event B, change cleanly by picking ていねいに脱ぐ, 服をたたむ, ズボンをはく, then 髪をとかす. When ソレッタ・織姫 leaves, chase after her for Orihime Points.
    • Accept Sakura's night patrol invitation. To convince Orihime (+6 points), click her hair, click her eyes then let タイムオーバー pass, click her mouth, then choose direct invitation, good change of pace, 10:00, and guiding her around the theater.
    • Free Event B (21:30 to 22:00, 6 visits). Revisit the 売店 while holding Sakura's portrait, answer William Shakespeare at 1F Seats and Turkish March at the Music Room, and visit the Costume Room to find Iris hiding in the shadow. In Forced Event C, answer 129 sheets for 高村椿's receipt question.
  • Battle · 帝劇前 (叉丹)
    • For Battle LIPS, stand beside Sakura ("About two years" grants +1 Trust and Coop) and Iris ("Did you grow taller?" grants +1 Trust). Having Orihime defeat 3+ enemies at once lets you praise Star Division (pick Eisenkleid or your command only if intentionally lowering her trust).
    • Switch to 火 stance and assign かばう to your priority heroine. When 叉丹 appears, protect Orihime during the ambush prompt for +3 Orihime Points (or let it time out for Sakura-Orihime Coop), then burst the boss down directly since defeating him ends the stage.

Episode 2 · アイリスの手紙

  • Free Events A / B & レニ・ミルヒシュトラーセ Arrival
    • Help either Sakura (talk to Kasumi, Yuri, and Tsubaki) or Iris (fetch plate from Kitchen and bring to Dressing Room). Free Event A (11:00 to 11:50, 12 visits) ends when returning to the Dressing Room after finishing your task.
    • Avoid entering Orihime's room early if you want her Attic Chain Event 1 and Music Room scene. Dial キネマトロン numbers for absent heroines (listen to 13510 before dialing Kohran at 13700) and visit Guest Entrance at 11:35 to 11:50 to see サキ.
    • In Forced Event B, pronounce Reni's full name ミルヒシュトラーセ accurately.
    • Free Event B (22:00 to 22:50, 12 visits) requires visiting the Prop Room (watch Saki's sprained ankle and click her free hand 3 times to finish cleanly without touching her ankle or chest) then heading to the Attic to end the phase.
  • Battle 1 · 鶯谷 (金剛)
    • Choosing Offense Focus in Forced Event B creates a cleaner lane layout than Defense Focus.
    • On Turn 1, answer Reni's tactical question ("How should we attack this enemy?") to raise trust and set her Episode 6 flag. Assign Reni to smash the line-firing 蒸気火箭 turrets (2 Defense, one-hit breakable).
    • Against 金剛, open with 風 to eliminate nearby 黄童子 on Turn 1, park Iris on the healing steam pad, then switch to 火 on Turn 2 for Special Attack burst.
  • Battle 2 · 渋谷 (木喰)
    • Choose Wait for Intel in Forced Event D so you only need to break the blue supply crates, clearing the first map in two turns via an Ogami-Iris 合体攻撃.
    • Against 木喰 (who acts 3 times per turn and deploys 円空 bits), climb quickly with 風, clear bits with a Combined Attack, and switch to 火 on Turn 2 to finish him before he retreats uphill.

Episode 3 · あぁ、お見合い

  • Free Event A & 桐島カンナ Sparring
    • Free Event A (14:20 to 15:00, 8 visits) ends automatically at 15:05. Visit the Attic for Orihime Chain 2, then visit the Training Room followed by the Locker Room (14:25 to 14:45) and scrub Reni's back to unlock Reni's secret early.
    • At the Terrace (14:25 to 15:00), click outside Sakura's face/body to earn +2 Trust without offending her.
    • In Forced Event A sparring against Kanna, pick Head Guard, Torso Guard, Crouch (wait for second half if needed), Jump, and Crouch + Low Kick to score +3 Trust.
  • 神崎邸 & 土蜘蛛 Battle
    • At the estate gate, choose whether to stop Kanna or egg her on (stopping her leaves a cleaner battle corridor and avoids Sumire's Episode 10 scolding). Crouch when dodging the ambush thrust (+3 Trust).
    • During the courtyard battle, choosing Sweeping the Enemies rewards speed with 風 and a Turn 2 合体攻撃. Against 土蜘蛛 (who phases through units), spend Sumire and Kanna's Specials on Turn 1 under 風 and switch to 火 on Turn 2.

Episode 4 · 大暴れ！火の玉芸者ガールズ

  • Hospital, Summer Uniforms & Free Event A
    • If injured stepping outside the hospital (-1 Move), heal in the Infirmary or Bathhouse (15 min) during Free Event A (15:00 to 16:00, 12 visits, with Kohran's room after 15:45 ending the phase).
    • Pick your preferred puppy name in the Salon, watching the compliment order if targeting Sumire or Orihime.
    • Key Free Event A targets include the Music Room for Orihime's ピアノ・レッスン？, Courtyard for Reni Chain 1 and Sakura's 明日に向かって斬れ！, Cafeteria (15:30+) for Kanna Chain 1, Salon (15:35+, or 15:05 after Stage) for Sumire Chain 1 (pick tea first so the chain continues), Attic (15:35+, or 15:05 after Music Room) for Orihime Chain 3 (stay silent without calling out), and Pool (15:45+) if Reni's secret is still hidden.
  • Deep River 料亭 & 火車 Battle
    • In the burning restaurant escape, open the sliding doors in reverse order of entry (Right, Center, Right, Left).
    • Choose Decoy Strategy before battle and bunch all units inside Iris's healing aura using 山 stance for four easy turns.
    • Against 火車, move Ogami adjacent to マリア・タチバナ and answer "...Welcome back" (+3 Trust and Coop), then surround Kasha under 火 stance to prevent him from fleeing three times per turn.

Episode 5 · 嬉し恥ずかし夏休み

  • Theater Packing, Bus トランプ大戦 & 熱海 Inn
    • Free Event A (09:00 to 10:00, 12 visits, ending at Entrance after 09:50). Hit Sakura's Room (09:00 to 09:25) for Sakura Chain 1, Maria's Room (click diary once) then Recreation Room for Maria Chain 1, Hangar for Kohran Chain 1, Courtyard for Reni Chain 2 and 烏！ (09:30+), and Cafeteria (09:30+) for Kanna Chain 2.
    • Play Daifugo with Iris on the bus to register トランプ大戦. On Day 2 choose your target heroine's excursion group, namely Sea (Sumire, Iris, Reni), Mountain (Kanna, Kohran, Orihime), or Inn Hot Spring (Sakura, Maria).
    • In Free Event B at the inn (10:20 to 10:55), visit 朱雀の間 BEFORE visiting all six other rooms or you miss Maria's scene there. Never repeat a room or time runs short.
  • Battle · 熱海 (水狐 & 金剛)
    • Requesting reinforcements from the Mountain offers tighter enemy clusters so you can clear all 脇侍 within 3 turns before heavy black units spawn.
    • Against 水狐 and 金剛, Kongou spawns two tiles behind Kohran and shields Suiko up to 9 times. Focus fire on Kongou first or ambush his spawn tile on Turn 1 under 風 then unleash 火 Specials on Turn 2.

Episode 6 · レニよ、銃をとれ

  • Disc 2 Opening & Free Events A / B
    • Free Event A (17:00 to 17:55, 12 visits, ending when entering Reni's room). Visit Attic (17:00 to 17:25) for Kanna's クライマー・クライマー, Recreation Room (17:00 to 17:25) for Maria Chain 2, Sakura's Room for Sakura Chain 2, Salon (17:30+) for Sumire Chain 2, Entrance (17:30+) for Maria's 雨に走れば, and Cafeteria (17:30+) for Kanna Chain 3.
    • Free Event B (23:00 to 23:30, 6 visits, ending at Reni's or Iris's room). Visit Sakura's Room if you promised to check on her, and visit the Hangar for Kohran Chain 2.
  • Battle · 池袋 (水狐 & 影山サキ)
    • Before battle, tell Iris to call everyone if you want earlier reinforcements, or stay with Ogami for extra Iris trust.
    • Destroy the 雷撃砲 early under 風, then brace with 山 before 水狐 triggers her high-damage range sweep or suicide 火乱 spheres.

Episode 7 · 季節はずれの七夕

  • Asakusa Festival & Free Events A / B
    • In Forced Event A at Asakusa, inspect the stalls to play Iris's 帝都の休日.
    • Free Event A (14:00 to 15:00, 12 visits, ending upon visiting Orihime's room). Complete Maria Chain 3 in Recreation Room (14:00 to 14:25), Salon Sumire Chain 3 (14:30+, warm cup with hot water then pour to the final drop), Courtyard Reni Chain 3 (14:00 to 14:25), Iris Room Chain 3 (14:30+), and Pool (14:30+) for Reni's おジョーズ！.
    • In Forced Event B, ask about both カリーノ・ソレッタ and Orihime. In Free Event B (21:30 to 22:00, 6 visits), visit Stage Wings for Sumire's 舞ガール, Hangar for Kohran Chain 3, and click Orihime's door 3 times last, choosing たしかに関係ない。でも…… and 卑怯だぞ、織姫くん！ to cash out Orihime Points before the battle.
  • Battle · 浅草 (火車)
    • Orihime's trust chimes fire at once during this battle, converting accumulated Orihime Points into massive stat bonuses.
    • Clear 十字焔 and escort drones quickly under 風, avoid leaving fragile allies in front of exploding 火乱, and corner 火車 with 火 Specials.

Episode 8 · 帝都の一番長い日！？

  • Theater Escape & 帝劇前 (木喰)
    • Escape underground with Sakura after speaking with Yoneda. At Hanayashiki, ask 加山雄一 to disable the cannons (Turn 3 destruction of 黒鬼砲 makes the stage much safer than disabling the barrier).
    • Switch to 風 to clear 帝防 walls and defeat 木喰.
  • Theater Recapture & 新宿 (土蜘蛛)
    • Before battle, read all briefing topics from 京極慶吾's files.
    • Use Special Attacks to smash obstacles rapidly under 風, then finish 土蜘蛛 cleanly with 火.
  • 赤坂 Route Choice & 闇神威 Battle
    • Keep a save before the route split on the 翔鯨丸. Infiltration fights through caves into 八葉 (start in 山 on Turn 1 to survive her opening Special, then switch to 火), whereas Frontal Assault fights heavy reinforcements into 大日剣 (kill 黄童子 first).
    • In the final 闇神威 battle, send two units south to destroy the 蒸気火箭 launchers and keep the squad together against awakening stone statues.

Episode 9 · 劇場のメリークリスマス

  • Free Event A, Helper Choice & Lead Selection
    • Free Event A (16:15 to 16:45, 5 visits, ending when visiting Kaede's Room after 16:40). Buy any missing regular ブロマイド portraits now, and answer Reni in the Dressing Room ("Is it Reni?").
    • Choose which Wind Division girl (Kasumi, Yuri, or Tsubaki) to help, then save at the 煉瓦亭 save prompt.
    • During Forced Event C night patrol, you meet the top four 恋愛度 heroines. Confirming your lead in Forced Event D locks that heroine through the ending.
  • 奇跡の鐘 & Christmas Eve Date
    • Direct the play's final acting cue to match your lead heroine for peak audience applause (Pure for Sakura/Maria/Iris/Reni, Bright for Kohran/Kanna, Elegant for Sumire/Orihime).
    • Complete Reni's birthday celebration and your chosen heroine's Christmas Eve date.

Episode 10 · 巷に雪の降るごとく

  • New Year's Free Event & スペシャルブロマイド
    • Free Event A (08:00 to 08:30, 6 visits, ending at Captain's Room). Visit the 売店 while holding all 8 regular bromides to buy one スペシャルブロマイド.
    • Say goodbye to departing squadmates in their rooms before entering Ogami's room.
  • Heroine New Year Excursion & 王子 Rescue
    • Enjoy your locked heroine's home-visit or New Year outing (including Iris's letter-memory callback and Kohran's gadget inspection at Howard's estate).
    • At 王子, stay in 風 stance to sweep enemy infantry quickly and rescue every Flower Division unit without retreats.

Episode 11 · 地上最大の作戦

  • Squad Split, Engine Room Defense & 土蜘蛛
    • Insert Disc 3. Your Episode 9 heroine automatically joins Ogami with maxed stat bonuses (+10 Atk, +6 Def, +2 Move). Pick three additional escorts (Kanna, Reni, and Maria make fast work of the boss, or bring Iris if you prefer heals) and keep a save before selecting.
    • Defend at least one of the seven 機関部 under 風 stance, prioritizing long-range green 降魔.
    • Against 土蜘蛛, watch its color shifts after Specials. Purple gains Attack when hit (burst fast with 火 or hold 山 if low on firepower), while White heals when struck (use that turn to recover and charge).
  • ミカサ Deck Interception & 土蜘蛛 Round 2
    • Prevent 8 降魔 from diving into the central vent and protect the four anti-aircraft guns. Pair one heavy hitter with one support unit on each flank (Kanna + Iris, Maria + Kohran, Reni + Sumire, Sakura + Orihime) under 風. Have Ogami kill 5 enemies while your heroine stands un-retreated for +3 Trust and Coop.
    • Round 2 against 土蜘蛛 must be cleared within 3 turns before he dives into the vent. Advance with 風 on Turn 1, then unleash 火 Specials on Turn 2.
  • 光武・改2 Upgrade, 武蔵 Cannons & 金剛
    • Speak with all eight squadmates aboard Mikasa to unlock 光武・改2 (check your heroine's cabin for unique dialogue).
    • During the cannon assault, step onto the glowing pipes beside cannons to neutralize them while destroying 魔来器 spawners with upgraded wide Specials.
    • Against 金剛, the squad is restricted to one action per turn for 10 turns. Start in 山 on Turn 1, box Kongou with melee units on Turn 2, eliminate the distant shielding 黄童子 on Turn 3, then finish with 火 Specials on Turn 4.

Episode 12 · 乙女たちの挽歌

  • 鬼王 & 御柱 Battles
    • Against 鬼王, align Sakura, Ogami, and your heroine directly behind a raised stone pillar on Turn 1 to block his opening Special, and pick "For Sakura too..." when adjacent to Sakura (+3 Trust and Coop). Track the real body via the Communications command after he clones himself on Turn 2, then burst him down before he cycles through all Five Demons abilities.
    • In the 御柱 chamber, split into fast 風 teams and focus on whichever pillar glows pink each turn (1 Defense), clearing all 8 pillars to end the map immediately.
  • Final Boss · 新皇 (Forms 1 to 3) & Ending
    • Ascend the stepped approach quickly under 風 to smash the turrets, then strike 新皇 whenever its hands open (20 Defense exposed versus 51 guarded).
    • Form 1. Hold 山 stance while attacking its two warp positions.
    • Form 2 (Both Arms & Drones). Never park pilots between the two arms. Have Iris or ranged units clear the 従うモノ, charge everyone's 気力 to full, and position melee units behind Kanna's side before landing the finishing hit in 火 stance (finishing Form 2 with a late-turn member like Kohran lets Ogami act immediately in Form 3).
    • Form 3 (Core). Survive the opening full-map Special with Iris's heal or 山 defense, then unleash a full-squad 火 Special and Combined Attack barrage.
    • Visit your chosen heroine in the post-battle Free Event (Sakura Grand Staircase, Sumire Salon, Maria Terrace, Iris Room, Kohran Hangar, Kanna Cafeteria, Orihime Music Room, Reni Courtyard) to watch her ending.

Secret Epilogue · 第十三話「君がために汽笛は鳴る」

  • Unlock & Clear Episode 13

    Triggered automatically after the credits whenever your cumulative War Record across Episodes 1 to 12 reaches 15 or more points (優 equals 2, 良 equals 1, 可 equals 0). Accept Yoneda's naval assignment and share a final station farewell with your chosen heroine, then save your Dreamcast clear file for 帝都の長い一日 and サクラ大戦3 〜巴里は燃えているか〜.
