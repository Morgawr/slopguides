Missables

Every Free Event runs on a strict clock, meaning chapter events, chain flags, ブロマイド purchases, and mini-game unlocks lock permanently once you trigger an ending room or let time expire. Use the filters below to verify every missable window before moving to the next episode.

Disc 1 Missables (Episodes 1 to 5)

  • Episode 1 空き部屋 Import Scene Only appears during Free Event A when a cleared サクラ大戦 Dreamcast save is linked at New Game. Visit before reporting to the Manager's Office at 12:00.
  • Episode 1 Night Patrol Persuasion & ソレッタ・織姫 Trust Persuading Orihime to join the patrol requires +6 points across the cursor and dialogue prompts. Failing locks out her theater patrol LIPS scenes at the Entrance, 1F Seats, and Music Room in Free Event B.
  • Episode 1 Shop Bonus & 伝票 Count Buying 真宮寺さくら's bromide in Free Event A grants extra trust when revisiting the shop in Free Event B. In Forced Event C, answer 129 sheets when asked about receipts.
  • Episode 2 ソレッタ・織姫 Chain 1 & サキ Encounter Entering Orihime's room in Free Event A cancels both her Attic Chain 1 and Music Room scenes. Also visit the Guest Entrance between 11:35 and 11:50 to meet サキ before Forced Event B.
  • Episode 3 レニ・ミルヒシュトラーセ Early Secret Reveal Visit the Training Room first in Free Event A, then enter the Locker Room between 14:25 and 14:45 and scrub her back to reveal her secret early and unlock her trust chimes. Missing this delays the reveal to Episode 4's Pool or Episode 5.
  • Episode 3 神崎邸 Gate Choice Telling Kanna to smash the gate alters the tactical battle layout and triggers an extra scolding event from Sumire during New Year's in Episode 10.
  • Episode 4 Ogami Injury Penalty & Healing Stepping in during Forced Event A without resisting properly inflicts -1 Move on Ogami's mecha in battle. Heal the injury before 16:00 by spending 15 minutes in the Bathhouse or visiting サキ in the Infirmary.
  • Episode 4 Summer Uniform Compliments & Chain Locks Complimenting three or more heroines before speaking to 神崎すみれ (or two before ソレッタ・織姫) reverses their trust gain. In Sumire Chain 1 at the Salon, intentionally pick tea first to continue the chain into Episodes 6 and 7.
  • Episode 4 Mini-Games (ピアノ・レッスン？ & 明日に向かって斬れ！) Play both Orihime's Music Room rhythm challenge and Sakura's Courtyard straw-cutting challenge during Free Event A so they register for 帝都の長い一日.
  • Episode 5 朱雀の間 & Bus トランプ大戦 Accept Iris's card game on the bus, then in Free Event B at the inn visit 朱雀の間 before finishing all six other rooms or Free Event B ends and locks out its scene.

Disc 2 & 3 Missables (Episodes 6 to 12)

  • Episode 6 Mini-Games (クライマー・クライマー & 雨に走れば) Visit the Attic before 17:25 for Kanna's climbing game and the Entrance after 17:30 for Maria's errand run before entering Reni's room.
  • Episode 7 Mini-Games (帝都の休日, おジョーズ！, 舞ガール) Inspect the festival stalls with Iris in Forced Event A, swim with Reni in Free Event A at 14:30, and spotlight Sumire in the Stage Wings during Free Event B before entering Orihime's room.
  • Episode 7 ソレッタ・織姫 Door & カリーノ・ソレッタ Questions In Forced Event B ask about both Carino and Orihime. In Free Event B click Orihime's door three times and pick the sympathetic responses to cash out her accumulated Orihime Points during the battle.
  • Episode 8 Route Split & Episode 10 スペシャルブロマイド Purchase all 8 regular Flower Division bromides before Episode 10 Free Event A (08:00 to 08:30), then visit the Souvenir Shop before entering Ogami's office to claim one Special Bromide. Note that Episode 8 also branches between Infiltration and Frontal Assault routes at Akasaka.
  • Episode 11 ミカサ Squad Split & Separate Unit Dialogue Selecting three teammates to accompany Ogami and your Episode 9 heroine grants +1 Atk and +1 Def to those picks, while leaving 李紅蘭, ソレッタ・織姫, or レニ・ミルヒシュトラーセ on the detached squad shows unique reunion lines during the Tsugumo fight.
  • Secret 第十三話「君がために汽笛は鳴る」 Earn at least 15 total War Record points (優 equals 2, 良 equals 1, 可 equals 0) by the end of Episode 12. Falling below 15 skips the post-credits farewell epilogue.
