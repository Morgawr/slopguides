Play Order & Endings

Every playthrough locks in a single heroine at the クリスマス公演 in Episode 9 from the four members with the highest cumulative 恋愛度. Planning save anchors and gauge leanings lets you complete all eight endings, unlock the secret epilogue, and prepare Dreamcast clear saves for later entries in minimal runs.

Chronological Guide Flow

Recommended Reading Sequence

  • Before Episode 1 Read Beginner Tips and Game Mechanics, then verify your サクラ大戦 save on the same ビジュアルメモリ.
  • Episodes 1 to 8 Follow Episodes 1 to 12 while checking Missables before every Free Event so you buy all eight regular ブロマイド items, trigger chain events, and play each mini-game.
  • Episode 9 Selection Choose your target heroine from the top four 恋愛度 candidates during Forced Event D. Your choice governs Episode 10's New Year excursion, Episode 11's partner bonuses, Episode 12's finale scene, and the ending.
  • Episodes 11 to 12 & Epilogue Keep battle retreats to zero wherever possible so your cumulative war record reaches at least 15 points by the end of Episode 12, unlocking 第十三話「君がために汽笛は鳴る」.
  • Post-Game Load your cleared system data on Disc 2 or Disc 3 to enter 帝都の長い一日 and complete Bromides & Extras.

Two-Run Save Branching Strategy

Covering Eight Heroines in Two Playthroughs

Because Episode 9 presents the top four 恋愛度 candidates, splitting your campaign into a 硬派 playthrough and a 軟派 playthrough covers all eight heroines cleanly with one Episode 9 save branch per run.

  • Run 1 硬派 Quartet Setup Favor 真宮寺さくら, マリア・タチバナ, 桐島カンナ, and レニ・ミルヒシュトラーセ across Episodes 1 to 8, calling Maria and Kanna on the キネマトロン whenever absent.
  • Run 1 Anchor Save at Episode 9 Forced Event C Save before entering the dressing room in Episode 9 so all four 硬派 heroines appear in the top four selection list, then clear Episodes 10 to 12 for each one.
  • Run 2 軟派 Quartet Setup Push the gauge pink across Episodes 1 to 8 while prioritizing 神崎すみれ, アイリス, 李紅蘭, and ソレッタ・織姫, accumulating Orihime Points before Episode 7.
  • Clear Data Carryover for Sequels Keep your final clear save on a ビジュアルメモリ for サクラ大戦3 〜巴里は燃えているか〜 and サクラ大戦4 〜恋せよ乙女〜 to unlock reminiscence photos and bonus dialogue.

All 8 Heroine Endings & Recommended Order

Completion Checklist

  • 1. 真宮寺さくら Ending Recommended first run due to natural story centrality, frequent appearances, and high 硬派 compatibility.
  • 2. マリア・タチバナ Ending Pair with Run 1 via キネマトロン calls in Episodes 1 to 4 and the hot spring path in Episode 5.
  • 3. 桐島カンナ Ending Pair with Run 1 by sparring in Episode 3, completing her three cafeteria meals, and calling 14000 in Episodes 1 to 3.
  • 4. レニ・ミルヒシュトラーセ Ending Fourth slot of Run 1, requiring her bathhouse reveal in Episode 3 or pool reveal in Episode 4 plus all three dog chain events.
  • 5. 神崎すみれ Ending First clear of Run 2, calling 13800 in Episodes 1 to 3 and completing her tea ceremony chain events.
  • 6. 李紅蘭 Ending Second clear of Run 2, favoring 軟派 jokes, calling 13700 early, and completing her pun chain events.
  • 7. アイリス Ending Third clear of Run 2, helping her write the Episode 2 letter, answering arithmetic lessons, and treating her as an equal teammate.
  • 8. ソレッタ・織姫 Ending Fourth clear of Run 2, prioritizing Orihime Points in Episodes 1, 2, 4, and 7 so her Episode 7 burst pushes her into the top four.
