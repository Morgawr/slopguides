Souvenir Shop Bromides (9 Entries)

Buying Rules & Episode 10 Unlock

Starting in Episode 1 Free Event A, the 1F 売店 sells one regular ブロマイド per Free Event visit. Buying an absent member's portrait also reveals her キネマトロン number when you check rumors at the 事務局. Collect all eight regular portraits before Episode 10 Free Event A (08:00 to 08:30) to unlock one スペシャルブロマイド for any heroine.

9 Story Mini-Games

Campaign Unlock Locations & Score Strategies

  • 明日に向かって斬れ！ Episode 4 Free Event A, Courtyard. Slice flying straw bundles on the center line (2,500+ pts gives 3U, 1,000+ gives 1U). Clearing 20 straight bundles extends the round by 30 more. Dreamcast controller timing runs tighter than Saturn.
  • ピアノ・レッスン？ Episode 4 Free Event A, Music Room. Shift scrolling notes up with sharp and down with flat to match target staves (4+ lives left gives 3U, 1 to 3 gives 1U). Simply nudging notes up and down scores 1 point per input.
  • 烏！ Episode 5 Free Event A, Courtyard (09:30 to 10:00). Throw smoke pellets at crows targeting the tomato patch (15 max ammo, 4+ seedlings saved gives 3U). Hit red crows twice and eliminate green crows fast before they summon black flocks, chaining smoke clouds for bonus points.
  • トランプ大戦 Episode 5 Forced Event B, Bus Ride. Play 1v3 Daifugo to 16 cumulative points (4/2/1/0 per round). Ranks go 3 up to 2 and Joker, with no sequences, 8-clears, or winning on a 2 (or 3 during revolution).
  • クライマー・クライマー Episode 6 Free Event A, Attic (17:00 to 17:25). Swap between fast Kanna and double-jumping Ogami to collect screws (1,500+ pts gives 3U). Avoid falling into deep pits, as falling can freeze round completion until reset.
  • 雨に走れば Episode 6 Free Event A, Entrance (17:30 to 17:55). Dodge puddles while countering crosswinds by tapping the opposite direction (50%+ gauge gives 3U). Ride tailwinds whenever Maria calls out the wind.
  • 帝都の休日 Episode 7 Forced Event A, Asakusa stalls. Guide Iris into flashing white shops while dodging pedestrians (700+ pts gives 3U). Standing at the bottom of the right red shop and touching passing chindon bands (~22s in, then every 100s) farms high scores safely.
  • おジョーズ！ Episode 7 Free Event A, Pool (14:30 to 15:00). Collect targets while dodging mines (1,500+ pts gives 3U). Focus on orange targets first and hover near the center ladder for safety, as pink and yellow targets spawn extra mines.
  • 舞ガール Episode 7 Free Event B, Stage Wings. Keep the spotlight on Sumire without enlarging it except when the bottom-left prompt demands Light Up Chance (hold the button down instead of mashing, 4,000+ pts gives 3U). Track her shadow during jumps.

Long Day & Dreamcast Extras

Post-Game 帝都の長い一日 Facilities

After clearing the game, loading your cleared save adds 帝劇の長い一日 to the schedule menu. Choosing to dream of the past replays Softdec movies specific to whichever of the 3 GD-ROM discs is inserted, while exploring the theater unlocks rooms below.

  • 戦闘演習機 (Kaede's Room) Replay Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced tactical combat drills at any time.
  • 思い出 (1F Heroine Memories) View heroine-perspective flashbacks shaped by your LIPS answers across key chapters (Sakura Prop Room, Sumire Costume Room, Maria Lobby, Iris Entrance, Kohran Guest Entrance, Kanna Kitchen, Orihime Dressing Room, Reni Stage Wings).
  • Disc-Specific Softdec Movie & Terrace Galleries Browse collected bromides and soundtrack BGM on the Terrace, and boot Discs 1, 2, and 3 to view each disc's animated cutscenes.
