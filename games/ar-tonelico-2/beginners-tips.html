Beginner Tips

Unlike the first game, battle turn flow in アルトネリコ2 pairs timed active-guard inputs with dual-backline synchronization and bath-house Reyvateil growth. Build these six habits in パスタリア to stay ahead of every Phase spike.

Tips Applied: 0/6 (0%)

Core Pastalia & Combat Habits

  • Treat Defense Phases as your primary meter generator. Timing your button presses to achieve Perfect Guard cuts incoming damage near zero while filling ハーモニクス and advancing enemy Break gauges rapidly.
  • Level your Reyvateils at デュアルストール regularly. Normal battles yield zero EXP for ルカ, クローシェ, and ジャクリ. Bathe with 入浴剤 and デュアリスノ結晶 whenever they accumulate to boost stats and trigger bath トークマター.
  • Press SELECT in every field zone to scan the I.P.D. radar. Roaming encounters display on area frequency meters, letting you clear each map before advancing story flags and preventing tedious endgame backtracking.
  • Pause both heroines at Cosmosphere Lv.5 for route flexibility. Keep a permanent save prior to triggering any Lv.5 パラダイムシフト so you can lock ジャクリ or ココナ endings without replaying Phase 1 through Phase 3.
  • Upgrade shops and 調合 recipes with new hires. Recruit both shopkeepers and assign rescued I.P.D. girls through ダイバーズセラピ as soon as therapy items unlock in shops.
  • Prepare Lv.4 burst Song Magic and Fan Club stacks before Phase 5. Growing クローシェ's 親衛隊 powers レプレキア past 40,000 damage, which doubles as your clean bypass for the retail NISA ラキ turn-6 freeze.
