I.P.D. 100 Rescue & Fan Club Tracker

Rescuing and curing all 100 rogue I.P.D. citizens strengthens Reyvateil MP supply, fills クローシェ's 親衛隊, and maximizes レプレキア damage. Press SELECT on any field map to display remaining roaming targets.

Phase 5 Migration Guarantee & Diver's Therapy

Early areas such as 月下水路 and ミント区 seal permanently in early Phase 5, while roaming I.P.D.s migrate automatically to open late-game maps so all 100 remain obtainable. Curing captured targets via ダイバーズセラピ costs Level × 100 Leaf plus occasional synthesis items (such as Pippencuits or Herbal Jewels).

I.P.D. Tiers & Milestones: 0/12 (0%)

I.P.D. Suppression Milestones (Lv.1 – Lv.9 & Special Targets)

  • Clear all Lv.1 I.P.D.s (Pastalia, Mint District & Lower Moon Culvert). Establish basic Diver's Therapy routines early and convert initial rescues into starter 親衛隊 buffs.
  • Clear all Lv.2 I.P.D.s (Mikuri Forest & Rakshek approaches). Sweep early forest paths with SELECT radar active before entering Phase 2 routes.
  • Clear all Lv.3 I.P.D.s including ねねこ at Clouds Wharf. Bring mid-game armor and Harmonics buffers for ねねこ, since her stationary encounter summons Lollipop escorts far stronger than typical Lv.3 fights.
  • Clear all Lv.4 I.P.D.s including 那紗 in Verticave. Watch for high-pressure defense breaks from 那紗 inside ヴェルティカブ before attempting deep dungeon runs.
  • Clear all Lv.5 I.P.D.s (Phase 2 Route Zones & Enna's City). Sweep radar counts in route-specific outposts around エナの街 and ラクシャク before story progression locks them out.
  • Clear all Lv.6 I.P.D.s (Cat Mansion & Phase 3 Dungeons). Rescue targets along 猫屋敷 and recruit shop/synthesis expansion candidates through Therapy.
  • Clear all Lv.7 I.P.D.s (Gyro Stabilizer & Heavenly Tower). Utilize Synchronity Chains and Perfect Guards against high-speed jamming enemies during Phase 4 ascents.
  • Clear all Lv.8 I.P.D.s (Promise Hill & Metafalica Hill). Keep synthesized Therapy consumables stocked in reserve so every rescued I.P.D. enters 親衛隊 duty immediately.
  • Defeat every Lv.9 I.P.D. in ソル・マルタ and migrated zones. Eliminating random Lv.9 threats adds hidden treasure chests inside dungeons containing endgame デュアリスノ結晶.
  • Cure all 100 captured I.P.D.s via ダイバーズセラピ. Verify zero un-treated individuals remain inside ルカ's Therapy menu using matching item handouts and supportive dialogue picks.
  • Maximize クローシェ's 親衛隊 & ムスメパワード boosts. Allocate healed members across Fan Club slots to unlock massive stats and maximum レプレキア scaling before ラキ.
  • Confirm 100/100 I.P.D. completion & Cloche Fan Club events. Talk with Fan Club coordinators and check your system completion record prior to entering the Phase 5 final battle.
