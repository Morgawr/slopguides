Party Builds, Gear & Dualstall

Standard battles level only your two frontline Vanguards, while all three レーヴァテイル grow exclusively through デュアルストール baths combining 入浴剤 and デュアリスノ結晶. Pair strong vanguard-reyvateil affinity pairings to accelerate Synchronicity burst charging.

Vanguard Characters (Frontline)

クロア・バーテル

Protagonist wielding balanced halberd strikes, reliable Break damage, and strong natural compatibility across heroines.

  • Acquire フレリアの閃光 over high-variance synthetic poles. Outperforms ジャンバジャンブルグランデ in consistency during Lv.9 I.P.D. and Phase 5 boss encounters.
  • Loot ソルガード in Sol Marta. Exclusive high-defense plate paired with Break and Burst-building accessories.

ココナ・バーテル

High-hit speed striker generating massive Harmonics gauge increases on single Attack Phases.

  • Equip ブレイブバトン. Maximizes Cocona's multi-hit attack chains to jump Harmonics to Lv.3 rapidly.
  • Obtain 忍少女のマント. Grants top evasion and physical mitigation for frontline survival against endgame counters.

アマリエ・ジェラード

Versatile archer and Sacred Army researcher whose party-wide recovery skill 安息の奏で stabilizes late-game boss marathons.

  • Claim リクドウハジャ. Highest raw bow attack available for Phase 4–5 dungeons and Lv.9 I.P.D. suppression.
  • Equip 忍少女のマント and keep 安息の奏で slotted. Turns Amarie into a reliable sustain engine while both Reyvateils focus purely on offensive Dual Magic.

レグリス・ブランシェスカ

Grand Bell Knight Commander specializing in heavy dual-blade cleaves and guarding fragile party setups.

  • Acquire デュアルレイザー. Provides top-end attack and Break pressure during Phase 5 fights.
  • Equip 師範の道着. Anchors high DEF/HP thresholds so missed timed guards never break frontline stability.

Fifth Vanguard: シュン (Joins Phase 5)

  • Recruit シュン at Clouds Wharf and equip セイバーファング. High-multiplier martial blade commands (アインセイバー, Head Slash) shred Phase 5 enemies.
  • Equip シールドコア. Protects Shun inside ソル・マルタ where heavy multi-target sweeps punish light armor.

Reyvateils (Backline Singers)

瑠珈・トゥルーリーワース

Technical shield, ailment cure, and balanced Green/Red singer central to steady Synchronity chaining.

  • Equip ハーモボディスーツ from Sol Marta. +103 DEF top armor protects Luca against late boss splash waves.
  • Socket MP Sustain & Spirit デュアリスノ結晶. Use デュアルストール baths with 精神, 守護, and 成長 for deep MP pools.

クローシェ・レーテル・パスタリエ

Burst powerhouse scaling with 親衛隊 headcount and レプレキア damage multipliers.

  • Equip ハーモボディスーツ and ムスメパワード boosts. Tune Fan Club passives toward Burst Multiplier and Charge Speed before Phase 5 boss battles.
  • Bath-stack Flame, Destruction & God デュアリスノ結晶. Maximize MATK/Burst growth prior to triggering レプレキア against ラキ.

ジャクリ

Phase 3 powerhouse commanding extreme offensive Hymmnos and high-cap 合体詩魔法 multipliers.

  • Equip ハーモボディスーツ and Lv.4 Song slots. Pair Jacqli with Cloche inside ソル・マルタ to fire top Dual Magic (ファンタスマゴリア, ロリポップスイーツ).
  • Focus Darkness, Destruction & World crystals in デュアルストール. Pushes her raw offensive song output to wipe Lv.9 I.P.D. encounters inside one Synchronity Chain.
