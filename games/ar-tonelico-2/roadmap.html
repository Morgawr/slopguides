Play Order & Ending Roadmap

アルトネリコ2 features two Phase 2 story branches and four character endings determined by your Lv.5 パラダイムシフト commitments. Only one heroine can enter Lv.6 through Lv.9 on a single save file.

All Four Endings Matrix
Ending Phase 1 Choice Lv.5 Paradigm Shift Completion Target
ルカ Ending ルカを護る Commit on ルカ Lv.5 Clear Luca Lv.9 before Phase 5 boss
クローシェ Ending クローシェ様を護る Commit on クローシェ Lv.5 Clear Cloche Lv.9 (incl. Lv.6-R)
ジャクリ Ending Either choice Refuse Luca & Cloche Lv.5; commit ジャクリ Lv.5 Clear Jacqli Lv.9
ココナ Ending Either choice Stop all 3 Reyvateils at Lv.5 or refuse commitment Complete Phase 5 unattached

Recommended Two-Run Completion Order

  • Run 1 Save Archive A: Pre-Phase 1 Split. Create a permanent save right before choosing between ルカを護る and クローシェ様を護る at the close of Phase 1.
  • Run 1 Choice: ルカを護る. Push ルカ's Cosmosphere up to Lv.5 in Phase 2 without triggering パラダイムシフト yet.
  • Explore Cloche Lv.4 before Phase 4 locks Talk Topics. Check exclusive Topic pairs between ルカ Lv.2 and クローシェ Lv.4 while both remain open at Lv.1 through Lv.5.
  • Run 1 Save Archive B: Pre-Paradigm Shift Lock. Make a backup save once ルカ, クローシェ, and ジャクリ are all staged cleanly at Lv.5 entry.
  • Finish Luca Route & Ending. Confirm ルカ's Lv.5 Paradigm Shift, clear Lv.6 through Lv.9, resolve インフェルスフィア Lv.5, rescue all 100 I.P.D.s, and beat Phase 5.
  • Branch from Save Archive B: Clear Jacqli Ending. Reload Archive B, bypass Luca's Lv.5 lock, accept ジャクリ's Lv.5 Paradigm Shift, and clear Lv.6 through Lv.9 (Jacqli CS).
  • Branch from Save Archive B: Clear Cocona Ending. Reload Archive B, hold all three レーヴァテイル at Lv.5 or below (or choose to stay friends), and clear the final boss for ココナ's ending.
  • Run 2 from Archive A or NG+: クローシェ様を護る. Play through Cloche's unique Phase 2 story stages (ラクシャク underground events and 大鐘堂 perspective).
  • Unlock リドゥチャーム routes in Cloche CS. Use Redo Charms in Cloche Lv.4 and complete both Lv.6 and Lv.6-R so her Topic completion hits 100%.
  • Complete Cloche Ending & Extras Menu. Clear Cloche Lv.9 and finish Phase 5 to register all four endings, Dual Magic variants, and Extras gallery unlocks across your system file.
