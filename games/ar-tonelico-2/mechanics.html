Core Combat & Bath Systems

Understanding four interlocking systems makes every late-game encounter in アルトネリコ2 smooth: Active Timed Guards, インフェルスフィア Dual Magic, レプレキア scaling, and デュアルストール bathing.

1. Dual-Heroine Harmonics & Synchronity Chains

Attack Phases vs. Defense Phases

  • Two Vanguards + Two Reyvateils. Both backline singers chant simultaneously, enabling cross-casting and shared Harmonics buildup that unlocks Level 1 through Level 3 Vanguard Sync attacks.
  • Timed Defense builds Harmonics faster than normal swings. Pressing the button matching enemy strike prompts scores Perfect Guards, negating damage, recovering Break shields, and filling burst gauges cleanly.
  • Synchronity Chain triggers 合体詩魔法. Filling both Heroines' Harmonics bars merges their active spells into signature Dual Magic attacks such as インバートブリッド, デモンジェル, ロリポップスイーツ, and ファンタスマゴリア.

2. Method Replakia & Fan Club Power

Cloche Fan Club & Musume Powered

Every cured citizen from your I.P.D. rescue log joins クローシェ's 親衛隊. Adjusting ムスメパワード boosts active multipliers, while hitting Attack Sync Level 3 opens レプレキア to push Burst damage past standard caps against Phase 5 bosses.

3. Dualstall Growth & Infelsphere

Levelling Backline Singers at Camp

  • Mix 入浴剤, crystals, and bath toys. Pairing high-tier デュアリスノ結晶 (成長, 世界, 神, 破壊) with items such as 歩くゲルゴ yields extra level gains and triggers unique bath トークマター.
  • Clear インフェルスフィア Lv.1–Lv.5 alongside story Phases. Unlike individual Cosmospheres, Infelsphere binds both main heroines together, granting Dual Magic unlocks and shared story progression across Phase 2 through Phase 5.
