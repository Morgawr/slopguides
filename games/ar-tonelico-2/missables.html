Missables & Critical Bugs

Zone accessibility changes drastically between Phase 2 and Phase 5 in アルトネリコ2, and the North American PS2 disc carries a notorious Phase 5 freeze bug. Track every lock window and bug countermeasure below before clearing dungeons.

US PS2 Hardware Bug · Phase 5 Raki Freeze Bypass

In NISA's original English PS2 localization, the battle against ラキ crashes on Turn 6 (Defense Phase 3) because the localization left a bad pointer (0x165) for her scripted attack フラクタルチェンジ. Playing with the fan patch Project Metafalica eliminates this bug. On unmodified US discs, build 親衛隊 stacks for レプレキア and unleash Level 4 Song Magic to wipe her 40,000+ HP bar inside 5 turns.

Route Splits & Temporary Zones (Phase 1 – Phase 4)

  • Save before Phase 1's ルカ vs. クローシェ choice. Picking one locks out the other partner's route maps, recipes, and Phase 2 boss encounters for the rest of the playthrough.
  • Sweep Lower Moon Culvert chests before early Phase 5. Early zones including 月下水路 and Mint District seal permanently in early Phase 5. Collect all treasure boxes and synthesis recipes early even though roaming I.P.D.s migrate safely to open areas.
  • Sweep Phase 2 route-exclusive dungeons on your first visit. Maps exclusive to Luca's or Cloche's Phase 2 mission contain unique chests and boss drops that vanish once both paths converge.
  • Complete Phase 1–4 side quests and shop unlocks early. Shopkeepers, town NPCs, and 大鐘堂 districts shift after story upheavals, locking early quest rewards if ignored.
  • Buy out 猫屋敷 and regional specialty shops before Phase 5. Pick up limited 入浴剤 sets, toys such as 歩くゲルゴ, and exclusive synthesis recipes during Phase 3 and Phase 4.
  • Respect early dangerous I.P.D. spikes rather than under-saving. Lv.3 ねねこ at 雲の波止場 and Lv.4 那紗 inside ヴェルティカブ pack mid-game Lollipop and guard-breaking escorts capable of wiping unbuffered parties.

Talk Topics, Cosmosphere Branches & Phase 5 Locks

  • Navigate the mutually exclusive Luca Lv.2 / Cloche Lv.4 Topic conflict. Clearing Luca Lv.2 and Cloche Lv.4 affects mutual Topic availability such as シンシアのこと; plan which gallery Topics you prioritize on your current route.
  • Hold all three heroines at Lv.5 before committing a Paradigm Shift. Crossing Lv.5 into Lv.6 with ルカ or クローシェ permanently locks ジャクリ at Lv.5 and cancels ココナ's ending on that save slot.
  • Use リドゥチャーム items inside Cloche Lv.4 and Jacqli Lv.7/Lv.8. Multiple branching sub-levels (クローシェ Lv.6-R and ジャクリ Lv.7-C / Lv.7-P / Lv.8-2) hide distinct costumes, songs, and Topics behind Redo runs.
  • Advance インフェルスフィア Lv.1–5 steadily per Phase. Completing Infelsphere levels unlocks essential 合体詩魔法 combos and keeps both heroines synchronized for climax story fights.
  • Stack Burst Harmonics and レプレキア prior to Phase 5 Raki. Prepare Lv.4 Songs, high 親衛隊 headcount via rescued I.P.D.s, and offensive crystal setups so Raki falls on or before Turn 5 on retail US discs.
  • Defeat optional Raki rematches and collect ソル・マルタ Lv.9 I.P.D.s before final boss. Defeating Lv.9 I.P.D.s spawns extra dungeon chests containing rare デュアリスノ結晶, and repeated sparring bouts with Raki unlock bonus gallery CGs.
