Game Mechanics

Matches combine real-time pitch positioning with limited-area tactical duels driven by a shared テンション gauge. Understanding how each duel phase generates or spends resources, alongside elemental multipliers and rarity ceilings, lets you outplay higher-level opponents consistently.

Match Phases & Tension

Every midfield clash, loose ball, and goalmouth shot enters a dedicated tactical phase. Winning early duels fills the team-wide テンション meter needed for 必殺技 and 超次元タクティクス.

フォーカス

Triggered when a ball carrier enters range of an opposing defender in midfield. The attacker aims a breakthrough direction or passes, while the defender times an interception or tackle. Winning a フォーカス battle awards テンション and item drops such as スピリット. Using raw directional breakthroughs preserves gauge for shooting.

スクランブル

Triggered when a loose ball drops between opposing players. Time slows briefly so you can input the next action to claim possession and shift team momentum.

ゾーン and キャッスルウォール

Triggered near the penalty box during shots. The attacking side aims shot trajectories and triggers chain shots, while the defending side enters キャッスルウォール and manually drags up to two defenders into the ball's path to weaken or block the shot before it reaches the goalkeeper.

テンション

A shared team resource gauge. Winning フォーカス duels, claiming スクランブル balls, and clean defending build the gauge, while 必殺技 and tactics consume it.

Player Rarities

Rarity determines a player's stat ceiling and how freely you can customize their passive skills.

Abilearn Board & Beans

Each character upgrades through the アビラーボード, branching between stat nodes and new 必殺技. Some story nodes unlock only after reaching specific chapters. Upgrading branches consumes 特訓ビーンズ, so distribute beans to your core starters first and check branch paths before spending rare bean types.

Hyper Power-Ups

In クロニクルモード and endgame squads, players can activate 化身, ミキシマックス, and Awakening states. Summoning a 化身 boosts parameters and unlocks signature Keshin techniques, making timing around opposing summons critical in late historical routes.
