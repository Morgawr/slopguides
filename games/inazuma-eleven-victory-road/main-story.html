Main Story Walkthrough

0 / 10 (0%)

The campaign spans nine chapters plus a short postgame encounter, moving from town exploration and club recruitment into full 11-on-11 tournament matches. Links to newly unlocked side quests appear at the exact points they become available so you can clear them before advancing.

Chapter 1: 君はサッカーができる

  • Create your avatar and follow 笹波雲明 through 南雲原中学校.
  • Explore 南雲原 street, the seaside park, and 南雲原総合病院前 to unlock Chapter 1 Side Quests.
  • Defeat キングビッグ海坊主 in the chapter's introductory clash.

Chapter 2: どん底から最高へ

  • Complete the dance showdown with 忍原来夏 to recruit her into the emerging club.
  • Clear the newly unlocked Chapter 2 Side Quests around school and town to build early 特訓ビーンズ and gear.
  • Practice フォーカス battles and basic special training drills before advancing.

Chapter 3: サッカーが変わる日

  • Assemble the core early roster including 桜咲丈二, 木曽路兵太, and 四川堂我流.
  • Check Chapter 3 Side Quests as new town areas open.
  • Defeat the baseball club in the chapter's football battle to formalize the 南雲原中学校 club.

Chapter 4: 襲来 サッカーモンスター！！

  • Review the 15 セレクトキャラ on Missables and pick 5 recruits to fill your starting 11 (prioritize defenders to balance the squad).
  • Clear Chapter 4 Side Quests and upgrade アビラーボード nodes for your new recruits.
  • Meet the training quota and complete the chapter match featuring 西ノ宮中 and 円堂ハル.

Chapter 5: 泣き虫ライバル

  • Unlock expanded 絆クローバー scouting and special training regimens.
  • Clear Chapter 5 Side Quests and use town food buffs before grinding the chapter's training quota.
  • Win the official match against 北陽学園 by managing テンション for goalmouth ゾーン shots.

Chapter 6: 南雲原よ ひとつになれ

  • Clear Chapter 6 Side Quests to gather upgraded boots and pendants.
  • Prepare elemental coverage across your midfield and defense, equipping 風, 林, 火, and 山 special moves.
  • Defeat 東風異国館 in the tournament bracket.

Chapter 7: 化身は嵐の中に

  • Clear Chapter 7 Side Quests before the tournament pace accelerates.
  • Train your squad two to three levels above the chapter quota and assign 超次元タクティクス for defensive coverage.
  • Win the storm-phase tournament match using キャッスルウォール positioning to blunt opposing power shots.

Chapter 8: アリスの世界

  • Finish any remaining Chapter 8 Side Quests and finalize equipment upgrades.
  • Face 帝国学園, keeping defenders ready in the ゾーン to intercept curved shots.

Chapter 9: 雲から太陽はのぞく

  • Spend saved 特訓ビーンズ and verify your アビラーボード routes are maxed for your starting 11.
  • Win the finals against 雷門中学校 and 円堂ハル.
  • Clearing Chapter 9 rolls the credits and unlocks the remaining 10 Chapter 4 セレクトキャラ for scouting.

Postgame Encounter: Extended Story Boss

  • Immediately after the credits, complete the final postgame boss match to conclude the story arc.
  • Full mode integration unlocks afterward, allowing you to carry story progress into Chronicle Mode and online competition.
