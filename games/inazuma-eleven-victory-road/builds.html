Team Archetypes

Stacking passives from a single archetype activates its dedicated bonus structure. For reliable progression across both クロニクルモード and ranked matches, カウンター and キズナ offer the most consistent ceiling, while 正義 excels in clean PvE farming.

Archetype Core Trigger Best Application
カウンター Stacks Focus AT/DF, Shot AT, and Keeper Power after defending opponent actions Ranked PvP and high-level Hero Battles
キズナ Builds Bond Power through passes and scrambles to scale team AT/DF Possession play and chain-shot offenses
正義 Grants large AT/DF buffs while avoiding fouls Auto-grinding and disciplined PvE clears
テンション Accelerates Tension gain and rewards high gauge reserves Special Move-heavy lineups and hybrid builds
ラフプレー Boosts AT/DF through dash knockbacks and aggressive tackles Disrupting possession-based opponents
ブリーチ Increases RNG breakthrough chance past blocks and saves High-variance upset setups

桜咲丈二 (Primary Striker)

桜咲丈二 serves as your main forward from Chapter 3 through postgame. Focusing his アビラーボード on Shot AT and Kick power turns him into a reliable finisher inside the ゾーン.

Stage Target Priority Focus
Chapters 3 to 5 Unlock primary Shot node Equip Kick-boosting boots and matching-element shoot move
Chapters 6 to 9 Abilearn Shot AT branch Feed Attack and Kick 特訓ビーンズ
Postgame Legendary / Counter or Bond passives Stack Shot AT and Tension-efficiency passives

木曽路兵太 (Midfield Engine)

木曽路兵太 anchors midfield フォーカス duels to generate テンション for your forwards. Building him for Technique and Agility keeps possession flowing cleanly.

Stage Target Priority Focus
Chapters 3 to 5 Dribble / Block balance Win midfield Focus duels without overspending Tension
Chapters 6 to 9 Technique & Agility nodes Equip Speed and Technique gear
Postgame Tension or Bond generator passives Stack Focus AT/DF and Tension-gain passives

四川堂我流 (Goalkeeper Anchor)

四川堂我流 guards the goal throughout the campaign. Investing into Keeper Power (KP) and pairing him with strong キャッスルウォール defenders prevents late-chapter shutout losses.

Stage Target Priority Focus
Chapters 3 to 5 Core Catch Hissatsu Save Tension for opposing Zone shots
Chapters 6 to 9 Keeper Power & Physical nodes Equip Catch-boosting gloves and bracelets
Postgame Counter KP or Justice KP passives Maximize KP scaling under pressure

忍原来夏 pairs with 桜咲丈二 on the front line, providing speed on the wing and chain-shot support to overwhelm goalkeepers whose element counters Briar.
