Most quests, collectibles, and trophies remain accessible after clearing the campaign. The primary time-gated cutoff occurs in Chapter 4, where your selection from 15 セレクトキャラ locks the remaining 10 candidates out of your story playthrough until postgame.

Chapter 4 Roster Cutoff

During Chapter 4, 15 applicants try out for 南雲原中学校 and you pick 5 to join immediately. Because the story roster already includes strong forwards and midfielders, picking defenders fills immediate positional gaps, though all 15 characters can be customized later and the unselected 10 become scoutable after Chapter 9.
