The first six days form a shared prologue and investigation phase before choices on 7日目 split into three main heroine paths. Creating anchor saves at the end of 6日目 and before each late-game branch lets you clear every ending and gallery CG with minimal repetition.

Stage 1. Common Phase & Early Branches

Play through the opening six days once, collecting all early 紗枝, 楓玲, and 香淡 events while keeping dedicated saves for early exits.

Stage 2. Main Heroine Routes

Branch from your 7日目 Master Save for each of the three main heroines. Playing 楓玲 first establishes the baseline route before diverging into 命 and 桔梗.

Stage 3. Alternate Endings & CG Cleanup

Use your 11日目 and 12日目 saves to clear the remaining endings and gallery slots documented on Missables & CG.
