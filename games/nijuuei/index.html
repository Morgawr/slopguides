Days 1-6 Fuurei Mikoto Kikyou

二重影

Set in 寛永17年 on the isolated 淡炎島, this historical suspense visual novel follows 二つ影双厳 across twelve days as he investigates a curse of twin culling and two shadows. Because sudden lethal choices, real-time sword duels, and late-game deduction prompts branch across eleven endings, using dedicated anchor saves minimizes repeated reading.

Shared Opening & Route Split Start Days 1-6 →
The first six days form a shared common phase featuring early affection scenes for 紗枝 and an early branch on 5日目 night. Keep a permanent Master Save at the opening of 7日目 to branch cleanly into 楓玲, 命, and 桔梗.

Edition Comparison

Three commercial releases cover the same twelve-day narrative and eleven endings, differing in age rating, adult scenes, and character voice acting.

Title Platform & Release Rating & Voice Notes
二重影 Windows PC (2000) 18+ · Unvoiced Original release by ケロQ including adult scenes and unedited horror depictions.
