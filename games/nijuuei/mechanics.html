Progression relies on four interlocking systems across twelve in-game days, combining standard dialogue choices, location selection, timed sword duels, and a late-game mystery quiz.

Twelve-Day Structure

The story spans from 1日目 to 12日目, divided daily into morning, afternoon, and night phases. The opening six days establish the island setting and early side events, while 7日目 onward shifts into route-determining heroine interactions.

Map Movement & Sweeps

During exploration phases, you select destinations around 淡炎島 and the 阿波路家 estate. Key locations include 自室, 大広間, 廊下, 屋敷前, 蔵の前, 大岩, 海岸, 港, 村, 物見やぐら, 山道, 森林, and 遺跡.

Many time blocks require visiting a specific chain of locations to trigger hidden flags or advance the clock. On 10日目, after the outdoor choice, the game requires sweeping every available map area before resolving at your active heroine's final location (大広間, 屋敷前, or 海岸).

Affection & Route Branching

Three main heroines have dedicated late-game paths and endings: 楓玲, 命, and 桔梗. Choices from 1日目 through 6日目 primarily build 紗枝 affection and unlock early CGs, while 7日目 through 11日目 determine which main heroine route activates.

In general, 楓玲 serves as the baseline chart, while 命 and 桔梗 diverge through specific dialogue replies on 7日目, 9日目, 10日目, and 11日目.

Timed Combat Sequences

Duel scenes present rapid command menus under a strict countdown timer. Failing to pick a response before the timer expires, or selecting an incorrect defensive or offensive technique, results in immediate death.

Night Deduction Sequence

On 11日目 night, 二つ影双厳 organizes every clue uncovered on the island across an eight-part reasoning sequence. Answering every prompt accurately locks in your active heroine's true 12日目 climax.

Providing wrong answers during the deduction quiz diverts the story into a Bad Ending (and unlocks a PC-only 舞 CG). Always keep a save right before the reasoning sequence begins.
