Recommended Heroine Order

Optimal Multi-Playthrough Sequence

Although any heroine can be cleared in any order, エリカ・フォンティーヌ anchors the central narrative and serves as the strongest opening run, while second-loop bonuses make multi-route cleanup significantly faster.

  • Run 1 (Canonical Lead)
    Clear エリカ・フォンティーヌ first while raising グリシーヌ・ブルーメール and コクリコ into second and third place before Episode 9. Buy every shop bromide and complete all five heroine mini-games along the way.
  • Branch Saves from Run 1
    Reload your Episode 9 副隊長 save to finish グリシーヌ・ブルーメール and コクリコ rapidly, or replay from Episode 6 if you want each heroine's unique Episode 6 date and Episode 10 メル and シー dance dialogue.
  • Run 2 (New Game+ / Late Recruits & Ultimate Rank)
    Start a fresh second-loop campaign targeting ロベリア・カルリーニ and 北大路花火, who join in Episodes 4 and 5 and benefit from knowing their timed map events. Use the second-loop battle skip through Episode 6 and max sub-character trust to unlock 黒髪の貴公子.
  • Sub-Character Dance Trio
    On your way through Episodes 1 to 9, maintain high trust with グラン・マ, メル, and シー. Before the Vice-Captain date in Episode 10, you will enter a bonus dance hall scene with them and pick one partner whose farewell confession appears in the ending.

Episode 6 & Episode 9 Save Branching

Key Anchor Points Across the 11 Episodes

  • Episode 6 Apartment Save (Before Kinematron Date Call) lets you choose any of the five heroines for the holiday date and trigger their unique battle dialogue, leaving the remaining four heroines to react during the Episode 6 combat stage.
  • Episode 9 Strategy Room Save (Before Vice-Captain Selection) sits right before グラン・マ offers the top three highest-trust heroines as candidates for 副隊長. Note whether you tell the selected heroine about your eventual return to Tokyo, because Episode 10 checks that flag.
  • Episode 10 Theater Shop Save allows you to collect one Special Bromide from シー, advance to the next save point to write it to the system file, and repeat from the pre-shop save to complete all Special Bromides without grinding hundreds of thousands of casino chips.
  • Create Episode 6 & Episode 9 Branch Anchors on a spare ビジュアルメモリ block.
  • Clear All Five Heroine Endings into the Dreamcast system file.

Second Loop & Omake Progression

Post-Clear Features

  • Clear 巴里の優雅な一日 Quizzes & Unlock 光武ナックル
    After clearing the game once, enter 巴里の優雅な一日 and pass the Beginner Quiz at the Church, Intermediate Quiz at Bleumer Mansion, and Advanced Quiz at the Police Station in order to unlock 光武ナックル at テアトル・シャノワール. On your second campaign playthrough, you gain extra LIPS options, battle-skipping for standard encounters in Episodes 1 to 6, and option menu toggles for trust chimes and page sounds.

Sakura Wars 4 Dreamcast Export

Save Data Merging into the Finale

  • Export Clear Data for サクラ大戦4
    Keep your サクラ大戦3 system and clear save on your ビジュアルメモリ alongside Dreamcast サクラ大戦 and サクラ大戦2 data. Performing セーブデータ合体 at the start of サクラ大戦4 transfers every cleared heroine's status and unlocks full memory gallery CGs and crossover scenes.
