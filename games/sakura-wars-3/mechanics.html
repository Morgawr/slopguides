All LIPS Dialogue Variants

Conversation & Map Interaction Formats

Variant Behavior Practical Handling
通常LIPS Fixed list of choices under a single countdown bar. Read all options cleanly before selecting, as choices remain static until timeout.
タイミングLIPS Choices rewrite or expand once the timer passes the halfway point. Wait past 50 percent whenever a delayed fourth option yields the target trust gain.
ダブルLIPS Outer ring meter chains back-to-back choices such as cooking or bomb disposal. Follow the exact multi-step sequence listed in the walkthrough before the outer meter fills.
クリックLIPS Move the cursor over a character portrait or room object to inspect. Inspect hair, eyes, mouth, or specific objects multiple times to trigger secret responses, while avoiding repeated chest inspections on グリシーヌ・ブルーメール.
高速LIPS Short reflex prompt lasting only a couple of seconds. Keep a thumb ready on A for sudden hazards in Episodes 1, 8, and 10.
アナログLIPS Single choice with a vertical 7-step intensity bar adjusted by the analog stick. Level 7 delivers maximum energy (cheering, slamming), while levels 1 to 3 express quiet restraint or careful peeling.
無回答 Letting the countdown reach zero without pressing A. Triggers unique silence outcomes, including Episode 1 noble restraint, Episode 8 マリア・タチバナ room silence, and Episode 10 Erica's switch.

ARMS Combat & Dreamcast Controls

Dreamcast Battle Pad Layout & AP Economy

  • Analog Stick steers your 光武F freely across the 3D arena. Moving marks segments of the 7-slot ARMSゲージ in red, and walking back toward your starting point refunds them until you execute another command.
  • A Button executes a standard attack on a locked-on target in your front reticle. Pressing A repeatedly chains up to five strikes, with each hit dealing higher damage than the previous hit.
  • B Button primes a 必殺攻撃 or 合体攻撃 when 気合値 reaches 100 (confirm with A), or triggers ため when Spirit is under 100.
  • X Button activates 防御 once per turn, reducing incoming damage until your next turn.
  • Y Button casts 回復 to recover HP and clear status ailments such as AP drain.
  • L & R Triggers rotate the battlefield camera, while Start opens the pause and system menu.
  • VMU Screen displays real-time expected destruction percentage (破壊期待値) whenever your front reticle locks onto an enemy unit.

Tactics, Protect & Trust Modifiers

作戦コマンド

Tactic AP & Movement Effects Disabled Command Best Use Case
風 Increases movement per AP and lowers Charge and Heal AP cost. 防御 unavailable Racing to timed objectives such as Episode 10 bombs and rail switches.
林 Grants access to every command at higher standard AP costs. None Default starting stance before switching to Mountain or Fire.
火 Drops ため to 1 AP while raising 防御 to 4 AP. 回復 unavailable Charging 気合値 on 大神一郎 or sweeping weakened waves.
山 Drops 防御 to 1 AP and 回復 to 3 AP. ため unavailable Boss battles and heavy reinforcements where every pilot guards at turn end.
  • かばう lets 大神一郎 guard one squadmate per turn. Bosses always open Turn 1 with their signature Special Attack, so shielding fragile rear units nullifies early burst damage.
  • Trust Battle Bonus scales each heroine's stats according to her current motivation, adding up to Attack +8, Defense +5, and Movement +2.

Captain Types & Combined Attacks

大神一郎 Progression & Dual-Pilot Specials

  • 隊長タイプ shifts dynamically based on dialogue boldness into 通常型 (high single-target strike), 攻撃型 (multi-enemy strike around Ogami), or 防御型 (heals nearby allies).
  • 黒髪の貴公子 unlocks on a second playthrough or later once all Flower Division members and key Paris sub-characters reach high trust. It grants Attack +10, Defense +10, and the full-map Special 狼虎滅却・震天動地.
  • 合体攻撃 replace normal Specials whenever Ogami stands adjacent to an eligible high-trust heroine and both units hold 100 気合値. Activating from either pilot's turn spends both Spirit bars for massive burst damage or healing.

Mechanics Verification Checklist

  • Practice refunding red ARMSゲージ segments by walking back before attacking.
  • Use 山 Guard and かばう to absorb a boss Turn 1 Special Attack.
  • Trigger a 合体攻撃 with Ogami and a maxed-Spirit heroine standing adjacent.
  • Dial all three Imperial Combat Revue Kinematron numbers (13510) across Episodes 7 to 9 with merged save data.
  • Transfer the 巴里の二人 mini-game onto a Dreamcast ビジュアルメモリ.
  • Unlock 黒髪の貴公子 and 狼虎滅却・震天動地 on a second playthrough.
