Main Story Walkthrough (Episodes 1 to 11)

Episode 1 欧州は花の都

Paris Arrival & Theater Free Roam

  • At the Expo, pass the High-Speed LIPS with 跳んで避ける, then greet エリカ・フォンティーヌ using 君のことが知りたいな and チョンマゲなんてしないよ.
  • Inside テアトル・シャノワール, answer グラン・マ with さすがは巴里の舞台ですね and モギリをしていました, cheer Erica on アナログLIPS at level 6 or 7, and tell Ambassador Sakomizu はい、わかりました.
  • During the 19:00 Theater Free Roam, stop by the Bar for ジャン (じゃあ、), go to 売店 to purchase the Erica Bromide (50F) and pick 売店の仕事はどう?, then visit Erica's Dressing Room (inspect mouth そんなことないよ, outfit 似合ってると思うよ, touch hair, and mouth 日本での仕事を教える).
  • Visit Backstage (司会も重要な…), Secretary Office (どんな仕事…), and Prop Room (いや、気にしてないよ), complete the Kitchen crepe Double LIPS (イチゴ -> チョコレート -> アーモンド -> 生クリーム), and reach the Lobby before 19:30.
  • Next morning, wake Erica gently with Analog LIPS level 4 to 6, answer Glycine with 海軍中尉としての留学です, then run the 12:00 Paris Free Roam in order through Place du Tertre, Market (catch Napoleon and revisit for curtain talk だれにでも), Park (すこぶる良好), Riverside Bridge (俺も好きだよ), Circus (Analog LIPS level 1 or 2 gentle handshake), Restaurant (さすが芸術の都…), Library (本場のフランス料理がいいな, skipping the outside Event Catch), Church twice before 12:40 (優しいんだね), Erica's Room, Apartment, and Flower Shop (案内).
  • At the ball, hit Analog LIPS level 6 or 7 for the charge and issue 巴里華撃団、出撃 in the Strategy Room.
  • Complete Episode 1 Exploration, Erica Bromide purchase, and LIPS scenes.
  • Clear シゾー by destroying the 2 Jump Units on プレリュード while protecting Erica with かばう (pick 貴族を助ける).

Episode 2 かわいい手品師

Portable Kinematron & Circus Investigation

  • Answer わかりました (Erica), 情報収集 (Glycine), and ああ、わかったよ (Mell) or 緊急の用事 (Ci) to receive the 携帯キネマトロン.
  • In the 14:00 Free Roam, visit Dressing Room (情報収集, お客さんの心にも, inspect hair repeatedly), Hangar (すぐにでも), 1F Seating (ああ、いいとも), Restaurant (店主を -> コーヒー -> グリシーヌのところに), and Church for Erica's 連鎖イベント 1. Answer Mell's 14:35 Attic call (いや、手伝うよ), Ogami's Apartment call (エリカくん), Embassy (モギリは), and the 14:50 Police Station call (シャノワールの or 教会の).
  • At the Market and Circus, pick オジサンじゃ, 俺はできるよ, and 兄妹, then use Analog LIPS level 6 or 7 to shield コクリコ.
  • In the 22:30 Evening Free Roam, buy the Glycine Bromide (50F) at 売店, visit the Circus (きっと帰って), Bar (Grand-Mere Chain 1), Stage (Ci Chain 1), and answer the 23:20 Police Station call (はい、引き受けに) and Church (怪人).
  • After the 23:25 Cemetery alert, trigger Analog LIPS level 6 or 7, rescue Coquelicot, oppose Grand-Mere, and invite Coquelicot into battle (choose 敵の砲台をすべて破壊 or すべての宝石を回収するぞ).
  • Complete Episode 2 Free Roam, Glycine Bromide purchase, and 連鎖イベント starts.
  • Defeat ピトン (ベルスーズ) under 山 Guard without ending turns inside warning-beep hazard rings.

Episode 3 麗しの海賊娘

Bleumer Mansion & Chandelier Cleanup

  • Select 思い切ってみんなで遊ぶ, challenge Coquelicot in クレイジー・ラリー (よし、勝負だ), then accept Glycine's Park duel (わかった、受けて立とう) and choose 守り on the mast.
  • During Mansion Free Roam (13:00-14:00), answer Tallebou politely, enter Glycine's Room (phonograph オーケストラ, fireplace weapons twice そのとおり), tell Erica to confess the vase (素直に), find Coquelicot near the chairs, speak with Lola in the Yellow Flower Room, return the plate at the Entrance (はい、), pass the Pink Rose Room chandelier sequence (右に -> コクリコを -> エリカをはげます -> 気合を -> コクリコを), accept one more task at Entrance, and stop Erica in the Kitchen.
  • In the evening, use Analog LIPS level 1 on peeking (level 7 penalizes trust), protect Coquelicot from Count Liche, and reach Analog LIPS level 7 or 1 in the Courtyard for Glycine.
  • In the 17:30-18:00 Free Roam, visit Hanabi's Room (わかった), Glycine's Room (俺でよければ), Theater Front (そうだね), Church for Erica Chain 2 (エリカのことを), Cafe (ごまかす), Bar for Grand-Mere Chain 2 and Erica's prompt (犯人を), 売店 to buy the Coquelicot Bromide (50F) (そうじが), Secretary Office Analog LIPS level 5, and Riverside Bridge (そっとしておく).
  • Identify リッシュ, challenge him to a duel (横によける -> 斬るMAX), and choose either the Crate Key path or Disarming Pad path.
  • Complete Episode 3 Mansion chores, Coquelicot Bromide, and クレイジー・ラリー.
  • Defeat レオン (マルシュ) after shattering crates at range or stepping onto magic pads only when clear of purple hazard state.

Episode 4 泥棒は薔薇の香り

Sante Prison Break & Art Museum Choice

  • In the Strategy Room, pick ロベリア and 自分も (Glycine up).
  • During Paris Free Roam (13:00-14:00), visit Library for Glycine Chain 1 (昔から栄えて), Circus (inspect chair twice 大事に), Market (コクリコの), Church for Erica Chain 3, Shop (遊びに), Cafe after 13:30 for 軌跡の人, and Place du Tertre after 13:30 (Analog LIPS level 3).
  • Inside Sante Prison, insist 何としてでも, use Analog LIPS level 6 or 7 on ロベリア・カルリーニ, pick 自分で助け and 怒らせて.
  • In Prison Free Roam (16:00-17:00), pick 看守に in the corridor, visit Cell 1 after 16:25 (説教している), answer Cell 5 at 16:25 (牢獄に), wait for 扉を引いて in Cell 2, disarm the Cell 4 bomb at 16:35 (ふたを開ける -> 赤のコード -> 強さ5 or ネジをはずす -> 青のコード -> 強さ5), answer 16:40 Warden's Office, and inspect the chair, chain, and under the bed twice in the Underground Cell.
  • Back in HQ, praise Lobelia's dance, pick ルーブル美術館 (Lobelia and Erica up), use Analog LIPS level 6 or 7 in the museum, and accept よし、100年 without haggling.
  • Complete Episode 4 軌跡の人, prison bomb disposal, and Louvre selection.
  • Defeat ナーデル (ノクテュルヌ) after securing artworks (Analog level 3 at Louvre) and guarding its cross-shaped boss range.

Episode 5 黒衣の花嫁

Meeting 北大路花火 & Lockpicking

  • Tell Grand-Mere ちょうど, then run Free Roam 1 (15:00-16:00) through 売店 to buy the Lobelia Bromide (50F) (すこし), Lobelia's Room (inspect bed twice ベッドに横に, under bed twice もしかして, exit with カギ開けを to score 6,600+ in 明日の錠), Shower Room (Analog level 4 or 5), Secretary Office (砂糖じゃ), Place du Tertre, and Flower Shop after 14:30.
  • Follow 北大路花火 quietly from behind on the Bridge (うしろから), balance the 1F Seating Click LIPS across every teammate ending on 席を立つ, and comfort Hanabi at the Cemetery (お墓に花を -> 元気づける).
  • During Free Roam 2 (12:00), visit Library for Glycine Chain 2 and Hanabi inspections (mouth twice 剣術の, eyes twice 君の瞳に), Park (ロベリアを), Police Station (俺のことは), Embassy (やはり), Cemetery (それじゃ、), Hanabi's Room (screen twice ボウズがビョウブに->ジョウズにビョウブの->エをかいた, bamboo twice 俺は毎日にでも), Circus for Coquelicot Chain 1, Lobelia's Room for Lobelia Chain 1, Secretary Office for Mell Chain 1 (wait for きのうはすまなかった), Stage for Ci Chain 2, and Market (Analog LIPS level 3 peeling).
  • At the 13:00 Restaurant lunch choose 本日の, then launch under 巴里華撃団 or 花火くんを.
  • Complete Episode 5 Lobelia Bromide, 明日の錠, and Hanabi's room events.
  • Break the rising-water gate immediately with a Special, rescue Hanabi, and ground コルボー (セレナード) with ranged fire.

Episode 6 巴里の休日

Paris Holiday & Kinematron Date Anchor

  • Answer Grand-Mere and Jean with 休みを, わかりました、, and それは to unlock every squadmate's キネマトロン number, then pick 特に with Glycine.
  • In the 12:00-13:00 Free Roam, visit Bar (wait for ロベリアを捕まえる), Library (wait for イヤリング), Church (そんなこと), Restaurant before 12:30 (加山に), Market (ハムサンド or タマゴサンド), Place du Tertre (Analog level 7, then Market to recover wallet), Park (それぞれの個人を), Circus (加山 -> エリカくんを), Police Station (wait うまく), Hanabi's Room (Hanabi Chain 1), Glycine's Room (Analog level 3), Lobby (follow Ci to 売店 for the Hanabi Bromide (50F)), Secretary Office (かわいい服 and 剣の修行), and end at the elevator with シーの仕事を.
  • Save in Ogami's Apartment before dialing your chosen date on the キネマトロン, enjoy the date, and rally with 巴里華撃団 before launching.
  • Complete Episode 6 Hanabi Bromide, Hanabi Chain 1, and Apartment Date Save.
  • Clear the カルマール ambush by shielding surrounded allies with かばう and clearing heavy units with early Specials.

Episode 7 光は東方より

Flower Expo Guests & Casino Night

  • In the Secretary Office, pick 前回の, ああ、, and wait on Coquelicot's prompt if targeting Erica, Hanabi, or Lobelia.
  • During the 12:00-13:00 Free Roam, visit Place du Tertre first for the culprit clue (required before the 13:00 call), speak to Ci at 売店 (わかった), Secretary Office (俺じゃ), Flower Shop, then return to 売店 and speak with Mell for the Mell & Ci Bromide. Continue to Restaurant (Analog level 3), Riverside Bridge (だれかの), Library (Glycine Chain 3), Circus (Coquelicot Chain 2), Lobelia's Room (Lobelia Chain 2), Hanabi's Room (Hanabi Chain 2), Cafe (はい、), Outside Chattes Noires after 12:30 for 矢の当たる場所, and Ogami's Apartment to dial 13510 if merged data is present.
  • At the 13:00 alert pick 情報を得ている, then at the 帝国華撃団 banquet calm either 待つんだ、すみれ or 待つんだ、花火 and pick 「イチロー」.
  • In the 22:30-23:00 Free Roam, hit Stage for Ci Chain 3, Bar for Grand-Mere Chain 3, Secretary Office for Mell Chain 2, Kitchen (コクリコを), Shop (俺が), Kitchen Hallway (Eating flyer), Restaurant (Dance flyer), Cafe (加山雄一 Swimsuit flyer), and accept the 23:10 Casino invite (わかった, picking Poker for Ci or Blackjack for Mell). Next morning choose ダンスコンテスト (よし、ダンスで) or 大食いコンテスト, and rally with チームワークを.
  • Complete Episode 7 Place du Tertre clue, Mell & Ci Bromide, 矢の当たる場所, and Casino night.
  • Hold your starting perimeter against reinforcements and defeat whichever boss (シゾー flank/Jump Units or ピトン Mountain Heal) you keep for the Paris squad.

Episode 8 飛べない天使

Special Coaches & 光武F2 Debut

  • In the 12:00 Free Roam, visit Embassy (わかりました), Flower Shop (かれんな), Glycine's Room (4-step bluff 晴れました -> ゴホゴホ -> 本当に晴れて -> ゴホゴホ), Secretary Office for Mell Chain 3 (俺も), Riverside Bridge (どうして), Circus for Coquelicot Chain 3, Lobelia's Room for Lobelia Chain 3, Hanabi's Room for Hanabi Chain 3, and Place du Tertre after 12:15 to catch Erica on the High-Speed LIPS (エリカを受け止める).
  • In the Manager's Office, play 修道服と機関銃 with 李紅蘭, then check the hallway coaches (様子を見に行く, staying silent for マリア・タチバナ, picking circus members or animals for 桐島カンナ, and picking 愛情と for Kohran), and choose 俺が様子を in the Dressing Room.
  • In the 22:30 Free Roam, visit Chattes Noires (必ず), Library (よろしく), Church (今は), Bar (たしかに), Park (ああ、), and Riverside Bridge to find Erica. In Ogami's Room select 俺はいいから, Analog LIPS level 7 or 1, cooking Double LIPS 真ん中 -> 下 -> 上 -> 右, and 俺たちは.
  • Complete Episode 8 Final Chain Events, 修道服と機関銃, and Imperial coach check-ins.
  • Upgrade to 光武F2, clear ナーデル, destroy magic circles first in the dark Phase 2 hall, and defeat コルボー.

Episode 9 柵に囲まれし島

Vice-Captain Appointment & Notre-Dame Assault

  • In the 14:00-15:00 Free Roam, speak with Grand-Mere at Chattes Noires (俺たちで), Lobelia at Park (刑務所), Laurent at Circus (わかりました、), Hanabi at Cemetery (花火くんは), Erica at Church (神さまが), Glycine at Restaurant (かけがえのない), Evian at Police Station (警部は), Tallebou at Bleumer Mansion (グリシーヌが), Coquelicot at Market (コクリコなら), and Sakomizu at Embassy (俺たちに).
  • During Strategy Room briefings with 織姫 and レニ・ミルヒシュトラーセ, pick まずは, 織姫くん, and 唇の動きで, then help in the Prop Room (よし、) and Dressing Room (wait for 俺が正しい).
  • Save before speaking with グラン・マ to appoint your 副隊長 from the top three trust candidates, and tell your chosen heroine honestly that you will return to Tokyo.
  • Create Episode 9 Save Anchor and appoint your 副隊長.
  • Shatter barrier fulcrums as the light pulse passes, defeat レオン, and clear カルマール after breaking its legs and arrow pods.

Episode 10 神去りし後

Special Bromide Loop, Dance Hall & Eclair Escape

  • In the 08:30-09:00 Free Roam, visit 売店 first to claim a Special Bromide from シー (なんでも), then hit Park (川の中に入って取る), Place du Tertre (腕を見せるんだ), Circus (コクリコのことを思い出してるよ -> 誕生日おめでとう), Secretary Office (みんな、すごくよろこぶよ), Police Station (警部の努力), Church (俺が), and Attic Erica's Room (let switch timer expire via Time Out, read diary, pick エリカ君ならなれるよ).
  • If Mell, Ci, and Grand-Mere trust is high, choose one dance partner before the Vice-Captain cafe sequence, stall the 22:00-23:00 Lobby Click LIPS until 23:00, and reach Analog LIPS level 7 during the park date.
  • In Combat Phase 1, send two allies with Ogami against カルマール and assign three allies under 風 Tactics to cast 回復 on the four color-shifting time bombs whenever they approach red.
  • In Phases 2 and 3 aboard Eclair Fort, guard the six steam units from カラミテ with 風 movement and fire the main cannon (主砲発射だ) on Mell's High-Speed LIPS.
  • Complete Episode 10 Special Bromide shop pickup, Dance Hall event, and Park date.
  • Disarm Notre-Dame time bombs and clear Eclair Fort Phases 2 and 3 against カラミテ.

Episode 11 都市は生きている

Final Assault & Epilogue

  • In the Triumphal Arch HQ (迫水典通), wait past halfway for 何か方法があるはずだ (Hanabi up), refuse sacrifice orders, and lock Analog LIPS level 6 or 7 on 巴里は燃えているか.
  • In Stages 1-1 to 1-4 inside the Oak Tree, focus on breaking each 樹界門 rather than chasing every monster, shatter both rock walls in Stage 1-3 to rescue trapped squadmates, and descend Stage 1-4 using 風 to reunite the separated pilot while skirting mine hazards.
  • In Stage 2-1 against サリュ (オバド), strike the yellow-backed true unit whenever it splits into eleven copies.
  • In Stages 2-2, 2-3, 3-1, and 3-2 against デルニエ (1,800 HP, zero self-healing), dedicate Erica to グラース・オ・スィエール healing because its end-of-turn strike drops hit units to 1 HP regardless of かばう. Adjust positions as the arena rotates in 3-2 and unleash 合体攻撃 to finish.
  • Save at the final anchor, tour Paris without a timer to bid farewell to teammates and citizens, and meet your Vice-Captain heroine outside Ogami's Apartment.
  • Rescue all trapped teammates in Oak Tree Stages 1-1 to 1-4 and defeat オバド.
  • Defeat デルニエ across all phases and save your Dreamcast Clear Data.
