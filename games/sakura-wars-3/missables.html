Missables & Timed Cutoffs

Paris free-roam segments close permanently on fixed timestamps or story triggers. Track every 連鎖イベント, timed イベントキャッチ, shop bromide window, and route-locking deadline across Episodes 1 to 10.

Shop Bromides & Ep. 10 Special Loop

Per-Episode 売店 Cutoffs (50 Francs Each)

  • Episode 1 (テアトル・シャノワール 19:00) Visit 売店 before 19:30 to purchase エリカ・フォンティーヌ bromide.
  • Episode 2 (Evening 22:30) Return to 売店 before 23:20 to purchase グリシーヌ・ブルーメール bromide.
  • Episode 3 (Evening 18:00) Visit 売店 to purchase コクリコ bromide.
  • Episode 5 (Afternoon 15:00) Visit 売店 before visiting the Flower Shop after 14:30 to purchase ロベリア・カルリーニ bromide.
  • Episode 6 (12:00) Accept シー at the Lobby to visit 売店 and purchase 北大路花火 bromide.
  • Episode 7 (12:00) Speak with シー at 売店, visit the Flower Shop, then return to 売店 and talk to メル for the dual bromide.
  • Episode 10 (08:30) With every regular bromide owned, talk to シー at 売店 to receive one Special Bromide. Save at the next checkpoint and reload your 08:30 save to register all remaining Special Bromides.

Route Lockouts & Timed Story Triggers

Critical Story & Battle Deadlines

  • Episode 8 Imperial Coach Cameos & Ep. 1 Damage Flag During Episode 1 combat, destroying a parked car triggers a bonus Erica LIPS dialogue in Episode 2. In Episode 8, after training with 李紅蘭, choose to check on マリア・タチバナ and 桐島カンナ in the hallway instead of returning straight to work.
  • Episode 9 副隊長 Cutoff & Episode 10 Dance Scene Your target heroine must rank in the top three trust standings before Episode 9's appointment. Raise メル, シー, and グラン・マ trust before Episode 10 to unlock the bonus dance partner choice, and avoid skipping the Tokyo-return disclosure in Episode 9 if you want full Episode 10 trust.
