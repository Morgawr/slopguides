ブロマイド

売店 Lineup & Episode 10 Special Loop

Standard character bromides cost 50 Francs each at 売店, with only one purchase slot per episode. Collect all regular bromides before Episode 10 so シー awards a Special Bromide at the start of Episode 10's 08:30 free roam. Advance to the next save point to write it to the system file, then reload your 08:30 save to register the rest rapidly.

  • エリカ・フォンティーヌ Bromide Episode 1 (売店, 19:00 free roam).
  • グリシーヌ・ブルーメール Bromide Episode 2 (売店, 22:30 evening free roam).
  • コクリコ Bromide Episode 3 (売店, 18:00 free roam).
  • ロベリア・カルリーニ Bromide Episode 5 (売店, 15:00 free roam).
  • 北大路花火 Bromide Episode 6 (売店, 12:00 free roam via Ci's invitation).
  • メル & シー Bromide Episode 7 (Speak with Ci at the shop, complete her Flower Shop event, then speak with Mell at the shop).
  • All Special Bromides (5 Heroines + メル & シー) Registered via Episode 10 shop loop or Casino exchange.

Story Mini-Games

Five Heroine Mini-Games & Casino Tables

  • クレイジー・ラリー (Ep. 3, Circus)
    Hit incoming shuttlecocks using the matching Dreamcast face button as they enter each ring for 100+20n Smash chains per color. Listening to Coquelicot's strike rhythm helps track multi-shuttle volleys around 40+ hits.
  • 軌跡の人 (Ep. 4, Cafe after 13:30)
    Step across every traversable tile on 10 chess-style grids to reach Glycine through checkpoints 1 to 4. Prioritize keeping an unblocked return path around checkpoint 4, and test checkpoint order before yellow melt switches.
  • 明日の錠 (Ep. 5, Exiting Lobelia's Room)
    Guide three progressively larger spheres through the lock maze using D-pad on straight runs and the analog stick on diagonal bends. Hold speed boost on open corridors and collect clock coins.
  • 矢の当たる場所 (Ep. 7, Outside Chattes Noires)
    Pull the analog stick back to draw the bow, aim along the trajectory line while leading moving targets past black steel blockers, and release to clear all four ranks (300 / 400 / 600 / 800 points) into the 10-arrow bonus round.
  • 修道服と機関銃 (Ep. 8, Manager's Office)
    Pre-aim ahead of advancing mechs to trigger chain explosions, cancel reload animations early, hold high-level specials for dense late waves, and keep fire off Erica for the 500-point rescue bonus.
  • Poker & Blackjack (Ep. 7, Casino at 23:10)
    Accept Mell and Ci's late-night invite and win either table game for trust gains, as Ci favors Poker and Mell favors Blackjack. In Poker, bet maximum against Erica to accumulate fast bankroll.

Casino Prizes & Auto-Slot Medal Farm

Dreamcast Medal Farming & Prize Exchange

Build roughly 50,000 starting chips at Poker against エリカ・フォンティーヌ (all-in betting) or Roulette even-money 100-chip bets, then enter the 100-rate Sugoroku Slot machine. Hold down on the analog stick in the upper-left square loop (or unplug the Dreamcast controller while holding Up and reconnect it to lock Down input) to farm chips automatically.

巴里の優雅な一日 & Quizzes

Post-Clear Location Unlocks

Visit the Graveyard, Flower Shop, and Market for mode hints, the Library for archives, the Cafe for BGM, Place du Tertre for past memories, and Ogami's Apartment to save, view dramas, or connect Dreamcast Drama Download discs. Replaying story mini-games requires having played them during the main campaign.

  • Beginner Quiz (Church, 10 Questions) Unlock Intermediate Quiz at Bleumer Mansion. Key answers include Erica dismissed for repeated clumsiness, Red Maiden's Tear jewel, Notre-Dame underground base, Pudding favorite, Maracas dance, Music Box from Ci, Mountain defense focus, 2 flyers, Jean-Paul plush, and Grand-Mere as Countess Lilac.
  • Intermediate Quiz (Bleumer Mansion, 10 Questions) Unlock Advanced Quiz at the Police Station. Key answers include Heal for Notre-Dame bombs, Unnamed Canal, Corbeau's Serenade, Nadel's Nocturne, Fleur de Mal meaning, 6 nozzles on Ogami's F2, Blue button ends Kinematron calls, Red sunglasses on Ciseaux, Mast duel in Ep. 3, and Wind lacks Guard.
  • Advanced Quiz (Police Station, 10 Questions) & 光武ナックル Pass the 10-question Advanced Quiz (4 AP Guard under Fire, 11:05 arrival, 6 Champs-Elysees holes, 4 sign hits, Coquelicot's left-shoulder cat emblem, 11 split forms, 25 fingers on final boss) to unlock the 5-stage 光武ナックル action game at テアトル・シャノワール.
