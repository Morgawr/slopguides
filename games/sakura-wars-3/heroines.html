Paris Flower Division Heroines & Squad

Trust preferences, 光武F and 光武F2 battle setups, Special Attacks, and 合体攻撃 for every pilot in 巴里華撃団・花組.

Squad Mastery: 0/18 (0%)
Squad Roster
Erica Fontaine Glycine Bleumer Coquelicot Lobelia Carlini Hanabi Kitaoji Ichiro Ogami Command & Staff

エリカ・フォンティーヌ

Rear Support & Battlefield Healer

Devout novice nun and dancer at テアトル・シャノワール. Base 光武F stats stand at 120 HP, 12 Attack, 7 Defense, and 12 Movement, pairing long machine-gun range with low armor.

Trust Approach & Tactical Role

  • Preferred LIPS Style rewards warm, reassuring answers, enthusiastic Analog LIPS support (levels 6 to 7 when cheering, level 1 when shaking hands gently or sneaking quietly), and forgiving her clumsy mishaps.
  • Chain Events & Mini-Game run at the Church in Episodes 2A, 3B, and 4A, followed by 修道服と機関銃 in Episode 8.
  • Combat Setup keeps her behind frontline tanks with かばう active on boss openers. Her initial Special サクレ・デ・リュミエール restores 120 HP to allies in a cross shape (requiring at least one injured ally inside the zone), while her upgraded グラース・オ・スィエール expands the area and heals Erica as well. Combined Attack with Ogami is パラディスク・ポルト.
  • Clear エリカ・フォンティーヌ Church Chain Events and Ep. 9 Vice-Captain ending.
  • Upgrade to 光武F2 and cast グラース・オ・スィエール.
  • Execute Combined Attack パラディスク・ポルト with Ogami.

グリシーヌ・ブルーメール

Heavy Vanguard & Multi-Target Breaker

Aristocratic Normandy noblewoman wielding a heavy battleaxe. Base 光武F stats stand at 120 HP, 18 Attack, 9 Defense, and 10 Movement, giving her the squad's highest raw attack power.

Trust Approach & Tactical Role

  • Preferred LIPS Style favors direct, honorable, and disciplined answers that respect French heritage and aristocratic duty while standing firm in battle. During Click LIPS in Episode 2, inspect her hair repeatedly and avoid chest clicks.
  • Chain Events & Mini-Game take place at the Library in Episodes 4A, 5A, and 7A, plus 軌跡の人 in Episode 4.
  • Combat Setup uses trust bonuses (up to Movement +2) or 風 openings to compensate for 10 base Movement, then parks her next to clustered enemies on 山 Guard. Her initial Special グロース・ヴァーグ sweeps wide front rows, and フュリー・ネプチューン expands the wave further. Combined Attack with Ogami is ブルー・ラド・マレ.
  • Clear グリシーヌ・ブルーメール Library Chain Events and Ep. 9 Vice-Captain ending.
  • Upgrade to 光武F2 and cast フュリー・ネプチューン.
  • Execute Combined Attack ブルー・ラド・マレ with Ogami.

コクリコ

High-Mobility Jumper & Mid-Range AoE

Energetic Vietnamese circus illusionist joining in Episode 2. Base 光武F stats stand at 120 HP, 12 Attack, 8 Defense, and 12 Movement with early turn priority and terrain jump mobility.

Trust Approach & Tactical Role

  • Preferred LIPS Style treats her as a capable teammate rather than a child, praises animals and magic, and responds smoothly without startling her during Analog LIPS (level 3 when feeding birds in Episode 4).
  • Chain Events & Mini-Game appear at the Circus in Episodes 5A, 7A, and 8A, plus クレイジー・ラリー in Episode 3.
  • Combat Setup leverages her jump trait to bypass ledges and cars in Episode 2's gem-collection battle and flank elevated targets. Keep 山 Guard or かばう active when diving into crowds. Her initial Special マジーク・ボンボン hits a cat-shaped zone, upgrading in 光武F2 to マジーク・プティ・シャ. Combined Attack with Ogami is ルヴェ・ル・リドー.
  • Clear コクリコ Circus Chain Events and Ep. 9 Vice-Captain ending.
  • Upgrade to 光武F2 and cast マジーク・プティ・シャ.
  • Execute Combined Attack ルヴェ・ル・リドー with Ogami.

ロベリア・カルリーニ

High-Mobility Melee Striker & Radial Buster

Romanian master thief recruited from Sante Prison in Episode 4. Her 光武F delivers attack power third only to Glycine and Ogami alongside sturdy armor and strong movement.

Trust Approach & Tactical Role

  • Preferred LIPS Style responds to bold, unfiltered honesty and high-intensity Analog LIPS (levels 6 to 7 in Episode 4 prison scenes), while refusing overly timid moralizing or cheap flattery.
  • Chain Events & Mini-Game trigger inside Lobelia's Room in Episodes 5A, 7A, and 8A, alongside 明日の錠 in Episode 5.
  • Combat Setup places her right inside dense enemy formations because her initial Special フィアンマ・ウンギア strikes behind and to every side of her mech. Her 光武F2 Special デモン・ファルチェ covers an even wider radius. Combined Attack with Ogami is コンブスティオーネ.
  • Clear ロベリア・カルリーニ Room Chain Events and Ep. 9 Vice-Captain ending.
  • Upgrade to 光武F2 and cast デモン・ファルチェ.
  • Execute Combined Attack コンブスティオーネ with Ogami.

北大路花火

Long-Range Crossbow Sniper

Soft-spoken Japanese-French playwright joining in Episode 5. Her crossbow-armed unit trades close-quarters defense and speed for long-distance single-target suppression.

Trust Approach & Tactical Role

  • Preferred LIPS Style values gentle, patient encouragement, polite phrasing, and sharing quiet cultural moments rather than abrupt teasing.
  • Chain Events & Mini-Game take place in Hanabi's Room in Episodes 6A, 7A, and 8A, plus 矢の当たる場所 outside the theater in Episode 7.
  • Combat Setup stations her behind Glycine and Ogami to snipe aerial units (crucial against Episode 5's flying boss) and distant magic circles. Her initial Special 一の舞 金枝玉葉 projects a long fan zone, upgrading to 二の舞 落花啼鳥. Combined Attack with Ogami is 落花流水.
  • Clear 北大路花火 Room Chain Events and Ep. 9 Vice-Captain ending.
  • Upgrade to 光武F2 and cast 二の舞 落花啼鳥.
  • Execute Combined Attack 落花流水 with Ogami.

大神一郎

Flower Division Captain & Frontline Duelist

Dual-sword captain transferred from Tokyo. Base 光武F stats stand at 120 HP, 17 Attack, 10 Defense, and 13 Movement, making him the fastest heavy attacker on the team.

Captain Ranks & Tactical Usage

  • Role in ARMS pairs frontline burst damage with かばう coverage for Erica, Coquelicot, or Hanabi. Stand adjacent to a maxed-Spirit heroine whenever a boss opens an armor phase to trigger 合体攻撃.
  • Captain Type Tuning determines his Special Attack shape across 通常型 (狼虎滅却・刀光剣影), 攻撃型, and 防御型.
  • Second-Playthrough Ultimate unlocks 黒髪の貴公子 (Attack +10, Defense +10, and full-map 狼虎滅却・震天動地) once squadmates and Paris sub-characters reach high trust.
  • Unlock 大神一郎 光武F2 in Episode 8.
  • Max out Paris sub-character trust (グラン・マ, メル, シー, 迫水典通, ジャン, エビヤン, レノ, タレブー).
  • Obtain 黒髪の貴公子 and unleash 狼虎滅却・震天動地.
