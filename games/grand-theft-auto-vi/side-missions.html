Strangers & Freaks, Side Missions & Activities

Side missions appear on the Leonida map as black icons with pink text or purple markers. Completing 100 Optional Tasks is required for 100% completion, while finishing 8 qualifying stranger strands unlocks the Strangers & Freaks Silver trophy. Several strands wait for an in-game phone call, text, or chapter change before their next stage spawns.

Strangers & Freaks Character Strands

0/14 (0%)

Ordered chronologically by earliest chapter unlock. Multiple strands award individual Bronze trophies alongside contributing toward optional task completion.

1. Shirley (Chapter 1, Missable)

Location & Unlock: Key Lento in Mariana County, Jason's neighbor during early Chapter 1.

Tasks: Get Shirley's Rum and Steal the Scooter. Both tasks disappear permanently once Chapter 1 transitions into Chapter 2. Neither task is required for trophies or 100% completion.

2. Kenton Rees (Chapter 1 to Chapter 3)

Location & Unlock: Key Lento starting in Chapter 1, resuming automatically via text message in Chapters 2 and 3.

Tasks & Rewards: Maritime salvage and shipwreck diving missions (such as Maritime Disputes) utilizing an underwater metal detector. Unlocks the Bronze trophy This Was No Boat Accident upon finishing his final mission and connects directly to Kenton Sets (5 of 7 required for 100%) and the 33 Underwater Discoveries. Safe to complete post-game.

3. Kirk (Chapters 1 to 2)

Location & Unlock: Wetlands and Grassrivers perimeter starting in late Chapter 1 into Chapter 2.

Tasks: Cryptid investigation strand tracking down the Skunk Ape (Mr. Mangrove). Counts as a qualifying strand toward the Strangers & Freaks Silver trophy.

4. Cal Hampton Fishing Challenge (Chapter 1 Onward)

Location & Unlock: Becomes available after fishing mechanics are introduced in Recalibration during Chapter 1.

Tasks & Rewards: Compete against Cal in the Catch a Bigger Fish challenge out on the water. Defeating him unlocks the Bronze trophy Baited My Tackle.

5. Moses Vehicle Exports (Chapter 2)

Location & Unlock: Opens after the Chapter 2 main mission Moses.

Tasks & Rewards: Steal and deliver requested vehicle models on order for $1,000 to $3,000 per car. Counts toward optional tasks, vehicle fencing progress, and 100% completion (does not count as one of the 8 Strangers & Freaks trophy strands).

6. Bret Vogel & Target Decks (Chapter 2, Jason)

Location & Unlock: Accessible as Jason after Coffee Break in Chapter 2 via the intro mission Veteran Hunted.

Tasks & Rewards: Grants the unique Killtelligence SM-69 hunting rifle (reclaimable at any Ammu-Nation locker) and opens all 15 Target Deck hunting commissions tracked under Progress > Contacts > Bret Vogel. Clean heart shots or headshots preserve pristine carcasses for higher payouts. Hunting a unique target animal unlocks Fair Game, and clearing all 15 Target Decks is mandatory for 100% completion.

7. Billy (Chapter 2)

Location & Unlock: Thrillbilly Mud Club and off-road circuits starting in Chapter 2.

Tasks & Rewards: Mud-racing and off-road stunts strand. Completing Billy's final mission unlocks the Bronze trophy The Lone Rider.

8. Kyle (Chapter 2)

Location & Unlock: McKittrick Farm in rural Leonida starting in Chapter 2.

Tasks: Crop-spraying and agricultural flying errands. Counts toward the 8-strand Strangers & Freaks Silver trophy.

9. Yago Street Races (Chapter 3, Timed Event Alert)

Location & Unlock: Little Cuba in Vice City during Chapter 3.

Tasks: Underground street-racing strand including Join a Street Race, Bumper to Bumper, and Race Against Yago. Race Against Yago carries a time-sensitive hourglass icon and expires if you advance too far in the story, so complete it promptly when it appears.

10. Andy & Cellerica (Chapter 3, Lucia)

Location & Unlock: Vice City starting in Chapter 3, played as Lucia.

Tasks & Rewards: Multi-stage initiation storyline with the Cellerica group. Finishing Andy's final mission awards the Bronze trophy Road to Rehabilitation (tracked separately from the 8-strand Strangers & Freaks counter).

11. Tyrone & Kitty Litter Labs (Chapter 3)

Location & Unlock: Vice City after meeting Tyrone during Chapter 3.

Tasks & Rewards: Follow-up missions tied to Kitty Litter Labs. Finishing Tyrone's final mission unlocks the Bronze trophy Spin On This!

12. Randy's Wish List (Chapter 3)

Location & Unlock: Unlocks after Chapter 3 main mission Clonin' Keys, Makin' GS.

Tasks & Rewards: Track down and steal high-end sports cars across Leonida using the Key Cloner. Finishing Randy's final mission awards the Bronze trophy Supply & Demand and contributes significantly toward the $200,000 Vehicles Fenced requirement.

13. Yasmani & Bank Vault Tips (Chapters 3 to 4)

Location & Unlock: Vice City fence contact in Chapter 3, expanding after VCIA Takedown in Chapter 4 with the optional mission That's Where The Money Is... at Cafe Orquidea.

Tasks & Rewards: Launders marked bills once you own the Dye Pack Jammer and sells $10,000 security van tips pointing to the 8 repeatable Large Bank Vaults (walkthrough).

14. Dunk (Chapter 3)

Location & Unlock: Vice City starting in Chapter 3.

Tasks: Optional character strand contributing to Vice City side tasks and Stranger completion milestones.

All 9 Activity Types (Master of None Trophy)

0/9 (0%)

Trying each of the nine open-world activity types at least once unlocks the Bronze trophy Master of None. Bringing your partner along when prompted also raises the shared relationship meter.

  • 1. Fishing

    Cast from boats or piers around the Keys and Leonida waterways. Also builds Focus stat.

  • 2. Hunting

    Hunt wildlife across Grassrivers and Mount Kalaga or complete any of Bret's Target Decks.

  • 3. Golf & Mini Golf

    Play a round at Leonida's golf or mini golf courses (2 Mini Golf locations are required for 100%).

  • 4. Shooting Ranges

    Available at Griffin Shooting Range and Rodgers Ranch (all 42 Gold Medals required for 100% and The Shootist trophy).

  • 5. Pool

    Challenge local bar opponents across Leonida (defeat 5 Pool opponents for 100%).

  • 6. Basketball

    Shoot hoops at neighborhood courts across Vice City and Leonida (score at 90 hoops for 100%).

  • 7. Video Games & Arcades

    Play any of the four in-game video games on your safehouse console or open-world arcade cabinets.

  • 8. Races & Top Fun Drone Trials

    Enter street races, off-road circuits (Gellhorn Raceway, Thrillbilly Mud Club, Boone Speedway), or Top Fun Drone Time Trials.

  • 9. Stunts

    Land any vehicle stunt jump (250 required for Cunning Stunts trophy and 300 required for 100% completion).

All 4 Playable Video Games (Stick Fiddler Trophy)

0/4 (0%)

Playing all four video games is mandatory for 100% completion, while beating any single arcade game unlocks the Bronze trophy Stick Fiddler. Three are playable on your home TV console and the fourth sits strictly on arcade cabinets out in the open world.

  • 1. Tenebrae Lux

    Playable from your safehouse home TV console.

  • 2. Street Trash III (Dezombifier)

    Playable from your safehouse home TV console.

  • 3. Time Jackers: Rewind

    Playable from your safehouse home TV console.

  • 4. Bang For Your Buck

    Located exclusively on physical arcade cabinets in the open world rather than on the home console.
