Missables, Permanent Cutoffs & Timed Content

Every requirement on the in-game 100% completion checklist remains available in post-game free roam, yet several chapter-locked events, one potentially missable trophy, and two permanent save-file locks can still catch players off guard. Read this list before completing Chapter 1 and keep rotating manual saves across the 15 save slots.

Trophy, Cutoff & Permanent Lockout Checks

1. Chapter 1 Balcony Meditation (Positive Mental Attitude Trophy)

Cutoff Window: Chapter 1, on the morning immediately following the main mission Down to Earth, before leaving Jason's Key Lento house permanently at the end of Chapter 1.

What to Do: After Down to Earth, Lucia mentions meditating and walks out onto the balcony of Jason's house on Peach Palm Dr. Follow her outside, aim/target her with L2 (PS5) or LT (Xbox), and select Accept when the prompt appears.

Why It Matters: This scripted event registers the first of 10 meditation spots (18 total character sessions). Skipping it before transitioning to Chapter 2 permanently locks out Jason's Key Lento house event and can block the Bronze trophy Positive Mental Attitude. The 100% checklist only asks for 15 of 18 sessions, so 100% completion remains unaffected.

2. Permanent Irredeemable Criminal Profile Lock

Cutoff Window: Active throughout the entire game, tracked independently for Jason and Lucia.

What to Do: Stop escalating indiscriminate civilian or police massacres if your Criminal Profile reaches the Psycho tier and triggers on-screen warnings.

Why It Matters: Pushing past Psycho fractures your Criminal Profile icon permanently into the Irredeemable tier for the rest of that playthrough. Irredeemable status cannot be reversed through good behavior and causes law enforcement to escalate to shoot-on-sight responses much faster. See Criminal Profile.

3. Shirley's Chapter 1 Errands (Get Shirley's Rum & Steal the Scooter)

Cutoff Window: Chapter 1 in Key Lento, prior to the late-chapter transition around Tourist Trap and Discharge Time.

What to Do: Visit Jason's neighbor Shirley in Key Lento and finish both Get Shirley's Rum and Steal the Scooter before closing out Chapter 1.

Why It Matters: Both errands disappear permanently once you leave Chapter 1. Neither task is required for the 100% checklist or any trophy, though completing them adds early optional task credit and unique Chapter 1 dialogue.

4. Chapter 1 House Prep Before Coming Home

Cutoff Window: Chapter 1, before driving to pick up Lucia in Coming Home.

What to Do: Complete Jason's optional preparation prompts around Key Lento (fixing up the house, picking up groceries, and buying flowers) prior to triggering Coming Home.

Why It Matters: These optional prompts expire once Lucia returns. Finishing them provides an immediate relationship boost and early scene variations.

5. Race Against Yago & Hourglass-Marked Side Missions

Cutoff Window: Chapter 3 (for Yago in Little Cuba) and any chapter where a map marker displays an hourglass icon.

What to Do: Whenever a black-and-pink or purple side mission icon appears with an hourglass indicator, finish it before clearing additional pink main story missions.

Why It Matters: Hourglass missions expire if the story advances past their active window. Race Against Yago in Chapter 3 is the most commonly missed example. Because the game provides a wide surplus of optional tasks, missing an hourglass event still leaves 100% completion and trophies attainable.

6. BuckMe Family Savings One-Way Deposit Lock

Cutoff Window: Anytime after unlocking the BuckMe app and the optional Save for the Future task.

What to Do: Transfer only surplus cash into BuckMe's Family Savings account while keeping operational spending money inside your standard BuckMe Checking Account.

Why It Matters: Funds placed into Family Savings are permanently protected from death penalties and unlock account perks, yet money deposited there cannot be withdrawn back into spendable cash.

7. No Mission Replay Menu & Cheat Trophy Lockout

Cutoff Window: Entire campaign and post-game.

What to Do: Keep clean manual saves at the start of Chapters 1 through 5 across the 15 available manual slots. Leave all post-game cheats disabled on your main trophy save file.

Why It Matters: Completed story missions cannot be replayed from the pause menu. In addition, activating any cheat disables saving and blocks trophies for the active session.

Confirmed Safe in Post-Game Free Roam

Early launch-day discussions occasionally mislabel certain characters or checklists as missable. Verified launch testing confirms that the following systems remain permanently accessible after finishing Chapter 5.

Verified Non-Missable Content
  • All 35 Items on the 100% Completion Checklist: Every Gang Operation, Bank Job, Vault, Payday Loan Job, Smash 'n' Grab, Stick Up, Underwater Discovery, Shooting Range, Drone Time Trial, Race Challenge, Bret's Target Deck, and collectible threshold can be completed after Chapter 5.
  • Kenton Rees (Underwater Treasure Strand): Kenton temporarily disappears from Key Lento after Chapter 1, then automatically texts you during Chapters 2 and 3 to resume his strand. He is safe to finish later.
  • Strangers & Freaks Silver Trophy (8 Strands): Qualifying character strands remain open in free roam after the finale.
  • Nine Open-World Meditation Spots & Ascent Mountain: All nine map-marked meditation sites remain open permanently in free roam for both Jason and Lucia. Only the Chapter 1 balcony interaction is chapter-locked.
