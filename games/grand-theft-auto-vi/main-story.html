Main Story Missions (Prologue & Chapters 1 to 5)

The core campaign spans 91 main story missions across a Prologue and five chapters (taking roughly 60 to 80 hours). Some community walkthroughs split multi-stage objectives and inline optional jobs into up to 148 entries, while this checklist tracks the 91 core story missions alongside inline links to optional side missions as soon as they unlock. Mission gold medals and mission replay are absent, so rotate manual saves at every chapter boundary.

Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise

0/22 (0%)

Opening arc set across Key Lento and the Leonida Keys. Watch closely for Shirley's missable errands, the optional house prep before Coming Home, and the missable balcony meditation immediately following Down to Earth.

  • 1. Prologue

    Introductory sequence featuring Lucia inside the correctional facility before the transition to Jason in the Leonida Keys.

  • 2. Gone Fishing

    Opening Keys operation assisting Brian Heder's local shipment. Unlocks the Just Like Paradise Bronze trophy.

  • 3. Gear Up

    Pick up required equipment and longarms from Chuck's Tackle & Shot in Key Lento. Purchase a pistol suppressor here as soon as free roam opens.

  • 4. Just a Scratch

    Repair Jason's Ganado at Joey's mod shop. Introduces vehicle customization and repair basics.

  • 5. Lab Fees

    Collect overdue rent from Moonstone Boutique. Verified keypad codes during early Key Lento lockups are 0420 (Moonstone Boutique lab) and 7700 (Brian's lockup).

  • 6. Unwanted Attention

    Head out to Hamlet alongside Cal Hampton. Side strands for Shirley (missable in Chapter 1), Kirk, and Kenton become accessible during Chapter 1 free roam.

  • 7. Pipework

    Collect overdue rent from Mandy for Brian.

  • 8. Hey Arnold!

    Meet Brian and Cal to track down a local broker.

  • 9. Island Dreams

    Diving and underwater pickup mission introducing underwater swimming controls and coastal salvage.

  • 10. Feng Shui

    Assist Cal with moving furniture. Finish optional Key Lento house prep errands (groceries, flowers, tidying) before starting the next mission for an early relationship boost.

  • 11. Coming Home

    Pick up Lucia upon her release and return to Key Lento.

  • 12. Buck Me

    Sets up the BuckMe phone banking app and ATM deposit network. Start depositing physical pocket cash at ATMs immediately after this mission.

  • 13. Clean Up After Donnie

    Retrieve a dropped package for Donnie. Create a manual safety save right before starting Down to Earth.

  • 14. Down to Earth

    Joint smuggling run with Jason and Lucia. Missable alert: On the morning after this mission, follow Lucia onto the balcony of Jason's house, target her with L2 / LT, and select Accept to register the Chapter 1 meditation spot (details).

  • 15. Clear Skies

    Visit a local biker camp alongside Donnie.

  • 16. Day Release

    Night outing across the Keys with Mattia, Jason, and Lucia.

  • 17. Swamped

    Meet with the Kingfishers alongside Brian and Lori. Optional task Donnie's Lost Drugs also unlocks in this stretch for extra cash.

  • 18. Don Gone

    Mandatory follow-up assignment from Brian triggered after Swamped.

  • 19. Recalibration

    Pick up a six-pack of beer for Cal, head to his Key Lento dock, and go fishing. Introduces open-world fishing mechanics and connects to Cal's fishing challenge. Make sure Shirley's errands are done before continuing.

  • 20. Tourist Trap

    Late Chapter 1 operation in the Keys.

  • 21. Home Sweet Home

    Penultimate Chapter 1 sequence leading toward the chapter exit.

  • 22. Discharge Time

    Concludes Chapter 1 and unlocks the Paradise Lost Bronze trophy.

Chapter 2: Paradise Lost

0/11 (0%)

Create a fresh manual save at the start of Chapter 2. This shorter chapter opens free-roam switching between Jason and Lucia during Coffee Break and unlocks major mainland side strands.

  • 23. What's in the Bag?

    Chapter 2 opening mission establishing the duo's mainland foothold.

  • 24. Ambulance Chaser

    Early Chapter 2 mainland job.

  • 25. Just Moved In

    Sets up the couple's new operating base.

  • 26. Girls' Trip

    Lucia-focused Chapter 2 mission.

  • 27. Moses

    Introduces Moses and opens Moses's vehicle export tasks ($1,000 to $3,000 per vehicle).

  • 28. Daily Driver

    Vehicle acquisition job expanding garage and driving logistics.

  • 29. Slam Dunk

    Mid-chapter job preparing for larger partnership operations.

  • 30. Breakfast Run

    Partner mission unlocking the Trust Bronze trophy upon completion.

  • 31. Coffee Break

    Unlocks free-roam character switching (hold D-Pad Right and press R1 / RB). Opens Bret Vogel (Jason), Billy, Kyle, and dual-protagonist meditation sessions.

  • 32. Afternoon Delight

    Late Chapter 2 mission. Optional Chapter 2 jobs Thrillbillies and Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab are also available in this window.

  • 33. Checking Out

    Concludes Chapter 2 and unlocks the T**s Ass Drug Money Bronze trophy.

Chapter 3: Tits Ass Drug Money

0/26 (0%)

The longest chapter in the campaign at 26 missions, shifting operations into Vice City. Save in a fresh slot upon arriving and watch for Yago's hourglass-marked race mission in Little Cuba alongside the Dye Pack Jammer and Key Cloner unlocks.

  • 34. Welcome to Vice City

    Arrive in Vice City, connect with Dre'Quan at Jack of Hearts, meet Boobie Ike, and unlock the Bayside Apartment. Chapter 3 strands for Yago, Andy, Yasmani, and Dunk begin appearing across Vice City.

  • 35. Dealer's Choice

    Jason's Vice City favor for Boobie Ike.

  • 36. Meet the Heders

    Story follow-up tied to Brian Heder's network.

  • 37. Dubious Provenance

    Early Vice City procurement assignment.

  • 38. Mami Knows Best

    Lucia-exclusive Vice City mission.

  • 39. South Leonida Real Estate

    Vice City property and commercial expansion job.

  • 40. Tyrone

    Introduces Tyrone and connects to Tyrone's Kitty Litter Labs strand.

  • 41. Here, Kitty Kitty

    Follow-up Vice City operation connected to Tyrone.

  • 42. Car Services

    Vehicle logistics mission inside Vice City.

  • 43. Top Billing

    Vice City entertainment and music-scene job.

  • 44. Boobie Time

    Assignment for Boobie Ike.

  • 45. First Impressions

    Introductory job working with Raul Bautista's crew.

  • 46. Caught Slipping

    Mid-chapter Vice City complication.

  • 47. Crosstown Traffic

    Crew preparation job across Vice City.

  • 48. Credit Check

    Meet Raul's crew and unlock the Dye Pack Jammer, required for fencing marked bank bills.

  • 49. Clonin' Keys, Makin' GS

    Steal the Zorrusso for Randy using the Key Cloner. Unlocks Randy's Wish List high-end car theft strand.

  • 50. Sinfrontera National Bank

    Major Vice City banking operation.

  • 51. Security Expert

    Heist security setup mission.

  • 52. Ride or Die

    Joint mission for Jason and Lucia.

  • 53. Company Property

    Asset recovery setup leading into the late Chapter 3 arc.

  • 54. The Missing Link

    Investigation and setup step ahead of the chapter climax.

  • 55. Safe Word

    Late Chapter 3 Vice City mission.

  • 56. Cooties

    Pre-climax mission in Chapter 3.

  • 57. Bricked Up

    Setup job immediately preceding the final pair of Chapter 3 missions.

  • 58. Hidden Charges

    Final setup prior to the Chapter 3 heist. Restock trunk body armor and rifle ammo before advancing.

  • 59. The Cartel Heist

    Chapter 3 finale. Unlocks the Break the Bank and Vice City, Baby! Bronze trophies.

Chapter 5: Top of the World, Ma

0/7 (0%)

Seven-mission finale arc. Make a clean manual save before starting Bird in the Hand and stock body armor and preferred longarms in your trunk. Post-game free roam returns automatically after credits.

  • 85. Bird in the Hand
  • 86. Taking Liberties
  • 87. VC Baby
  • 88. Doggie Bag
  • 89. Operation Savage Glory

    Unlocks the Snow Storm Bronze trophy upon completion.

  • 90. Protective Custody

    Penultimate mission leading straight into the final sequence.

  • 91. The End of the Road

    Campaign finale. Unlocks the Gold trophy You Gotta Know When to Get Off, reveals the in-game 100% Completion tracker under Progress > Stats, and unlocks the post-game Cheat menu.
