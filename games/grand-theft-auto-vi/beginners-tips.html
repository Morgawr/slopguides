Beginner Tips & Early Habits

Leonida penalizes reckless spending, careless gunfights, and neglected save management much harder than past entries. Adopting these twelve operational habits in Chapter 1 saves hours during post-game 100% cleanup and prevents unnecessary cash loss or permanent Criminal Profile penalties.

0/12 beginner habits applied (0%)
  • 1. Keep Permanent Chapter Saves Across the 15 Manual Slots

    Mission replay is absent from the pause menu. Save to a fresh dedicated slot at the beginning and end of each chapter, and keep one untouched slot right before completing Down to Earth in Chapter 1 so you can revisit the missable balcony meditation if needed.

  • 2. Stash Loose Cash Early and Use BuckMe ATMs Regularly

    Dying or getting busted wipes out physical pocket cash. Before unlocking the BuckMe banking app during Buck Me in Chapter 1, stash spare cash under the floorboards in Jason's bedroom. Once BuckMe unlocks, open the ATM Finder on your phone and deposit cash into your Checking Account after every major payout.

  • 3. Equip a Suppressor at Ammu-Nation in Key Lento

    Purchase a suppressor for your primary sidearm as soon as free roam opens in Key Lento. Silent takedowns prevent witnesses and security alarms from cascading into multi-star chases and help maintain a Professional Criminal Profile.

  • 4. Stock Extra Armor, Weapons, and Outfits in Your Personal Trunk

    Because on-person weapon capacity is restricted (typically two sidearms and two long guns), treat your personal vehicle trunk as a mobile armory and wardrobe. Buy body armor directly into trunk storage at Ammu-Nation and store spare outfits so you can swap clothes quickly to defeat police suspect descriptions.

  • 5. Scan Every Unfamiliar Vehicle with the WAiNK App

    100% completion requires cataloging 200 out of 277 base vehicle models (cars, trucks, boats, and aircraft) plus scanning 100 WAiNK landmark locations. Scanning new vehicles organically whenever you pass parking lots, docks, or marinas eliminates a multi-hour post-game grind. Scanning also reveals security alarms, trackers, and value before you steal a car.

  • 6. Scope Wildlife with Binoculars Instead of Hunting Every Species

    100% completion requires studying 100 out of 255 animal species. Pull out your binoculars and study birds, dogs, marine life, swamp reptiles, and forest fauna whenever you travel through Grassrivers, the Keys, or Mount Kalaga. Killing or skinning animals is unnecessary for this requirement.

  • 7. Prioritize Hourglass Side Markers Before Advancing Chapters

    Pink icons with black letters advance the mandatory main story. Black icons with pink letters and purple icons mark optional side content. Any marker showing an hourglass symbol is time-sensitive and expires if you advance too far in the campaign.

  • 8. Hold Off on Heavy Money Grinds Until Mid-to-Late Game

    Early-game finances fluctuate heavily across Chapters 1 and 2. Focus on story progression, unlocking Moses in Chapter 2, unlocking the Dye Pack Jammer in Chapter 3, and reaching repeatable Bank Vaults in Chapter 4 (which pay $65,000 to $205,000 per five-minute run). See Money & Cheats.

  • 9. Watch Your Criminal Profile and Color-Coded Hit Markers

    White hit markers indicate standard hits, yellow indicates non-lethal incapacitation, and red indicates fatal kills. Shooting civilians or fleeing witnesses pushes your profile toward Violent, Psycho, and eventually a permanent Irredeemable lock where police shoot on sight. See Criminal Profile Mechanics.

  • 10. Complete Optional House Prep Before Coming Home

    Before picking up Lucia during Coming Home in Chapter 1, finish Jason's optional preparation errands in Key Lento (such as tidying the house, buying groceries, and picking up flowers). These small actions immediately raise Jason and Lucia's shared relationship level.

  • 11. Train Both Protagonists at Gyms and Maintain Sleep

    Jason and Lucia track Strength (max health), Cardio (max stamina), and Focus (slow-motion duration) independently through the phone Fitness App. Working out together boosts stats and relationship level at the same time. Sleeping regularly prevents fatigue penalties that slow attribute regeneration.

  • 12. Check Settings > Gameplay for Combat and Vehicle Toggles

    Difficulty presets are absent, so open Settings and then Gameplay if combat or high-speed pursuits feel punishing. Toggles such as Accessible Combat and Reduced Vehicle Damage soften damage scaling without disabling trophies or blocking 100% completion.
