Money-Making Progression & Economy Milestones

0/10 (0%)

Follow these staged economy milestones alongside the story so your cash flow scales naturally while clearing all four 100% fencing requirements at the same time.

  • 1. Early Stashes & BuckMe Setup (Chapter 1)

    Grab environmental hidden stashes (watch wildlife cues like parrots leading to coastal drops), clear optional Donnie's Lost Drugs, and finish Buck Me so you can bank cash safely at ATMs instead of losing pocket cash on death.

  • 2. Moses Vehicle Exports (Chapter 2: $1,000 to $3,000 per Car)

    Unlocked after the Chapter 2 mission Moses. Deliver requested civilian vehicles on order for reliable early income and vehicle fencing progress.

  • 3. Smash 'n' Grab Jewelry Stores ($10,000 to $25,000 per Run)

    Step up from convenience store stick-ups to jewelry and pawn shops across Leonida. Bring jewelry directly to a fence to build toward the $100,000 Jewelry Fenced requirement.

  • 4. Unlock the Dye Pack Jammer (Chapter 3: Credit Check)

    Complete Credit Check with Raul's crew in Chapter 3. You must own the Dye Pack Jammer before Yasmani can clean marked bank bills.

  • 5. Randy's Wish List High-End Car Thefts (Chapter 3)

    Unlocked after Clonin' Keys, Makin' GS. Use the Key Cloner to steal target luxury sports cars cleanly for higher payouts and rapid progress toward $200,000 Vehicles Fenced.

  • 6. Complete "That's Where The Money Is..." (Chapter 4)

    Unlocks after VCIA Takedown via Yasmani at Cafe Orquidea in Ocean Beach. Pays roughly $80,000 on the first run and unlocks repeatable $10,000 security van tips for Large Bank Vaults.

  • 7. Rob All 8 Large Bank Vaults ($65,000 to $205,000 per Vault)

    Buy security van tips from Yasmani and loot the Vault, Cash Drawer, and Safe in every large bank. Robbing 5 vaults unlocks Maximum Withdrawal, and robbing all 8 completes the 100% Vaults Hit requirement.

  • 8. Launder $250,000 in Marked Bills via Yasmani

    Bring marked bank bills from Bank Vaults straight to Yasmani in Vice City to clear the Marked Bills Fenced 100% milestone.

  • 9. Fence $100,000 in Narcotics & $100,000 in Jewelry

    Combine Gang Operation stashes, Cocaine Flamingo drops, and Smash 'n' Grab runs to finish both fencing totals.

  • 10. High-Tier Gang Operations & Circuit Races

    First-place finishes at Thrillbilly Mud Club, Gellhorn Raceway, and Boone Speedway pay solid mid-to-late cash while also advancing the Repair Vehicle cheat unlock requirement.

Bank Vault Walkthrough ("That's Where The Money Is...")

This optional Chapter 4 mission is the gateway to the fastest repeatable cash farm in Leonida. Make sure you already completed Credit Check in Chapter 3 for the Dye Pack Jammer and VCIA Takedown in Chapter 4 before heading to Ocean Beach.

Step-by-Step Vault Execution
  • 1. Wait at Cafe Orquidea: Travel to Cafe Orquidea in Ocean Beach, Vice Beach, and sit at the marked window table to trigger the setup.
  • 2. Tail the Armored Security Van: Follow the passing armored truck at a moderate distance so you keep visual contact without spooking the driver.
  • 3. Follow the Guard Inside: Park near the target bank once the van stops and slip inside behind the cash-carrying security guard.
  • 4. Put on Disguise and Breach the Vault: Equip your disguise, aim at staff to begin the robbery, head to the back vault doorway, plant explosives to blow the door, and clear any guards inside.
  • 5. Clear All Three Loot Points: Every large bank holds a Vault, a Cash Drawer, and a Safe. Loot all three before exiting so the job registers fully completed.
  • 6. Escape the 4-Star Wanted Level: Breaching a vault immediately triggers a 4-star chase. Break out of the search zone or drive straight into a Pay 'n' Spray to clear vehicle heat, then deliver the marked bills to Yasmani.

How Post-Game Cheat Codes Work

Cheat codes work strictly as a post-game sandbox system rather than an early campaign shortcut.

  • Campaign Completion Required: The Cheat menu under the Progress tab unlocks only after finishing the final mission in Chapter 5.
  • Challenge Unlocks: Each of the 61 total cheats has an individual gameplay challenge requirement that must be completed before its button input registers.
  • Saving and Trophies Disabled: Activating any cheat immediately disables saving and blocks trophies for that session. Make a clean manual save before testing any code.
  • How to Enter Cheats: Open your phone with D-Pad Up, hold L1 (PS5) or LB (Xbox), press R1 / RB, and enter the D-Pad directional sequence.
