Grand Theft Auto VI

Set across the sun-drenched state of Leonida, this guide tracks the five-chapter campaign of Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos alongside all 35 requirements for 100% completion, Strangers & Freaks mission strands, collectibles, money routes, and trophy milestones. Because the game launched on November 19, 2026 and lacks a mission replay menu, keeping disciplined manual saves and checking chapter cutoffs early protects a clean playthrough.

Leonida Regions & Core Cast

The map expands beyond Vice City into six major regions across Leonida. Knowing the six geographic zones and the primary contacts introduced in promotional materials helps orient missions and collectibles early without spoiling the plot.

Six Major Regions of Leonida
Vice City Sprawling coastal metropolis containing Ocean Beach, Downtown, Little Cuba, Bayside, and major commercial corridors.
Leonida Keys Tropical island chain where Chapter 1 begins, anchored around Key Lento, beach hideouts, and coastal diving waters.
Grassrivers Wetlands and mangrove swamps populated by alligators, dense wildlife, and airboat routes.
Port Gellhorn Industrial coastal city along the forgotten coast featuring highways, bays, and blue-collar criminal hubs.
Ambrosia Rural heartland centered around its sugar refinery and surrounding small-town operations.
Mount Kalaga Northern wilderness national park with forests, cliffs, waterfalls, hunting grounds, and elevated meditation spots.
Protagonists & Early Contacts
Jason Duval Former U.S. Army soldier running local smuggling work in the Leonida Keys at the start of Chapter 1.
Lucia Caminos Co-protagonist beginning the story inside Leonida's correctional system before reuniting with Jason.
Brian Heder Veteran smuggler and boatyard operator in the Leonida Keys who assigns Jason's opening jobs.
Cal Hampton Jason's boatyard friend and fishing partner who monitors Coast Guard channels.
Boobie Ike Vice City entrepreneur, realtor, and club owner tied to Jack of Hearts and local music ventures.
Dre'Quan Priest Jason's longtime Vice City contact partnering with Boobie Ike on Only Raw Records.
Raul Bautista Experienced bank robber who introduces high-stakes crew work in Vice City.
