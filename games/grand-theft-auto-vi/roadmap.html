Phase 1: Prologue & Chapter 1 (Just Like Paradise)

0/5 (0%)

Chapter 1 takes place primarily in the Leonida Keys and introduces the game's only potentially missable trophy trigger alongside several Key Lento errands. Review Beginner Tips and Missables before advancing past the halfway mark of this chapter.

  • 1. Clear Prologue and Gone Fishing, Then Create Save Anchor #1

    Complete Prologue and Gone Fishing (unlocks the Just Like Paradise trophy). Buy a pistol suppressor at Ammu-Nation in Key Lento and begin scanning vehicles with WAiNK and wildlife with binoculars immediately. Follow Chapter 1 Main Story.

  • 2. Finish Shirley's Missable Errands Before Leaving Key Lento

    Complete Shirley's two optional tasks (Get Shirley's Rum and Steal the Scooter) and start Kenton and Kirk while operating from Jason's house. Shirley's errands lock out permanently once Chapter 1 finishes.

  • 3. Unlock BuckMe Banking and Prep the House Before Coming Home

    Complete optional house preparation errands ahead of Coming Home to raise your early relationship score, then finish Buck Me to unlock the BuckMe phone app and ATM deposits.

  • 4. Trigger the Missable Balcony Meditation After Down to Earth

    Make a safety save before Down to Earth. On the morning after Down to Earth, follow Lucia onto the balcony of Jason's house, target her with L2 or LT, and select Accept. This registers the Chapter 1 meditation spot needed for Positive Mental Attitude.

  • 5. Clear Optional Donnie's Lost Drugs and Complete Discharge Time

    Finish the optional Donnie's Lost Drugs task if you want extra early cash, then finish Recalibration, Tourist Trap, Home Sweet Home, and Discharge Time to unlock the Paradise Lost trophy and enter Chapter 2.

Phase 2: Chapter 2 (Paradise Lost)

0/3 (0%)

Chapter 2 expands operations into mainland Leonida and opens free-roam character switching alongside major hunting, racing, and vehicle delivery strands.

  • 6. Create Save Anchor #2 and Play Through Coffee Break

    Save in a fresh slot at the start of Chapter 2. Progress through Chapter 2 Main Story until Coffee Break, which unlocks free-roam switching between Jason and Lucia (hold D-Pad Right and press R1 or RB).

  • 7. Open Chapter 2 Strangers & Vehicle Deliveries

    Once Coffee Break is complete, start Bret Vogel as Jason (obtaining the Killtelligence SM-69 hunting rifle and Target Decks), Moses vehicle exports for steady early cash, Billy, and Kyle.

  • 8. Complete Optional Chapter 2 Tasks and Checking Out

    Clear optional jobs Thrillbillies and Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab, then complete Afternoon Delight and Checking Out to transition into Vice City.

Phase 3: Chapter 3 (Tits Ass Drug Money)

0/4 (0%)

Chapter 3 is the longest chapter in the campaign at 26 main missions and moves the couple into Vice City. Key robbery hardware, fencing networks, and Vice City stranger strands unlock throughout this act.

  • 9. Create Save Anchor #3 and Establish Bayside in Welcome to Vice City

    Save at the start of Chapter 3 and finish Welcome to Vice City to unlock your Bayside Apartment and Vice City contacts. Follow Chapter 3 Main Story.

  • 10. Watch for Yago's Hourglass Race Mission and Chapter 3 Strands

    Start Yago's street races in Little Cuba and complete Race Against Yago promptly whenever its hourglass icon appears. Open Andy (Lucia), Tyrone, Yasmani, and Dunk as they appear.

  • 11. Unlock the Dye Pack Jammer and Randy's Key Cloner

    Play through Credit Check to unlock the Dye Pack Jammer (required later for cleaning marked bills) and Clonin' Keys, Makin' GS to unlock Randy's Wish List high-end vehicle theft tasks.

  • 12. Complete the Chapter 3 Finale

    Stock full body armor and trunk weapons before starting The Cartel Heist. Completing it finishes Chapter 3 and unlocks the Vice City, Baby! trophy.

Phase 4: Chapter 4 (Vice City, Baby)

0/3 (0%)

Chapter 4 spans 25 main missions centered around Ocean Beach and Vice City and unlocks the game's most profitable repeatable money method near the end of the chapter.

  • 13. Create Save Anchor #4 and Progress Through Mission 22

    Save in a new slot at Ocean Beach and work through Chapter 4 Main Story through VCIA Takedown. Keep scanning new cars and picking off nearby Gang Operations and Smash 'n' Grabs along the way.

  • 14. Complete "That's Where The Money Is..." at Cafe Orquidea

    After VCIA Takedown, wait for Yasmani's optional mission at Cafe Orquidea in Ocean Beach. Completing it unlocks Yasmani's $10,000 security van tips for repeatable large Bank Vault robberies. See Bank Vault Walkthrough.

  • 15. Finish Sunset and the Chapter 4 Closing Mission

    Complete Sunset and Starting from Zero to enter the final chapter and unlock the Top of the World, Ma trophy.

Phase 5: Chapter 5 (Top of the World, Ma)

0/2 (0%)

Chapter 5 is a focused seven-mission finale. Make a fresh save before starting because free roam temporarily pauses during the final sequence before returning permanently after credits.

  • 16. Create Save Anchor #5 and Clear the Seven Finale Missions

    Save before Bird in the Hand and complete all seven missions in Chapter 5 Main Story. Finishing the finale unlocks You Gotta Know When to Get Off, opens the in-game 100% Completion menu, and unlocks the post-game Cheat menu.

  • 17. Inspect Progress > Stats > 100% Completion

    Open the newly revealed 100% Completion board to audit every category that already received retroactive credit from your story run before starting targeted cleanup.

Phase 6: Post-Game 100% Completion & Platinum Cleanup

0/3 (0%)

Free roam continues across Leonida after the credits roll and nothing on the 100% checklist is missable. Leave cheats disabled until every trophy unlocks.

  • 18. Finish Strict Full-Clear Categories First

    Knock out the categories that require 100% completion on 100% & Collectibles: all 39 Gang Operations, 22 Bank Jobs, 8 Vaults, 7 Payday Loan Jobs, 33 Underwater Discoveries, 42 Shooting Range Gold Medals, 42 Drone Time Trials, 15 Bret's Target Decks, and all 4 Video Games.

  • 19. Complete Partial-Threshold Collectibles, Fencing, and Strangers

    Clean up the remaining partial requirements: 100 Cocaine Flamingos (of 125), 15 Collectible Card Sets (of 18), 5 Kenton Sets (of 7), 100 WAiNK Locations, 200 Base Vehicles, 100 Animals, 300 Stunts, 70 Challenges, 100 Optional Tasks, all four Fencing totals, and remaining Strangers & Freaks strands.

  • 20. Clean Up Remaining Miscellaneous Trophies

    Visit all 10 Meditation Spots (completing the final Ascent Mountain unlock) for Positive Mental Attitude, finish any remaining activity or partner trophies on Trophies, and collect A Long Road plus Vice Got a Price!
