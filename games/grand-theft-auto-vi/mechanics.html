Core Systems & Mechanics

Leonida runs on interconnected simulation rules governing police memory, long-term criminal reputation, dual-protagonist habits, trunk inventory, and vehicle security. Understanding how these systems interact lets you escape multi-star chases cleanly, avoid permanent reputation locks, and build Focus and Strength efficiently.

1. Wanted Level vs. Regional Heat & Suspect Descriptions

Law enforcement combines an active 6-star Wanted Level with a persistent regional Heat memory system. Breaking line of sight alone leaves a district hot if officers still recognize your face, outfit, or car.

Witnesses and Suspect Data

Crimes generally require a live witness or triggered security alarm before police dispatch activates. Once spotted, dispatch broadcasts specific physical descriptors including your current clothing, your vehicle model and paint, any visible weapon, and whether you were seen operating together as a couple.

Active Wanted vs. Regional Heat

Your Wanted Level controls immediate response strength, scaling from taser-equipped patrol officers at low stars up to heavily armed tactical units at maximum six-star escalation. After escaping an active pursuit, regional Heat lingers over that district on your pause map. Returning immediately to a hot district in the same vehicle or outfit re-triggers police recognition.

Countering Suspect Descriptions

  • Change Clothing or Headwear: Swap into one of the spare outfits stored in your vehicle trunk or buy a quick hat at a nearby shop to wipe the clothing descriptor from your HUD.
  • Swap Vehicles or Visit Pay 'n' Spray: Ditch a flagged getaway car for an unseen vehicle or run it through Pay 'n' Spray to clear vehicle tracking.
  • Split Up the Couple: When wanted together as Jason and Lucia, separating reduces dispatch's ability to flag a matching pair.
  • Holster or Stash Flagged Weapons: Putting away or dropping the weapon seen during the crime removes weapon-match alerts.

2. Criminal Profile (5 Tiers) & Color-Coded Hit Markers

Jason and Lucia maintain independent Criminal Profiles measuring operational discipline rather than traditional morality. Mandatory story kills and legitimate self-defense are generally ignored by the tracker.

Tier Icon Color How It Develops Gameplay Effects
Professional Yellow-Gold Stealth takedowns, non-lethal control, destroying security tapes, clean getaways without civilian deaths. Builds Focus progression through stealth and low-profile tactics, keeping standard police response calmer.
Hardened Orange-Peach Calculated force such as limb shots or neutralizing armed threats who fight back. Balanced intermediate reputation between stealth and lethal playstyles.
Violent Purple Repeated lethal escalation against fleeing witnesses, police, or bystanders beyond job requirements. Shifts Focus progression toward combat headshots and lethal actions while raising police aggression.
Psycho Light Violet-Gray Sustained indiscriminate violence against crowds and law enforcement. Triggers aggressive police response and explicit on-screen warnings before permanent lockout.
Irredeemable Dark Teal-Gray (Fractured) Continuing mass violence past Psycho warnings. Permanent for that playthrough. Cannot be reversed, and police escalate to shoot-on-sight rapidly.

Combat feedback uses three color-coded hit markers so you can manage lethality accurately: White registers a standard hit, Yellow confirms a non-lethal incapacitation, and Red confirms a fatal kill.

3. Character Switching & The 4-Level Relationship Scale

Free-roam switching between Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos unlocks in Chapter 2 during the main mission Coffee Break.

Switching and Duo Controls

  • Switch Characters: Hold D-Pad Right and press R1 (PS5) or RB (Xbox) to open the bottom-right switcher and swap protagonists.
  • Meet Up: From the character switcher, press Triangle / Y to call your partner to meet up for joint free-roam activities or robberies.
  • Pass Stash Bags: While running a heist or robbery together, press D-Pad Right followed by Circle / B to pass loot bags between Jason and Lucia.

Relationship Scale (Levels 1 to 4)

Jason and Lucia's bond moves across four levels: Level 1 (Running on Empty), Level 2 (Partners in Crime), Level 3 (Crazy in Love), and Level 4 (Hot Together). Shared lifestyle habits raise the meter (signaled by a chime and pink heart icon), including joint store or bank robberies, pool, mini golf, kayaking, skydiving, beach walks, and working out side by side at the gym.

4. Fitness App, Character Stats, Focus Mode & Sleep

Both protagonists track three primary physical attributes separately via the phone Fitness App, paired with fatigue and weight maintenance.

Stat HUD Bar Gameplay Benefit How to Train
Strength Pink / Rose (Heart) Increases maximum health and physical resilience. Weightlifting and resistance workouts at gyms using analog trigger rep controls.
Cardio / Stamina Mint-Cyan (Lightning) Increases maximum sprint and swim stamina. Gym treadmills and stationary bikes, plus sprinting, swimming, and cycling outdoors.
Focus Violet-Purple (Target) Extends combat slow-motion Focus Mode (activated with L3 + R3). Shooting ranges, golf, pool, fishing, and Criminal Profile aligned combat actions.

Skipping workouts gradually reduces muscle mass, while excessive junk food causes visible weight gain. Monitor sleep through the phone tracker because sleep deprivation slows attribute recovery. Drinking protein shakes from your fridge or buying protein bars at gyms speeds up post-workout recovery.

5. Weapon Carry Limits & Working Car Trunks

Equipment management follows grounded carry rules paired with a redesigned rectangular weapon-and-item wheel accessed via R1 / RB.

  • On-Person Carry Cap: Jason and Lucia carry a compact personal loadout (typically up to two pistols and two rifles or longarms) rather than an entire arsenal.
  • Trunk Loadout Storage: Personal vehicle trunks store spare longarms, specialized gear, extra body armor, and up to five spare outfits.
  • Direct Shop-to-Trunk Buying: Inside Ammu-Nation equipment menus, buy extra body armor directly into trunk storage (displayed as X/3 in trunk loadout) so replacements stay within reach between gunfights.
  • Quick Consumables: While holding the wheel open, bottom-left quick slots consume painkillers (Square / X) and focus/energy items (Triangle / Y).

6. WAiNK App, Vehicle Security & Key Cloners

Open your phone with D-Pad Up to access the WAiNK app, which serves both vehicle reconnaissance and landmark collectible scanning.

  • Vehicle Scanning: Point WAiNK at any car, boat, or aircraft to log its model toward the 200-vehicle 100% goal and inspect its market value, lock state, alarm/tracker tier, and registration cost.
  • Landmark Scanning: Your controller vibrates near WAiNK scan points (Landmarks, Curiosities, Sculptures, and No Flusher Tags). Scan 100 locations for 100% completion and the You WAiNKer! Silver trophy.
  • Security Tiers & Key Cloner: Older cars can be broken into or hotwired directly at the risk of loud alarms, whereas high-end sports cars require bypass tools such as the Key Cloner (introduced in Chapter 3 during Clonin' Keys, Makin' GS) to steal cleanly and strip trackers through fences.

7. BuckMe Banking, Fences & Garages

Physical cash and stolen goods follow strict laundering rules across Leonida.

  • BuckMe Checking vs. Pocket Cash: Unlocked in Chapter 1 (Buck Me). Dying strips loose pocket cash, so use BuckMe's ATM Finder to deposit earnings into Checking regularly. Prior to Buck Me, stash cash under Jason's bedroom floorboards.
  • BuckMe Family Savings: Tied to the optional task Save for the Future. Deposits are completely immune to death penalties and unlock account limits, yet cannot be withdrawn back into spendable funds.
  • Fences & Dye Pack Jammer: Narcotics, jewelry, hot vehicles, and marked bank bills must be sold or cleaned through fences. Marked bank bills require the Dye Pack Jammer (unlocked in Chapter 3 during Credit Check) before Yasmani can clean them.
  • Garages & Safehouses: Story housing shifts across chapters as Jason and Lucia move across Leonida, while standalone garages can be acquired to store vehicles, gear, and loadouts.

8. Gameplay & Accessibility Settings

Standard Easy, Normal, and Hard difficulty presets are absent. Navigate to Settings > Gameplay to adjust combat and driving survivability at any time without disabling trophies or 100% progress.

  • Accessible Combat: Adjusts incoming combat damage scaling to make heavy firefights more forgiving.
  • Reduced Vehicle Damage: Lowers collision and gunfire damage taken by your vehicle during high-speed chases and getaway sequences.
