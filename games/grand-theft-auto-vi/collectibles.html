100% Completion Checklist & Collectibles

Reaching 100% Completion unlocks the Gold trophy A Long Road. The in-game checklist appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion after finishing Chapter 5, counts all earlier progress retroactively, and contains zero missable items. While the full Progress Database holds roughly 3,000 entries, the 35 checklist requirements below ask for roughly 60% of the total database.

Part 1: Story, Criminal Ventures & Fencing (12 Requirements)

0/12 (0%)

Robbery categories such as Gang Operations, Bank Jobs, Payday Loan Jobs, and Vaults require complete clears. Loot key valuables inside Gang Operations and grab all three containers (Vault, Cash Drawer, and Safe) during large bank hits so each location registers fully completed.

  • 1. Story Completion (100% of Main Story Missions)

    Complete all 91 core main missions across Chapters 1 through 5.

  • 2. Optional Tasks Completed (100)

    Complete 100 optional missions, errands, and stranger tasks across Leonida.

  • 3. Gang Operations Cleared (39 / 39)

    Full clear required. Collect the marked valuable stash at each site.

  • 4. Bank Jobs Fully Completed (22 / 22)

    Full clear required across all Leonida banks.

  • 5. Payday Loan Jobs Fully Completed (7 / 7)

    Full clear required across all seven Payday Loan branches.

  • 6. Smash 'n' Grabs Fully Completed (34 / 35)

    Jewelry store and pawn shop robberies across Leonida.

  • 7. Stick Ups Fully Completed (50 / 91)

    Rob 50 out of 91 convenience stores.

  • 8. Vaults Hit (8 / 8)

    Crack and rob all eight Large Bank Vaults (unlocked via Yasmani in Chapter 4, covered in Bank Vaults).

  • 9. Marked Bills Fenced ($250,000)

    Launder $250,000 in marked bank bills through Yasmani using the Dye Pack Jammer.

  • 10. Vehicles Fenced ($200,000)

    Deliver and fence $200,000 worth of stolen vehicles (Moses exports and Randy's Wish List count strongly toward this).

  • 11. Jewelry Fenced ($100,000)

    Sell $100,000 in stolen jewelry from Smash 'n' Grabs and safes at jewelry fences.

  • 12. Narcotics Fenced ($100,000)

    Sell $100,000 in stolen narcotics collected from Gang Operations, Cocaine Flamingos, and drug stashes.

Part 2: Exploration, Collectibles & Activities (19 Requirements)

0/19 (0%)

Shooting Ranges, Top Fun Drone Time Trials, Bret's Target Decks, Unique Playlists, and Video Games demand full completion, while collectibles like Cocaine Flamingos, Card Sets, and Meditation Spots allow partial leeway for the 100% checklist.

  • 13. Kenton Sets Completed (5 / 7)

    Complete five underwater shipwreck artifact sets for Kenton Rees.

  • 14. Collectible Card Sets Found (15 / 18)

    Complete 15 out of 18 total card sets (480 individual cards exist in total across Leonida). Completing 3 sets unlocks Don't Touch My Deck.

  • 15. WAiNK Locations Scanned (100)

    Scan 100 landmarks, curiosities, sculptures, and tags with the WAiNK app (controller vibrates nearby). Also unlocks You WAiNKer!

  • 16. Magazines, Books & Comics Found (30 / 34)

    Collect 30 scattered publications across interiors and shops.

  • 17. Meditation Spots Completed (15 / 18)

    Each open-world spot done by both Jason and Lucia counts twice. All 10 locations are listed below.

  • 18. Cocaine Flamingos Destroyed (100 / 125)

    Shoot pink lawn flamingos with black-and-white beaks to expose hidden cocaine packages. Destroying 50 also unlocks the Inflatable Flamingo cheat reward.

  • 19. Unique Playlists Completed (2 / 2)

    Find and complete both collectible audio playlists.

  • 20. Discovered Styles (70 / 79)

    Unlock 70 clothing and visual styles across Leonida's shops and world drops.

  • 21. Unique Races Won (30)

    Win 30 different races across street, dirt, and water circuits. Winning 20 unlocks Earn Cash, Not Kudos.

  • 22. Race Challenge Gold Medals (10 / 13)

    Earn Gold Medals in 10 of the 13 dedicated Race Challenges tracked under Progress > Activities > Racing.

  • 23. Shooting Range Gold Medals (42 / 42)

    Earn Gold on every challenge across Griffin Shooting Range and Rodgers Ranch. Also unlocks The Shootist Silver trophy.

  • 24. Video Games Played (4 / 4)

    Play Tenebrae Lux, Street Trash III, Time Jackers: Rewind (home TV), and Bang For Your Buck (open-world arcade cabinet).

  • 25. Pool Opponents Defeated (5)

    Beat five Pool opponents across Leonida bars.

  • 26. Mini Golf Locations Played (2)

    Complete rounds at both Mini Golf courses.

  • 27. Basketball Hoops Scored (90)

    Find and sink shots at 90 neighborhood basketball hoops.

  • 28. Drone Time Trials Completed (42 / 42)

    Win all 42 Top Fun Drone Time Trials across Leonida. Also unlocks the Top Fun Bronze trophy.

  • 29. Stunts Completed (300)

    Land 300 vehicle stunt jumps (250 unlocks the Cunning Stunts Silver trophy).

  • 30. Challenges Completed (70 / 80)

    Complete 70 out of 80 open-world gameplay challenges (50 unlocks the One Last Thing Bronze trophy).

  • 31. Bret's Target Decks (15 / 15)

    Complete all 15 hunting commissions for Bret Vogel as Jason (unlocked in Chapter 2).

Part 3: World Research & Discovery (4 Requirements)

0/4 (0%)

Underwater Discoveries require finding all 33 sites across Leonida's waters, whereas Vehicles, Animals, and Weapons only require partial catalog completion.

  • 32. Base Model Vehicles Scanned (200 / 277)

    Scan 200 unique base model vehicles (cars, trucks, boats, and aircraft) using the WAiNK phone app. The full database holds 277 base models and 280 standout variants (557 total).

  • 33. Animals Studied (100 / 255)

    Zoom in and study 100 different wildlife species through your binoculars. Killing or skinning animals is unnecessary.

  • 34. Standard Issue Weapons Collected (50)

    Acquire and log 50 standard issue firearms and melee weapons in your Progress Database.

  • 35. Underwater Discoveries Found (33 / 33)

    Full clear required. Locate all 33 shipwrecks and ocean-floor stashes across Leonida using diving gear and the metal detector.

All 10 Meditation Spot Locations (Positive Mental Attitude)

0/10 (0%)

Track progress in-game under Progress > Collections > Exploration > Meditations. Spot #1 is the only potentially missable trophy trigger in the game, and Spot #10 unlocks automatically after clearing the first nine. Completing open-world spots with both Jason and Lucia counts twice toward the 18 total sessions.

  • 1. Jason's House Balcony (Missable, Chapter 1 Only)

    Key Lento, Mariana County (Peach Palm Dr). On the morning after Down to Earth, follow Lucia onto the balcony, aim at her with L2 / LT, and choose Accept.

  • 2. Smooth Sand

    Sandy bar just west of Capri in Mariana County.

  • 3. Swamp

    Buckskin Swamp in Kelly County, located at the end of a wooden pier near a lookout tower.

  • 4. Worn Stone

    Camp Moonflower in Kelly County.

  • 5. Red Rocks

    Manifest Canyon in Kelly County.

  • 6. Water Wheel

    Near the paper mill in Lummox County.

  • 7. Jutting Cliff

    Mount Kalaga in Lummox County.

  • 8. Waterfall

    Northwest of Tallaloko in Lummox County.

  • 9. Seaside

    Rocky shoreline stretch in Leonard County.

  • 10. Ascent Mountain (Final Unlock)

    Stone circle summit in Lummox County. Appears automatically after clearing the other nine locations.
