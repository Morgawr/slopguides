Game Mechanics

Every episode alternates between theatrical アドベンチャーパート segments and grid-based 戦闘パート missions. Understanding how timed dialogue feeds directly into mech stats keeps both story routes and combat effortless.

LIPS Dialogue & Free-Roam Exploration

During conversations, LIPS presents three spoken replies beneath a shrinking countdown bar. Allowing the bar to empty completely acts as a fourth option representing silence, restraint, or holding your ground. Later series entries added Timing LIPS in サクラ大戦2 and Analog LIPS in サクラ大戦3 and the PlayStation 2 remake, whereas Dreamcast サクラ大戦 uses standard timed LIPS prompts exclusively.

  • Free-Roam Room Limits. Several exploration windows end automatically once you enter a specific destination room or exceed a hidden move counter across rooms in 大帝国劇場. Visit side targets and the 売店 before entering the plot destination.
  • Cursor Inspection Events. Certain scenes switch to a free-moving pointer over a room or character. Clicking too many times on sensitive details, such as staring at マリア・タチバナ in Episode 1 or clicking away from 真宮寺さくら during her Episode 6 conversation, triggers scoldings or lowers trust.
  • Episode Eyecatch Saves. Saving is restricted to アイキャッチ transitions, pre-battle deployments, and post-battle screens. Loading works from any non-combat menu.

Trust Chime Cues vs. Battle Morale

Two separate mechanics govern how your troupe responds to 大神一郎 across the game. 信頼度 is a hidden cumulative score tracked across all ten episodes, while やる気 reflects how strongly a pilot fights during a given stage.

Indicator When It Appears Meaning & Effect
Rising Chime Tone Immediately after a LIPS choice Increases 信頼度 with one or more heroines.
Low Dull Tone Immediately after a LIPS choice Decreases 信頼度 due to rude, careless, or indecisive replies.
Pre-Battle やる気 Start of 戦闘パート Scales a 光武 unit's attack power, defense, and movement range upward when Trust is high. 神崎すみれ and 李紅蘭 show the widest swing between low and high Trust.
気力 Gauge 0% to 100% inside combat Builds when attacking, taking hits, or using Charge. Reaching 100% enables 必殺攻撃 and 合体攻撃.

Grid Tactical Combat & Unit Upgrade

Battles unfold on a square-tile grid in turn order displayed along the HUD. Unlike the PlayStation 2 remake's real-time ARMS engine, the Dreamcast release uses pure turn-based grid tactics, enhanced over the 1996 Saturn original with M2's responsive movement cancellation so you can preview attack ranges before committing.

  • Standard Turn Actions. Each unit moves once and performs one action per turn from 攻撃, 防御, ため, 回復, 待機, or 必殺.
  • 蒸気スタンド. Standing adjacent to or atop a field steam stand restores 光武 HP without spending a personal repair turn.
  • Episode 8 神武 Upgrade. During the second battle of Episode 8, every 花組 pilot transitions from 光武 to 神武, expanding special attack zones dramatically for 真宮寺さくら, 神崎すみれ, and マリア・タチバナ.
  • Zero-KO Priority. Completing stages quickly earns praise from 藤枝あやめ in the following episode, while keeping every heroine above critical HP prevents trust penalties from retreat. If 大神一郎 falls, the mission ends immediately.

Protect & Combination Attacks

Team-wide 風林火山 Captain Tactics stances belong to サクラ大戦2 and later installments. In Dreamcast サクラ大戦, 大神一郎 commands two signature leadership tools instead.

  • かばう (8 Uses Per Battle). Target any allied heroine on Ogami's turn to shield her until she is hit or you reassign the guard. Whenever an enemy attacks a protected heroine, both she and Ogami take zero damage and her 信頼度 increases. Use this to bait bosses safely with fragile units such as アイリス or マリア・タチバナ.
  • 合体攻撃. Available from Episode 8 onward when Ogami stands adjacent to your highest-trust heroine and both units hold 100% 気力. The strike hits every enemy within five tiles of the heroine using Ogami's high base attack power. Pairing Ogami with アイリス turns an otherwise non-offensive healer into a heavy boss-clearing partner.

Mini-Games & System File Persistence

Mini-games appear exclusively during even-numbered episodes (Episodes 2, 4, 6, and 8). Any mini-game you trigger in the story permanently registers into your VMU System File, unlocking both a replayable challenge version and a secret reward illustration inside 帝劇の長い一日 after clearing the game once.
