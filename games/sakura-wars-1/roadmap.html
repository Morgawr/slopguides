Play Order & Ending Roadmap

Every playthrough follows a single ten-episode arc and finishes with an individual epilogue movie for whichever 花組 heroine holds the highest trust at the end of Episode 10. Clearing all six heroine endings unlocks the Grand Finale sequence, while smart save-branching at Episode 8 cuts total replay time dramatically.

How Ending Selection Works

Two major checkpoints govern your route. During Episode 1 through Episode 7, every LIPS choice, free-roam visit, and かばう shield shapes your hidden 信頼度 rankings. Entering Episode 8, your top-ranked heroine invites you to 初詣, while you may still freely invite any member standing within your overall top three.

Whichever heroine accompanies you to 明治神宮 receives a decisive trust surge, making her your lead partner for 合体攻撃 and Episode 9 and 10 centerpiece scenes unless later choices overtake her.

Save-Branching & Collectibles Strategy

Run 1 Baseline and Episode 8 Split

  • Episode 1 Opening Setup. When 米田一基 asks whether you already have a favorite in the troupe, answer yes to grant an immediate starting trust boost to your Run 1 target. Review Episode 1 and Heroines before answering.
  • Episodes 1 through 6 Collectibles Pass. Play every mini-game in Episodes 2, 4, and 6 at least once so all six become permanently playable in 帝劇の長い一日, and buy all six regular heroines' ブロマイド cards one per episode.
  • Episode 8 Shrine Anchor Save. Keep a permanent VMU save right at the start of Episode 8 before choosing your 初詣 partner. From a balanced run where 真宮寺さくら, 神崎すみれ, and マリア・タチバナ occupy your top three, you can clear all three endings from this single save.
  • Episode 8 Gift Shop Special Bromide Loop. Create a second temporary save right before returning from the three-week training trip in Episode 8. Collect one New Year Special ブロマイド at the 売店, advance to the next pre-battle save prompt to write it to your System File, reload, and repeat for all six heroines plus 藤枝あやめ.
  • System File Accumulation Rule. Always overwrite or save onto the same Dreamcast VMU system profile across runs. Clear flags for endings, bromides, movies, and mini-game high scores accumulate across separate saves in the shared system file.

Recommended 6-Ending Completion Order

Because 真宮寺さくら receives generous story trust, 神崎すみれ gains huge combat stats from high trust, and マリア・タチバナ spikes naturally in Episode 3, pairing those three into Run 1 via the Episode 8 split leaves Run 2 focused cleanly on the remaining trio (アイリス, 李紅蘭, and 桐島カンナ).

  • Ending 1: 真宮寺さくら (Run 1 Main). Recommended first ending. Her route features the unique animated train-station 初詣 cutscene and anchors the core story.
  • Ending 2: 神崎すみれ (Run 1 Ep.8 Branch). Reload your Episode 8 anchor, accept or invite Sumire to the shrine, and witness her full-strength 神武 area sweep through Episodes 9 and 10.
  • Ending 3: マリア・タチバナ (Run 1 Ep.8 Branch). Reload the same Episode 8 anchor and invite Maria for her quiet shrine route and final epilogue.
  • Ending 4: アイリス (Run 2 Main). Start Run 2 picking Iris in Yoneda's opening question, keep her paired with 李紅蘭 and 桐島カンナ in the top three by Episode 8, and finish Episode 4's birthday date cleanly.
  • Ending 5: 李紅蘭 (Run 2 Ep.8 Branch). Reload Run 2's Episode 8 anchor, ride Kohran's upgraded steam motorcycle to the shrine, and complete her Ep.8 airplane dream scenes.
  • Ending 6: 桐島カンナ (Run 2 Ep.8 Branch). Reload Run 2's Episode 8 anchor and sprint with Kanna to the shrine. Completing this sixth ending immediately triggers the Grand Finale card and message.
  • Post-Game & Sequel Transfer. Enter 帝劇の長い一日 to clear all seven mini-game reward CGs, Ayame's quiz, and こいこい大戦, then keep your final クリアデータ ready on the VMU for サクラ大戦2.
