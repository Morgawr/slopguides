Beginner Tips

Success in サクラ大戦 connects daily theatre life directly to tactical mech performance. Building consistent habits around LIPS timing, gift shop visits, save timing, and battle positioning prevents missed collectibles and keeps your chosen heroine ready for late-game chapters.

Core Campaign Habits

  • Treat Silence as an Active LIPS Option. Letting the timer expire without pressing a button counts as staying silent or holding back. Multiple tense scenes, such as 真宮寺さくら and 神崎すみれ arguing in Episode 2 or マリア・タチバナ confronting you in Episode 3, reward silence more safely than picking sides.
  • Listen for the Sound Chime After Every Choice. Rising chime tones signal increased 信頼度, while flat or dull tones signal a drop. Keep game audio audible during adventure scenes so you always know how a response landed.
  • Distinguish Trust from Battle Morale. Adventure 信頼度 accumulates across episodes to determine your ending and unlocks 合体攻撃 in Episode 8, while per-battle やる気 scales movement, attack, and defense inside combat. Allowing a unit to fall in battle penalizes that heroine's trust.
  • Visit the Gift Shop in Every Episode from 1 to 6. You may purchase exactly one ブロマイド per episode at the 売店. Buying all six regular heroine photos by the end of Episode 6 is mandatory for unlocking the Episode 8 New Year specials.
  • Anchor Separate VMU Saves at Eyecatches. Saves happen exclusively at episode アイキャッチ prompts, pre-battle briefings, and post-battle transitions. Maintain dedicated VMU slots before Episode 4's date prompt, before Episode 7's finale, and at the start of Episode 8.
  • Use All Eight Charges of Protect Each Battle. Unlike later sequels limited to three uses, Dreamcast サクラ大戦 lets 大神一郎 activate かばう up to eight times per battle. Both the shielded heroine and Ogami take zero damage while raising her trust.
  • Watch the Turn Order Strip and Enemy Ranges. Always check the turn-order indicator before moving and inspect enemy attack tiles before ending a turn. Picking off enemies that act next prevents damage entirely, and keeping 神崎すみれ and 真宮寺さくら near 100% 気力 lets them clear groups in one sweep.
  • Create the System File and Keep Clear Data. When prompted at boot, initialize the system file on your VMU and save your post-credits クリアデータ. That file unlocks 帝劇の長い一日 and imports into the Dreamcast release of サクラ大戦2 for special opening events.
Dreamcast Controls & Display Note

The Dreamcast port supports the standard controller, Dreamcast Mouse (especially helpful for the Episode 8 shooting gallery mini-game), and Purupuru Pack rumble. Because the first two Dreamcast entries output 480i interlaced video at boot, standard direct-VGA cables show an unsupported-cable notice. Players using a VGA box with a TV/VGA switch boot in TV mode and flip to VGA once the title loads.
