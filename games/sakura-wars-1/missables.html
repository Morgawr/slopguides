Missables

Every episode in サクラ大戦 closes its free-roam windows permanently once you trigger the next story scene. Track every episode-locked ブロマイド, mini-game unlock, room-step timer, and hidden shower or side scene below.

Missables Secured: 0/14 (0%)

Bromides & Mini-Game Locks

  • Episodes 1 to 6 Gift Shop Bromide Quota. Buy one ブロマイド in each of Episodes 1 through 6 at the 売店. Because Episode 7 has no shop window, missing even one episode makes all six New Year Special Bromides in Episode 8 unobtainable on that run. Counter order from 高村椿 outward is 真宮寺さくら, 神崎すみれ, マリア・タチバナ, 李紅蘭, アイリス, and 桐島カンナ.
  • Episode 6 Tsubaki Dinner vs. Bromide Lockout. Talking to 高村椿 at the start of Episode 6 and inviting her to dinner immediately locks out buying your sixth ブロマイド. Either skip inviting her on the opening LIPS prompt or leave the counter before asking her out and re-enter to buy the photo first.
  • Episode 8 New Year & Ayame Special Bromides. With all six regular heroine photos owned, visit the 売店 after returning from training. Pick one from the first LIPS menu (真宮寺さくら, 神崎すみれ, マリア・タチバナ), let the first timer expire for the second menu (アイリス, 桐島カンナ, 李紅蘭), or let both menus time out to claim 藤枝あやめ.
  • Episode 2 Night Patrol Mini-Games (花も嵐も & 帝都のトビウオ). Answering the door knock and refusing sleep is mandatory to trigger night patrol. Inside patrol, visit 李紅蘭 in her room before entering the basement training pool for 神崎すみれ, because Sumire's full escort sequence ends the night patrol.
  • Episode 4 Outfit Mini-Game (着替えまショウ). This mini-game unlocks only if you accept アイリス's birthday date and agree to help pick her outfit that night. Declining the date or asking 真宮寺さくら when Sakura's trust is high skips the mini-game.
  • Episode 4 & 6 Mini-Games (お掃除しちゃうぞ！, オー迷子ッド！, 料理でハラショー). Any mini-game skipped in the main story stays locked inside 帝劇の長い一日. Always play each one at least once during Episodes 2, 4, 6, and 8.

Timed Free-Roam & Story Flags

  • Episode 1 Blindfold Event (楽屋 Wing). Head toward the dressing rooms before visiting 米田一基's office, since entering the Manager's Office immediately ends free roam and skips Sakura's hallway event and the Gift Shop.
  • Episode 2 14部屋 Patrol Cap. Night patrol terminates automatically on your 14th room transition. Visit 李紅蘭, アイリス, マリア・タチバナ, and 真宮寺さくら before spending turns with Sumire.
  • Episode 3 李紅蘭 Early Shower Window (3 Moves). Right after the ticket-tearing scene with Maria and Kasumi, go to the basement changing room within your first three room transitions to witness Kohran's missable shower scene without losing trust.
  • Episode 5 Mansion Antidote Speed & Courtyard Meet Cap (4 Rooms). Before departing for Fukagawa, stay under five room moves before meeting Sumire and Kanna at the entrance. Inside the mansion, collect the antidote promptly to minimize 神崎すみれ and 桐島カンナ HP loss before battle.
  • Episode 6 Sakura Salon Shortcut & Kohran 3-Move Banquet Prep. Refusing Sakura's offer to walk straight to the 2F Salon lets you see Sumire, Maria, Iris, and Kohran first. Later, during banquet prep, visit 李紅蘭's room within three moves to unlock her extra banquet applause trust gain.
  • Episode 6 Underground Rubble Search. When trapped with Sakura underground, find マリア・タチバナ's practice pistol in the bottom-left rubble with minimal clicks, because excessive stray clicks drop the other five pilots' starting battle HP from 50% down to 33%.
  • Episode 8 Sumire Funding Reveal (Before 11th Move). After returning from training, speak with 神崎すみれ early and leave time before your 11th room move so 榊原由里 intercepts you to explain Sumire's 神武 funding sacrifice, letting you apologize in Sumire's room.
  • Episode 9 Basement Shortcut & Maria Shower Trigger. Selecting "Catch Iris" in the 2F Salon spills juice on Maria, unlocking her basement shower scene with 米田一基. When Yoneda intercepts you on your third side room, pick the stall option to finish exploring the theater before entering the hangar.
