Heroines & Combat Roles

大神一郎

Captain of 帝国華撃団・花組. Highest base melee attack and durable frontliner whose defeat triggers an instant mission loss.

  • Tactical Role & Moves. Specializes in 狼虎滅却・快刀乱麻 for single-target boss bursts and かばう (eight charges per map) to nullify damage on a chosen heroine while earning trust. Stand beside your top heroine at 100% 気力 from Episode 8 onward to execute 合体攻撃.

真宮寺さくら

Lead heroine from Sendai. Sincere, earnest, and quick to grow jealous when Ogami lingers with other women.

  • Route & Combat Role. Respond earnestly, remember her stage rehearsals, and visit her room immediately after Episode 2's repair misunderstanding. She unlocks the unique animated 初詣 cutscene. In battle, she boasts the highest average mobility. Keep her at 100% 気力 to sweep lines of enemies with 破邪剣征・桜花放神 and the three-column 破邪剣征・百花繚乱, pairing with Ogami for 狼虎滅却・桜花絢爛.

神崎すみれ

Aristocratic stage star. Sensitive to 信頼度 rank, revealing genuine warmth when kept inside your top three.

  • Route & Combat Role. Indulge her refined pride, deliver her fan letter in Episode 1, scold her firmly after rescuing her in Episode 2 without peeking in the shower, and apologize in Episode 8 once Yuri explains her 神武 funding effort. High trust transforms her into the strongest crowd-clearing unit via obstacle-ignoring 神崎風塵流・胡蝶の舞, five-tile 神崎風塵流・鳳凰の舞, and 狼虎滅却・阿吽の舞.

マリア・タチバナ

Disciplined sharpshooter. Values duty, historical literacy, and calm leadership over bravado or flattery.

  • Route & Combat Role. Answer book and tactical questions accurately, shield the child yourself in Episode 3, stay silent when she vents in your quarters, and support her in Episodes 4 and 6. Statistically reliable regardless of trust level, she snipes from five tiles away over cover with スネグーラチカ and pairs with Ogami for 狼虎滅却・ブードゥシシイ. Always take her into Episode 7's strike team.

アイリス

Young French aristocrat with immense spiritual power. Dislikes being treated as a helpless child.

  • Route & Combat Role. Remember Jean-Paul in Episode 1, accept her Episode 4 birthday date, buy the top-right pendant, take responsibility before Yoneda, and choose to rescue her directly in Episode 4's battle. Her teleportation bypasses barricades to flip switches (Episodes 4 and 6), イリス・マリオネット heals allies and restores their Spirit (excluding herself), and 狼虎滅却・ファンタスティーク・ベゼ gives her full boss-killing utility.

李紅蘭

Kansai-speaking inventor from China. Appreciates honesty, enthusiasm for her machines, and patient encouragement.

  • Route & Combat Role. Escort her directly in Episode 2 (stopping only at the Gift Shop), play Hanafuda on patrol, refuse stage repairs in Episode 2 so she joins you, support attack-type 光武 upgrades in Episode 5, and wait patiently outside her door in Episode 6. Her lobbed grenade normal attack hits across elevation and obstacles with a two-tile splash, while チビロボ focuses single targets and 狼虎滅却・天地風雲 covers wide groups.

桐島カンナ

Okinawan karate master joining in Episode 3. Respects straightforward courage, big appetites, and treating her as a woman.

  • Route & Combat Role. Eat her cooking patiently without nagging in Episode 3, stand your ground rather than fleeing if you enter the Episode 3 shower room, back her up in Episode 5's mansion, and claim a normal appetite in Episode 8's dining hall. In combat, she serves as a fast, high-HP melee vanguard using 一百林牌 against bosses and 狼虎滅却・白狼拳 when paired with Ogami.
