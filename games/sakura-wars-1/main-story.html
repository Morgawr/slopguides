Episodes 1 to 10 Walkthrough

Chronological spoiler-safe guide across Disc 1 (Episodes 1 through 7) and Disc 2 (Episodes 8 through 10). Follow each フリー移動 route in order to catch every ブロマイド, mini-game, and optimal LIPS response without tripping early room cutoffs.

Episode 1 · 帝都・花の華撃団

  • Arrival & First Free Roam. Answer 米田一基's opening question with "Yes" if targeting a specific heroine from the start. Outside the Manager's Office, avoid staring at マリア・タチバナ more than twice. Tell アイリス to guide you to reception (or stay silent after scolding them to gain Maria trust instead). During ticket-tearing, accept the patron's fan letter for 神崎すみれ. Once フリー移動 starts, skip the Manager's Office and walk toward the Dressing Room hallway first to trigger Sakura's blindfold game ("Is it Sakura?"), then visit the Dressing Room (Iris, ask about her and name Jean-Paul), Stage Wing (Maria, ask about the Assault Force), 2F Salon (Sumire, hand over the letter or pick your preference, while silence keeps both Sakura and Sumire neutral), Sumire's Room (wait outside without entering), and the 売店 to buy your Episode 1 ブロマイド.
  • Night Patrol & Morning Rehearsal. Leaving the Manager's Office, let 真宮寺さくら walk you back. Inviting her in raises trust, and staying silent at the door knock avoids offending whichever low-trust member knocks. On night patrol, visit the 2F Library first to answer Maria's book prompt (boosts Maria and Sakura), check the 売店 if you bought Sakura's bromide, then trigger Iris outside the stairwell, fetch Jean-Paul from the Dressing Room, and return it to Iris (letting patrol time out while holding Jean-Paul auto-delivers it as a final scene for Iris affinity). Next morning, pick "Go to Reception" rather than heading straight to the stage to meet Sumire, Iris, and Maria along the way (tell Maria "I haven't forgotten our mission"). At stage rehearsal, step between Sakura and Sumire immediately during the fight.
  • Battle · 上野公園. Face 葵叉丹 and six 脇侍 units. During battle, Sakura asks about your first meeting, so choose a kind reply. Watch the turn-order strip to eliminate the enemy unit queued between your squadmates using Sakura's line 必殺攻撃. Focus fire on Aoi Satan to clear the stage in around five turns with zero damage.

Episode 2 · 敵の名は黒之巣会

  • 李紅蘭's Arrival & Daytime Shop. Check on Kohran and offer to guide her directly to the Manager's Office. Stop at the 売店 along the way to purchase your Episode 2 ブロマイド (buying Kohran's boosts her trust, while avoiding extra upstairs detours if aiming for Kohran), tell her the battle was easy in the Dining Hall, praise Sakura's outfit outside the office, and pick "As captain" (Maria) or "As a man" (Kohran) when meeting Maria.
  • Night Patrol (14-Room Cap) & Stage Repairs. Answer Kohran's door knock and refuse sleep so patrol begins. Visit in strict order starting with Kohran's Room (play 花も嵐も and win 20+ mon), Iris's Room (pajama compliments), 2F Terrace and Basement Ops Room (Maria), Stage Wing (watch Sakura rehearse for an Episode 2 repair bonus), and finally Basement Training Room (Sumire's swimsuit and 帝都のトビウオ). After rescuing Sumire, scold her ("You idiot, be careful"), escort her to the locker room, return to the training hall rather than peeking in the shower, and choose "Panic" when Sakura catches you outside Sumire's room so both keep trust. Next day during the stage mishap, stay silent to earn Iris and Kohran trust, then accept repair duty (refuse only on a dedicated Kohran run so you meet her at the stage instead). Afterward, always visit Sakura's room to apologize after Ayame bandages your finger.
  • Battle · 芝公園. Clear the 脇侍 and upper cannon with Kohran's lobbed shots. Once the invincible shadow spawns, withhold attacks on the decoy, shield your fastest pilot with かばう so the shadow strikes twice and forces the real body to materialize at the bottom edge, then surround and burst the true body in one turn before it reaches Teito Tower.

Episode 3 · オレは隊長失格！？

  • Morning Free Roam & 桐島カンナ's Debut. Right after talking with Maria and Kasumi, go straight to the Basement Locker Room within three room moves for Kohran's missable shower scene. Next, hit the 売店 for your Episode 3 ブロマイド, visit Dressing Room (Iris, June rainy season), 2F Salon (Sumire, "That's how magic works"), 2F Terrace before Maria's Room (stay silent when Maria hesitates), and Ayame's Room before heading to the Stage Wing. Stay silent while holding up the falling pillar (gains Sakura, Sumire, and Kohran trust). Spar with Kanna, wait silently while she cooks without complaining, and tell Maria "Is that a charm?" afterward.
  • Tsukiji Battle 1 & Night Search. Before deploying, choose "Call out to Maria". In Stage 1 against 蒼き刹那, advance Kanna or a fast unit to the pier block holding Setsuna (you do not need to wipe out every enemy). Press the sudden LIPS prompt to shield the child yourself. Upon waking, ask "What happened to the child?" for broad squad trust, stay silent when Maria confronts you in your room, hide when Kanna exits Maria's room, then explore during night free roam through Stage Wing (Sumire shoulder massage, stay silent for top gain), 2F Library (carry Kohran to her own room), Basement Locker Room (Kanna shower, stand your ground instead of fleeing), and finally 1F Lobby to confront Maria.
  • Tsukiji Battles 2 & 3 · Rescuing Maria. In Stage 2, break the left oil drums with Sumire's special to clear both drums and nearby guards, move any unit right of Maria's 光武, then step onto the glowing tile to its left. In Stage 3, Setsuna moves twice per turn but fights alone. Place Maria on the edge of his range while clustering everyone else nearby so he dives into an instant encirclement.

Episode 4 · 暴走！暴走！大暴走！

  • Birthday Date Setup & Morning Rounds. Agree to help Sakura clean the prop room, then handle Iris's birthday invite carefully. Accept immediately (or hesitate and ask Sakura if Sakura's trust is low) to unlock Iris's outfit mini-game 着替えまショウ that night. Next morning, make quick stops before Iris's Room (excessive wandering displeases Iris), stopping at the Prop Room for Sakura's お掃除しちゃうぞ！, 売店 for your Episode 4 ブロマイド, Sumire's Room (answer "Kashiko"), 2F Terrace (Maria, Star Festival wishes), Dining Hall (Kanna, rice and miso soup), and Ayame's Room followed by the Basement Locker Room for Ayame's shower event.
  • Asakusa Date & Theater Incident. Take Iris to Asakusa, buy the top-right pendant, claim you are sweethearts, hold hands in the park, and stay quiet during the cinema scene. Back in the Manager's Office, say "It's my fault" for Iris (or tell Iris to apologize for Maria). Pick whichever heroine you want to raise outside Iris's door. Note that rejecting the date entirely triggers Kohran's lie-detector test and requires checking Iris's diary on her bed to locate her in Asakusa.
  • Battle · 浅草 vs. 白銀の羅刹. Choose "Rescue Iris first" at sortie so you fight with all seven units and unlock Iris's teleportation to open Kaminarimon Gate by Turn 4 or 5 (saving Sakura's victory pose too). On Turn 2, Rasetsu asks who matters most. Naming a heroine boosts her trust and causes Rasetsu to warp her into his courtyard (silence warps Ogami instead). Have the warped unit lure Rasetsu toward the gate while the squad waits outside, then burst him down once the gate opens.

Episode 5 · 花と咲かせよ！乙女の意地で！

  • Stage Quarrel & Pre-Briefing Roam. During Sumire and Kanna's argument, select "Have at it" to keep both girls happy (or "Enough already" for Sakura). Before entering the Ops Room, buy your Episode 5 ブロマイド at the 売店 (it closes during the second free roam), then visit your preferred rival's room (invite both out for dinner to balance both without a hallway clash, or pick one and defend her in the hall). After the briefing, visit up to four rooms maximum before the 1F Lobby, including the Hangar (recommend Attack type for Kohran's 光武), Ops Room (praise Iris to Sakura and answer "Ancient Chinese tactics" to Maria), Iris's Room (tell "The Crab and the Monkey"), and Ayame's Room for Episode 4 turn praise.
  • Fukagawa Mansion Investigation. Choose who to follow ("Wait, both of you" follows the higher-ranked heroine), stay silent during the quarrel to raise both girls, and inspect cleanly for the antidote. On Sumire's side, the spider medicine sits in the 1F Waiting Room, and on Kanna's side, inspect the 1F Bedroom kit first and grab the medicine in the 2F Dining Room. Retrieve the keys from the 1F Servant Room and 2F Bedroom to open the 1F Kitchen, and knock the spider and snake away by hand when cornering the enemy.
  • Battle · 深川 vs. 紅のミロク. Clear the initial three guards immediately with Sumire's special. Place Sumire and Kanna side by side for their banter and stay silent to raise both. Keep Sakura or Maria charged at Tier 3 to wipe out the two 紅蜂隊 spawns, then stage your squad on Tier 6 before stepping onto Tier 7 so you clear all six wave-two spawns in one turn.

Episode 6 · 帝都大崩壊！？

  • Final Day Roam & Sixth Bromide. Do not invite Tsubaki to dinner before buying your Episode 6 ブロマイド at the 売店. Pass through the 1F Dining Hall (or Lobby later) to help Kanna with オー迷子ッド！. Decline walking straight to the Salon with Sakura so you can visit the Dressing Room (praise Maria's costume), Backstage (Sumire, ask about the stage and stay silent), Basement Locker Room (call out to Iris rather than peeking, then re-enter for bonus trust), and Kohran's Room (wait out both silent bars patiently while she builds Enkai-kun).
  • Banquet Prep & Underground Escape. At the 2F Salon, join the party and pick "Help everyone else" (or help Sakura, though rain sends her alone). Visit Kohran's Room within three moves first to unlock applause points later, play Maria's 料理でハラショー in the Kitchen, help Kanna move tables from the Basement Storage (accept Iris's teleport help), and visit the Dressing Room (Sumire/Iris) before returning upstairs. During the storm scene with Sakura, click away from her face at most twice and hold her firmly. Once trapped in the basement rubble, click the bottom-left rubble directly to recover Maria's pistol with minimal wasted clicks.
  • Battle · 帝劇地下 vs. 紅のミロク. Clear disguised props shaped like Koubu in the five-pilot chamber, repair while Iris sprints to flip the door switch, and gather all seven units outside Miroku's door. Bait Miroku forward with a かばう decoy, chip her just above 25% HP on Turn 1 of engagement so she skips healing, and finish her cleanly on Turn 2.

Episode 7 · 決戦 命の限り！

  • Infirmary & Briefing Events. In the infirmary inspection with 真宮寺さくら, keep clicking limited to neutral spots, put on a cheerful front for the team, then step outside to meet your top-ranked heroine (if Sumire triggers her blindfold surprise, stay silent for top trust. If Kohran appears, smile bravely for a double boost, and if Kanna appears, stay confident). On the Skywhale deck after rescuing Sakura, teasing "Weren't you cold?" unexpectedly raises her trust.
  • 日本橋 Underground Stages 1 & 2. Form your four-unit advance party with Ogami, 神崎すみれ, マリア・タチバナ, and 真宮寺さくら (or 李紅蘭). In Stage 1, send Ogami to break the top 脇侍 hut alone while the other three drop downstairs to destroy the lower two huts, watching cannon ranges. In Stage 2, HP resets, so surround 天海 and burst him down in one to two turns.
  • Stage 3 · Final 天海 Showdown (Disc 1 Clear). All seven pilots reunite at full HP. Ignore the eight royal guards (they vanish when Tenkai falls) and box Tenkai in. Leave Tenkai just above one-third HP at the end of Turn 1 so he does not self-heal, then unload every Special Attack on Turn 2 to finish Disc 1 and save before swapping to Disc 2.

Episode 8 · 平和な日々はデートだよ！

  • Shrine Anchor Save & 初詣 Date. Keep a permanent VMU save here. Your top-ranked heroine invites you on the way to the Manager's Office. Accept immediately if she is your target, or decline politely to invite any heroine inside your top three after talking with 藤枝あやめ. At 明治神宮, click the leftmost booth to play the shooting gallery ステキに射的. In the following ambush battle, deal two normal attacks on Turn 1 to trigger the retreat order, evacuate every unit behind the torii gate by Turn 3 while conserving Spirit, and eliminate the 降魔 wave after the airship bombardment.
  • Post-Training Roam & Special Bromide Loop. Pick "Leave it to each member's judgment" after the skirmish, and create a temporary VMU save right before returning from training. Visit the 売店 to collect a New Year Special ブロマイド (use reload-and-save at the next pre-battle prompt to log all 6 heroines plus Ayame's double-timeout special). During the roam (alarm sounds on room 11). Check 2F Salon (stay silent with 神崎すみれ, then talk to 榊原由里 and apologize in Sumire's room), Stage Wing & Basement Locker Room (Sakura's singing shower), 2F Library & Terrace (Maria), Dining Hall & Training Room (Kanna), Iris's Room then Ayame's Room, and Kohran's Room (ask to ride her airplane together).
  • Battle · 銀座 vs. 猪. Pilot your newly deployed 神武 mechs. Send Sakura and Sumire up the center and Maria left to wipe out the lesser demons with expanded Specials while the rest advance right. Place an adjacent かばう decoy within Inoshishi's fire-line range to bait his special safely, then surround and finish him before his Spirit refills.

Episode 9 · 現れた最終兵器

  • Salon Incident, Theater Trap & Late-Night Secret. In the 2F Salon when Iris trips with juice, pick "Catch Iris" and ask "Are you alright, Maria?" if pursuing Maria (unlocking Maria's basement shower scene with Yoneda), or "Protect the book" and choose your own handkerchief for neutral balance (silence costs both Sakura and Sumire trust). In free roam, stall Yoneda on your third side room if you still need Dressing Room (Kohran), Costume Room (Iris), or Basement Shower (Maria). In the basement corridor trap LIPS, pick "Steam jet" to block two infiltrators (washtub blocks one, pitfall blocks zero). That night when your top heroine questions you, either deflect safely or tell the truth for an extra boost, then pick "Resist" during 藤枝あやめ's corridor ambush so your top heroine intervenes without dropping morale.
  • Battle 1 · 銀座 vs. 鹿. Split immediately to smash the four corner spawners (魔来器) with Maria and Kohran top-right, Sakura bottom-left, Ogami or Kanna bottom-right, and Iris top-left, leaving Sumire guarding the theater breach. Stand diagonally down-right of a spawner to block spawns while chipping it. Leave the top-left spawner for last so everyone gathers fully charged outside the theater steam stand before breaking it. When Shika drops down, surround it and finish it in one to two turns with 合体攻撃.
  • Post-Battle Console & Battle 2 vs. 蝶. After 藤枝あやめ steals the 魔神器 and becomes 殺女, visit every teammate (Sumire Salon, Sakura 2F Terrace, Kanna Dining Hall, Kohran 2F Library, Maria's Room, and Iris's Room or 2F Hall if #1) before seeing Yoneda. In Battle 2, destroy the shock towers along the path using Sakura's long-range special from across the bridge, bait Chou forward with a protected long-range decoy, and pin it in melee.

Episode 10 · 最後の審判

  • 聖魔城 Breach & Gate Battle. If Sakura or Maria holds #1 trust, view their special 翔鯨丸 bridge dialogue en route. After the ミカサ blasts open the fortress gate, clear all 16 lesser demons smoothly using Sakura's wide 神武 special on the right, Sumire's five-tile sweep on the left, and Maria for cleanup.
  • Inner Sanctum Gauntlet. Inside the castle, five members sacrifice themselves one by one against the revived Three Kouma and demon crest (Kanna, Sumire, Kohran, Maria, and Iris in order, with Sakura standing in if that heroine holds #1 trust) until only Ogami and your lead heroine reach the 霊子砲. Defeat 殺女 in a one-on-one duel, let the finisher LIPS timer run out to spare her, then defeat 葵叉丹 by shifting one tile after every attack so he spends turns chasing instead of charging Spirit.
  • Final Showdown · 大魔王サタン & Epilogue. With all seven 花組 pilots reunited, unleash every Special and 合体攻撃 aggressively while keeping Iris alive for party heals and 気力 refills. After the credits and your chosen heroine's epilogue movie, save your クリアデータ onto your VMU.
